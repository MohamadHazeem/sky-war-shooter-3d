# Phase 6 goal 1 — dogfight feel

Implemented on `phase6/01-dogfight-feel`; baseline: `1558f59`.

## Changes and hooks

- `src/world/AI.ts:164`: with a live player escort within 3.5 km of the bomber, setup offsets shrink to 45% (65% for Me 262/163). Prop head-on setups become roughly 1.05 km instead of 2.35 km. Gunner arcs and distant setups retain their geometry.
- `src/world/AI.ts:230`: a Mustang on the tail within 1.2 km, aimed fire within 1.4 km, or recent hits within 1.6 km trigger a skill-scaled, time-based reaction into BFM. Mere proximity does not divert bomber attackers. Withdrawal, steady raid overrides and AI-escort responses remain intact.
- `src/world/DogfightAI.ts:157`: close contact keeps an engaged fighter fighting beyond its original engagement timer; after losing contact for eight seconds, an expired engagement returns to bomber duty.
- `src/world/DogfightAI.ts:224` and `:287`: bounded breaks, spirals and scissors lead back to pursuit. Fw 190s use occasional short zooms; heavies briefly recover energy rather than run away. Jets extend for at most 5.5 seconds or 1.1 km, then commit to a direct reversal. A defensive decision can no longer interrupt that reversal with another extension.
- `tests/dogfightSim.test.ts:46`: seeded pursuit and ballistic duel harness; `:110` proximity/kill/bomber-threat regressions; `:164` detection, skill, 30/60/120 Hz, setup, withdrawal and disengagement checks.

No shared Game/World/UI hooks were needed. No flight-performance, mission-wave, round-limit, player-weapon, material or player-visible text changes; no new runtime allocations in the changed paths.

## Before / after measurements

Same harness against baseline AI and final AI, seeds **3, 7, 11, 19, 42**, nominal enemy skill 1 with the existing spawn variation. Flight integrates at 60 Hz; the player uses the existing autoplay/duel-style full-throttle lead pursuit and 20 Hz trigger decision. No pursuit improvements were made to obtain these results.

Proximity: 90 seconds, player starts 1.1 km behind a bomber attacker; damage disabled so each run has equal duration. Duel: starts 650 m behind a bandit already in BFM, capped at 120 seconds, real Fighter hit zones and Ballistics, six .50s with game spread/convergence, Veteran structural/zone strength and high-assist hit scale. Visual models are stubbed; return damage, collisions, perks, wingmen and damage-induced flight effects are omitted. These are isolated AI comparisons, not full mission kill-rate forecasts.

| Type | Median seconds <600 m, before → after | Duel kills / 5, before → after | Median TTK of completed kills, before → after |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Fw 190 | 56.8 → 77.7 | 5 → 5 | 15.9 → 12.0 s |
| Bf 109 | 56.3 → 67.9 | 5 → 5 | 14.7 → 11.7 s |
| Me 262 | 9.2 → 47.3 | 1 → 3 | 110.3 → 18.3 s |
| Bf 110 | 64.0 → 73.3 | 5 → 5 | 18.9 → 14.3 s |

Jet failures are censored (>120 s), excluded from the completed-kill median. Final per-seed TTKs (same seed order): Fw 190 **11.3/10.7/12.0/20.4/49.0**; Bf 109 **10.9/11.7/11.8/18.5/11.4**; Me 262 **>120/>120/18.5/18.2/18.3**; Bf 110 **7.5/36.0/14.5/14.3/10.9** seconds. Final median defensive time before kill/cap is respectively **10.7/10.1/5.7/11.0 s**; the bandits still evade.

In the equal-duration proximity runs, median firing time changes **15.1→9.6 / 20.9→8.5 / 1.1→9.5 / 30.2→24.5 s** in the same type order. Closer combat does not automatically mean easier continuous tracking; the prop fighters now spend more time reversing near the player.

With the escort holding beside the box and looking away, every unengaged Fw 190 continues targeting the bomber. Across five 90-second runs: median time within 1.2 km of the bomber **61.2→68.2 s**, firing time **3.2→4.1 s**, completed passes **3→3** (after range 3–4). This checks retained threat, not bomber-loss balance.
