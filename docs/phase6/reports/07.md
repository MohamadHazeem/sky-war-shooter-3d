# Phase 6 · Goal 07 — Second tour

Implemented on `phase6/07-second-tour`.

- Tour-summary and completed-map screens offer **Volunteer for a second tour**, alongside the existing story-tour restart.
- `src/game/SecondTour.ts` generates deterministic seed/index operations using the raid targets and strategic effects, plus industrial city targets. Alternating B-17/P-51 roles, five weather moods and fronts, rising escorts, armoured stern-attacking Fw 190s from operation 7, Me 262s from 9, Me 163s from 13, and a final-round battle group every fifth operation. Difficulty rises smoothly toward bounded counts/skill; strategic modifiers still apply.
- Dates advance fortnightly after the last story mission through 1944–45. The endless extension is explicitly fictional and holds at 31 December 1945 once reached. After 25 victories the player earns the Second Tour Medal and continues to “Extended operation 26” and beyond.
- The optional `secondTour` field in the existing v2 campaign save stores completion, successful-operation score and medal status. Old first/repeat-story tours retain their behavior. Failures can be retried; replays cannot advance the campaign or duplicate its score/medal. The strategic situation and independent Experten roster carry forward. History and the map window remain bounded; generated missions are not cached indefinitely.
- Generated debriefs use the existing morale/merit and career systems. Crew-room scenes reuse up to three existing outcome lines, without story-specific dialogue. Arabic phrases/patterns are registered in `src/i18n/ar/secondTour.ts` and `src/i18n/phrases.ts`; no new recorded radio lines or materials were introduced.
