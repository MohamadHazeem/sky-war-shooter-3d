# Phase 6 goal 10 — Europe below the clouds

Implemented on `phase6/10-europe-below`.

- Added deterministic, mipmapped 16 km land-cover tiles: fields/hedgerows, woods, winding rivers and village roofs. Summer greens, autumn browns and Dec–Feb snow use mission dates. Ground remains at y=0 with weather-dependent aerial perspective.
- Extracted the unchanged briefing geography to `src/world/EuropeChart.ts`; `BriefingMap.ts` re-exports its existing API. A mission-start coast strip follows the same outbound/homeward waypoints. Route distance is compressed to the scheduled bombing approach (or 80% of mission duration without a bomb run). Absolute coordinates keep ground fixed across floating-origin shifts; endpoint clamping prevents artificial ocean beyond the chart.
- Reused the existing detailed industrial city, impacts, fires and ground flak flashes. Extended impact-driven smoke toward 7.4 km and added 48 soft instanced puffs to carry its upper portion through the low deck.
- High/Ultra sample the existing upper-cloud shadow map; Medium uses coarser land-cover mips and 24 upper-smoke puffs; Low uses flat seasonal land/sea and disables the new upper-smoke overlay. No new player-facing strings, assets, dependencies or radio lines.

## Scope and limitations

Uses the existing sky ray/ground-plane intersection with texture mip LOD rather than adding terrain rings/draw calls. Geography is deliberately schematic and compressed along the game's short missions; local fields repeat, coast outlines gain small stable inlets, and terrain is flat at this altitude.
