# Phase 6 · 02 — Weak-laptop pass

Implemented on `phase6/02-weak-laptop`. `npm run check` passes: TypeScript, production build,
46 test files / 495 tests. No dependencies installed, push or deployment.

## Changes and hooks

- `src/core/Engine.ts:32,223`: Low now has a 1024² sun shadow map over ±24 m. Its world-space
  texel size matches Medium, so the hull shades the interior and nearby aircraft cast real
  shadows. Low retains the 0.9 MP budget, 36-step volumetric clouds at 0.33 scale, no temporal
  accumulation, heat haze, AO or living sky. No fifth “Low+” setting needed.
- `src/render/PostFX.ts:336,446,498`: Low uses three bloom levels (five passes instead of eleven),
  no camera motion blur, near DOF or sun shafts. Film grade, bloom, damage cues and lens flare
  remain. Stationary lookups now use one scene tap instead of ten coincident taps at every tier.
  Reused camera vectors/matrix remove four existing per-frame allocations with Engine's change.
- `src/render/Explosions.ts:147,234`: Low marches four samples instead of six, preserving
  integrated opacity through the adjusted step length. `src/game/Game.ts:427` sets the uniform.
  The existing `World.prewarm` explosion warms this same program; no new material/define variants.
- `src/render/QualityPick.ts:25,57`: tier-specific cost and pixel-share model replaces the fixed
  0.6 tier multiplier; malformed percentiles cannot promote a tier.
- `src/game/AutoGraphics.ts:134`: before a downgrade, measure the starting tier at 0.8 render
  scale. A successful recovery keeps that tier and starts at the proven scale. Aborts restore
  the previous quality/scale. Even very slow devices collect at least 12 samples, instead of
  silently retaining High when the time limit expires too early.
- `src/render/FrameTiming.ts:59,62` and `src/game/Game.ts:533`: session fallback explicitly waits
  for the 60 Hz fallback and DRS floor, then the existing ten seconds below 40 fps. The existing
  one-drop-per-session policy remains. No new player-visible strings.
- `src/dev/weakLaptop.ts`: reusable GPU-synced sampler. Tests: `qualityPick`, `frameTiming`,
  and new `autoGraphics` cover thresholds, spikes, caps, actual DRS controller recovery,
  measured recovery, insufficient/invalid data, slow sampling and abort restoration.

## Measurement setup

Own Vite server: `npx vite --port 5256 --strictPort`. Headless Chrome with ANGLE Metal on an
Apple M5 Pro, 1440×900 CSS viewport, device scale 2; Chrome DevTools Protocol
`Emulation.setCPUThrottlingRate({rate:4})`. This saturates the preset pixel budgets while
slowing JS submission/simulation. It **does not emulate weak GPU bandwidth or an integrated
GPU driver**. Results are a calibration/stress model, not a hardware FPS guarantee.

Frames were driven explicitly with `sws.step`, `sws.gallery`, `sws.vqa`, and the sampler;
rAF was stopped with `sws.vqa.freeze()`. Each after-sample has 24 untimed warm frames and
72 measured frames, synchronized by a one-pixel WebGL readback. DRS scaling measurements
use 16 warm / 60 measured fixed-camera frames. Before measurements use `sws.bench(72)`
after 24 warm frames. One initial series per configuration; sub-millisecond deltas are noisy.

Full-frame costs include game updates. Times are milliseconds:

|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Low | 1200×750 | 10.6 / 13.2 | 9.0 / 11.1 | 12.5 / 14.9 | 0.71 |
| Medium | 1549×968 | 11.8 / 14.0 | 13.9 / 16.1 | 16.6 / 18.1 | 0.94 |
| High | 1918×1198 | 14.5 / 17.4 | 14.6 / 15.9 | 17.6 / 20.5 | 1.00 |
| Ultra | 2433×1520 | 19.9 / 22.9 | 18.5 / 20.7 | 22.9 / 29.8 | 1.30 |

The camera moves in full-frame menu samples; don't interpret the Medium before/after
variation as a regression from Low-only changes. Fixed-camera render medians isolate the
pixel response more closely:

| Preset | Scale 1 | Scale 0.8 | Scale 0.55 | Scale-1 / High | Model cost / pixel share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Low | 8.6 | 8.3 | 8.2 | 0.65 | 0.70 / 0.10 |

| High | 13.2 | 11.8 | 9.6 | 1.00 | 1.00 / 0.40 |
| Ultra | 17.3 | 15.6 | 13.3 | 1.31 | 1.35 / 0.35 |

Low is mostly fixed submission/CPU cost here: assuming 70% pixel-bound work overstates
its DRS savings. Rounded tier costs use the fixed-camera and original moving-menu ratios;
the gunner results show that Medium can approach High when submission dominates. The
measured recovery phase avoids relying on the model to decide whether the starting tier holds.

## Feature cost model

| Feature | Low | Medium | High | Ultra | Main scaling factor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clouds | .33 × 36, legacy | .45 × 52, temporal | .50 × 64, temporal | .62 × 88, temporal | Pixels × scale² × steps; temporal marches ¼ of cloud texels, plus resolve/shadow passes |
| Sun shadows | 1024², ±24 m | 2048², ±48 m | 2048², ±40 m | 4096², ±40 m | Caster submission + map texels; independent of DRS |
| Post | 3 bloom levels; no blur/DOF/shafts/AO | 6 levels, blur/DOF/shafts | Same + AO .6 | Same + AO .7 | Full-resolution taps + bloom passes |
| Surface detail | Off | Panels | Panels + wear | Same as High | Material fragment work, no extra draws |
| Living sky | Off | Budget .5 | Budget 1 | Budget 1.6 | Instances/ribbons + CPU update |
| Explosions | 4 steps | 6 steps | 6 steps | 6 steps | Visible blast area × steps; pool remains 128 |
| Countryside | 1024 texture, 192 grid, ~3500 trees | 1024, 192, ~6000 | 2048, 256, ~9000 | Same as High | Chunk submission, instanced tree geometry, ground fill |

High fixed formation camera ablations (render only): baseline 15.4 ms, no cloud march/resolve
12.9, no shadows 14.3, post replaced by a copy 13.8, no surface detail 15.0, no living sky
14.9. A second baseline was 14.7: the ~0.4–0.5 ms surface/sky differences are within drift.
Clouds cost roughly 2.5 ms, shadows 1.1 ms and post 1.6 ms in this view; these differences
are **not additive GPU timings**. Shadow/surface ablations warmed their diagnostic shader variants.
A fixed 2× explosion at 45 m cost 16.2 ms versus 15.2 with its volume hidden; four steps
measured 15.3 ms. Actual shape/area strongly changes this cost.

Countryside camera, newly constructed per preset (on / hidden render p50): Low 7.9 / 6.7,
Medium 8.5 / 8.5, High 12.6 / 9.9, Ultra 17.2 / 15.6 ms. Tree/poplar counts were
3431/430, 5910/727, 8941/888 and 8941/888. Medium's zero delta is below measurement noise,
not zero rendering cost. Countryside construction budgets remain selected when the landscape
is first built; changing Settings later does not rebuild its cached geometry.

## Picker thresholds and recovery

`L = max(p50, p90 / 1.3) × 1.3` estimates mission load (previous multiplier: 1.1).
The gunner/menu median ratio was 1.21 on High, 1.24 on Ultra, and 1.39 on Low; 1.3 supplies
more headroom without claiming to cover every combat peak. At 60 Hz the hold budget is
`16.667 × 1.14 = 19 ms`. The model predicts scale cost as
`L × relativeTierCost × (1 - pixelShare + pixelShare × 0.8²)`.

| Measured tier | Unspiky menu p50 hold limit at 60 Hz (model, no recovery sample) |
|---|---:|
| Medium | 15.75 ms |
| High | 17.07 ms |
| Ultra | 16.72 ms |

Low is the final fallback, even if it cannot meet the budget. A full-size sample above
14.62 ms (or unusual p90 spikes) triggers the actual 0.8-scale probe. Its load must fit
19 ms to keep the starting tier. Thus a 24 ms High sample recovering to 14 ms stays High;
a CPU-bound sample that stays slow drops. These are covered by integration tests.
The 50 Hz limits scale by 1.2; 120/144 Hz still plan for 60 fps. Promotion requires the
measured upper tier to fit `16.667 × 1.054` after mission headroom, at full resolution.

Browser detector checks under accumulated session load: 4× CPU gave High 21.8 ms / recovery
21.2 ms; 8× gave 37.3 / 38.6 ms. Both correctly selected Low; reducing pixels could not
recover that CPU-bound workload. These are separate scenes/session states from the cost table.

To repeat after starting the server, freeze rAF and import the dev sampler:

```js
const { weakLaptopSample } = await import('/src/dev/weakLaptop.ts');
sws.vqa.freeze();
sws.game.settings.bookends = false;
sws.vqa.quality('low');
sws.engine.renderScale = 1;
sws.engine.resize();
sws.gallery.setup('waist'); // or game.startMenu(), gallery.setup('formation')
weakLaptopSample(sws.engine, true); // false: fixed-camera render only
```
