# Phase 6 · Goal 12 — Mode music

Implemented seven original procedural compositions, rendered offline to mono AAC. No borrowed
melodies or samples. Added **901,542 bytes (0.86 MiB)** of audio. Existing background preloading
fetches these after the menu; Sound decodes them after mission entry (or the menu's 60-second
idle delay). Missing/late tracks retain the existing score until available.

| Track / file (`public/audio/`) | Loop / cue length | Bytes | Plays |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `music_night.m4a` | 26.67 s | 137,558 | Night Fighter: low strings, sparse timpani, coned-raider lift |
| `music_field.m4a` | 17.14 s | 146,270 | Defend the Field: brass-and-snare march |
| `music_train.m4a` | 15.00 s | 126,679 | Train Busting: faster march variation |

| `music_crew.m4a` | 30.00 s | 142,905 | Crew room: quiet piano/strings beneath the existing gramophone |
| `music_crewLoss.m4a` | 34.29 s | 153,152 | Crew room with the existing empty-bunk flag: heavy losses or story loss |
| `music_breakup.m4a` | 4.50 s | 11,669 | Warship starts breaking apart; overlays the bed, rate-limited to 1.5 s |

Loop files include another 1 s of wrapped padding, covered by the existing AAC priming correction.
Crew-room heavy losses follow the existing rule: at least three bombers, or at least 25% of the
formation. Story empty bunks also select the sombre variant.

## Changes and hooks

- `src/audio/ModeMusic.ts`: mode selection and searchlight/boss threat floors; allocation-free frame hook.
- `src/audio/Music.ts`: mode beds, two-second mode/room cross-fades, interrupted-fade level holds,
  night-specific threat layers, breakup overlay, and Reduced detail dropping procedural layers.
  Normal detail uses existing tempo/root-locked layers. All new audio passes through the existing
  music volume, mute, reverb and ducking buses.
- `src/game/Game.ts:487`: mission/boss/searchlight context before `setThreat`.
- `src/game/BossBattle.ts:807`: breakup cue at the start of each ship's death sequence.
- `src/ui/CrewRoomScene.ts:945` / `:1008`: enter normal/sombre score, restore menu on exit/skip.
- `src/audio/Sound.ts:1113`: forward Audio detail to music.
- `tools/audio/mode_music_build.mjs`, `mode_music_meta.json`, `gen_meta.py`, README,
  `public/audio/manifest.json`, credits and seven rendered files: reproducible asset pipeline.
- `tests/modeMusic.test.ts`: seven tests covering actual mission IDs, threat, transitions,
  room restoration, Reduced detail, mute/resume, death overlap, late loading and asset budget.
- `tools/audio/mode_music_check.html`: reusable development-only browser verification harness.

No new player-facing strings, radio lines, dependencies, shaders or rendering hooks.

Music scheduling, full-threat layers, quiet night, Reduced boss, zero volume, and a sequence of
cross-fades with a breakup overlay. The stress graph sums the dry path and the full 0.9 wet send
coherently (without the room impulse). Worst peak **−4.60 dBFS**, **zero clipped/non-finite samples**;
zero volume is digital silence. Full-threat combat RMS spans approximately **−20.9 to −19.4 dBFS**
in that conservative graph; crew variants are **−30.5 / −33.1 dBFS**, before the room's additional
ducking. Encoded-file BS.1770 integrated loudness is also recorded in the JSON (−20.7 night,
−23.6 field, −24.8 train, −21.2 boss, −27.0 crew, −29.8 losses, −19.5 breakup LUFS).
No listening assessment was performed; these are numerical and routing checks, not a subjective
musical-quality evaluation or a full-game gunfire/explosion mix measurement.
