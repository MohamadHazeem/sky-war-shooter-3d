# Phase 6 · Goal 11 — Ball and tail turret interiors

Implemented on `phase6/11-turret-interiors`.

- Ball: paired M2 breeches/barrels, short animated feed chutes, curled knees/boots on pedals, reflector projector, overhead access hatch and fuselage ribs. Traverse drives a continuous hydraulic motor hum. Casings fall outside, without a floor pile or floor-clatter audio.
- Tail: paired M2s and forward-running animated belts, supported ring-and-bead sight, kneeling legs/pads and bicycle saddle, tapered ribbed cone, wheel-well cover and control cables. Looking forward reveals the existing waist compartment and crewman. The cabin stays fixed while the guns traverse; spent brass accumulates around the knees.
- Both reuse glass frost/weather, muzzle lighting, breath fog and sun shafts. C allows inspection behind/above the gun and holds the commanded gun aim, including with aim assist. The central aim path stays clear.
- New static details use two merged, vertex-coloured meshes per station and the existing crew material. Brass remains instanced/capped; belts reuse scratch vectors, including tangent sampling. Full station viewmodel materials are warmed before play. No dependencies, player-visible strings or radio lines were added.
