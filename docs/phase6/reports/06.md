# Phase 6 · 06 — Ditching in the Channel

Implemented a 31-second, skippable ditching/rescue ending: grey analytic ocean with waves, foam, fog and sun/moon glint; braced crew compartment; impact spray; settling player bomber; two yellow dinghies with ten survivors; signal flare and RAF high-speed rescue launch. Day/night lighting and Low work. The rescue scene remains behind the debrief and releases on menu, briefing, restart or replay.

## Trigger and outcome

`DitchingRules.ts` contains the tested rules. At mission end: three engines out, integrity below 22%, or two engines out plus a fuel leak. During the final quarter: two engines out (or worse), still controllable, and estimated coast distance above 25 km. Excludes pilots, co-op, tutorials, survival, destroyed wings/tail/cockpit and already-dead aircraft. Last Light's scripted two-engine handicap needs further deterioration before triggering early, so its original route remains completable.

The coast estimate is explicitly narrative: `clamp((1 - missionProgress) * 240, 0, 80)` km; World has no geographic return route. Bombing missions succeed if bombs were dropped. Other missions preserve their completion result. Exactly one bomber loss enters debrief/merit/campaign history (`ditched: true`); all crew survive, with possible roster injuries/rest. Goldfish Club unlocks. Disabling bookends or autoplay bypasses only the scene, preserving the outcome.

## Force it

Run `npx vite --port 5254 --strictPort`. Once `sws.assetsReady()` is true, use `sws.ditch()` from the title or a gunner campaign mission. It starts a mission if needed and disables three engines. `sws.ditch(false)` forces an unmet-objective ending. The hook respects the bookends setting and autoplay flag.

For deterministic inspection in a background tab:

```js
sws.engine.loopOverride = () => {}; // stop automatic frames
sws.game.settings.bookends = true;
sws.game.bookends.auto = false;
sws.ditch();
sws.step(720, 1 / 60, true); // impact at 12 s
sws.step(960, 1 / 60, true); // rescue at 28 s
sws.step(240, 1 / 60, true); // debrief
sws.engine.loopOverride = undefined;
```
