# Phase 6 · 08 — Crew that matters

Implemented on `phase6/08-crew-roles`.

- All ten men have five XP-growing stats and personality traits. Data-driven duties and cached effects stay within 0.75–1.25; player skill-tree purchases remain separate.
- Assigned gunnery controls AI spread, lead error and reaction. Lookouts advance radar warnings; the top-turret engineer improves repairs and extinguisher timing; radio first aid improves dressing time and serious-wound odds; pilots improve jink frequency and formation steadiness.
- Hangar: five stat bars, role tooltips, tap/click and keyboard swapping, exhaustive six-man recommended layout, hospital/replacement status, permanent memorial, Arabic and RTL. Fixed the hidden assignment hint and Enter accidentally launching from crew controls.
- Serious wounds require two completed bomber missions in hospital; abandoned missions do not discharge patients. Replacements have skill 24. A fatal casualty requires a wounded level-3+ veteran aboard a destroyed player bomber and at least three combat losses; at most one casualty, with a 3% roll modified by first aid. Death persists and cannot be healed.
