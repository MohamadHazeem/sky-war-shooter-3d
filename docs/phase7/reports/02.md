# Phase 7 / 02 — Sea Strike

Implemented on `phase7/02-naval-strike`. Entry: **Survival → Sea Strike → P-51 Escort**.

- Endless `survival-sea` waves: patrols, escorted merchant convoys, destroyers/U-boats, coastal assault, and a battleship group every fifth wave; later cycles add escorts and stronger fire. Bf 109/Fw 190 cover joins later waves.
- Thirteen procedural target classes, separate gun/flak parts, armoured hulls and magazine weak points. The battleship has four main turrets, eight flak mounts, a disarm gate, warned salvos, and a 32-second listing/splitting/settling death. U-boats crash-dive, leave a submerged wake, accept nearby bombs while shallow, and escape without awarding a kill.
- Existing P-51 flight and .50s, eight pooled HVAR rockets (**R**, existing reload binding), two 500 lb bombs (**N**), shallow skip bombing, clickable weapon buttons, and hangar rearming. Ship kills contribute to score and leaderboard statistics.
- Ocean, cliffs/beach, harbour mole, casemates, 88 mm batteries, flak towers, radar and night searchlights. Radar destruction reduces flak accuracy. Wakes, bow spray, bubbles, oil slicks, burning wrecks and debris; storm swell/pitch/spray; Low reduces spray. Geometry/materials are shared or merged; naval materials join World prewarming.
- Class markers, large-ship health bars, mount/magazine highlights, offscreen bearings, salvo warnings, wave banners and ammo HUD. Arabic table plus five recorded Control lines generated with `python3 tools/voice/generate.py --engine say --only sea_`.
- `Mission.naval` and `seaMission(rounds)` allow a future finite campaign operation. Leaderboard routing is distinct from air survival; only the allowed-mode regex changed in `firestore.rules`.
