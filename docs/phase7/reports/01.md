# Phase 7 · 01 — Hold the Field

Implemented the `survival-aa` Survival mode with five weather choices, fixed quad .50/Bofors mounts, limited traverse/elevation, recoil, muzzle flashes, casings, heat, ammunition and reloads. Two pits start available; the third is an upgrade. Keyboard, mouse, touch and gamepad pit switching are supported.

The reused airbase has 11 damageable assets: tower, two hangars, four parked B-17s, fuel and three pits. Damaged pits recover after 12 seconds. Losing the tower or all parked bombers ends the run. Destruction adds ruins, fire, smoke, craters, debris and pooled burning wrecks.

Bounded, data-driven waves mix strafers, screaming Ju 88 dives, He 111 bomb formations, Bf 110 cannon/rockets and later jets/rocket fighters. Every fifth wave is a major raid. The HUD includes asset integrity, threats, wave countdown, score/combo, kill feed and four between-wave choices. Night adds six searchlights. All new text has Arabic coverage; three Chatterbox radio clips were generated and decoded successfully.
