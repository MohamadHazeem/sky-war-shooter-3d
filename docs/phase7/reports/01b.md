# Phase 7 · 01b — Hold the Field polish

Rebuilt the quad mount with perforated M2 jackets, muzzle fittings, armour, ammunition chests and linked belts, framed Mk 9 reflector sight, grips and seat. The Bofors has a long barrel/flash cone, recoil sleeve, four-round loading guides and cartwheel sight. Both reuse the existing parkerised/riveted procedural textures, with edge wear, recoil, heat emission and smoke.

Each pit now has 136 rounded, irregular, colour-varied hessian bags in four courses (about 1.2 m above the field), plus a dirt floor. AA-only scenery adds ribbed T2 hangars, curved corrugated Nissen workshops, glazed watch-office windows/rails, bowsers, bomb trolleys, paths/verges, grass variation and hedges. Parked B-17s now sit on their tyres in a three-point attitude. The existing windsock remains. Nine simple crew figures run during raids above Low; details cull beyond 650 m, and trees leave an 18 m clearance around pits.

The sky streaks came from the dome's stretched cirrus plane, confirmed with [sky-only](../shots/01b/sky-only-before.jpg) and [raw-scene](../shots/01b/raw-scene-before.jpg) renders. AA disables that decorative layer through a uniform and restores it on exit; volumetric weather clouds remain.

The HUD now uses the game's Oswald/IBM Plex fonts, an 11-asset icon/bar list, slim ammunition/heat gauges and separated subtitles. Arabic labels and touch layout are covered. Near impacts add distance-scaled shake/dust with accessibility scaling. Quad shots eject four pooled cases; cases tumble/bounce. Bofors feeds four-round clips with a 0.9-second pause, preserving the existing reserve reload and timed/proximity black-flak effects.

## Files and hooks

- `AADefenceModels.ts`: cached/batched guns, shared textured materials, instanced bags and pit floor.
- `AADefenceGround.ts`: AA-only scenery, distance culling and crew animation.
- `AADefenceScene.ts:21,63,73,77`: warm-up, parked-aircraft grounding, pits and scenery. Uses the existing `World.prewarm` hook at `World.ts:304`.
- `AADefence.ts:87,121,166,241,276,350,357,464`: cirrus lifecycle, HUD, scenery/heat updates, shake, casings and impact feedback.
- `AADefenceRules.ts:54,72,81`: clip state, feed timer and firing gate; `tests/aaDefence.test.ts`: clip/reserve regression.
- `aa-defence.css`, `i18n/ar/aa.ts`: HUD layout/fonts and Arabic phrases.
- `Sky.ts:19,84`, `Effects.ts:204`: uniform-controlled cirrus and pooled dust. Gun smoke now uses the existing reusable emission options.
- `tools/aa_browser_check.mjs`, `tools/aa_polish_views.mjs`: repeatable gameplay and polish verification.
