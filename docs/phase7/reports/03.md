# Phase 7 · 03 — Weather

Implemented on `phase7/03-weather`. No dependencies added; no push or deployment.

## Changes and hooks

- `src/render/WeatherPresets.ts` (new): six condition presets, bounded spotting/aim/traverse modifiers, wind math and quality budgets. `WeatherState.ts` exposes the stable blended state; `Weather.ts:158` builds presets, `:194` applies them and `:250` blends fronts, including restart recovery. Time of day remains independent.
- `src/render/Precipitation.ts` (new): GPU rain/snow volume, airspeed streaks and pooled rare lightning. `src/core/Engine.ts:49,326` owns/updates it; `src/world/World.ts:305` prewarms both materials. Low uses 160 particles versus High's 960, in the existing transparent pass. `Atmosphere.ts`/`Clouds.ts` add fog to the existing composite, including clouds/sky.
- `src/game/Game.ts:219,231,438,483`: `setWeather(preset)` / `weatherNow`, unified lightning/thunder, airspeed and gentle turbulence. `GameFeel.ts` consumes the unified flash. `GlassFx.ts:545,656,711,827` reuses glass drops and frost; `Airbase.ts:926` adds wet ground/runway sheen; `DitchingSea.ts:141` consumes swell (1.8× in thunderstorms). `Bookends.ts`/`FighterBookends.ts` retain mission precipitation at take-off.
- Gameplay: `GameHud.ts:181` fades markers; `PilotController.ts:237` limits padlock acquisition; `GunnerController.ts:457` slows snowy traverse 10%. `AI.ts` and `DogfightAI.ts` reduce firing range and increase aim error up to 20%. `Ballistics.ts:109` applies symmetric gentle bullet drift; `TargetArea.ts` adds the same wind used by existing Bombardier impact/sight math to falling bombs.
- Selection/data: `CustomBattle.ts` adds all conditions; its existing screen discovers them through `MOODS`. `MissionFlow.ts:70,179` randomises Survival weather. `Missions.ts` adds autumn rain, winter fog and February snow; `RaidDefence.ts` starts in light rain, `StrafeOps.ts` brings a rain front. `UI.ts:467` adds Weather effects. New Arabic table `i18n/ar/weather.ts`, registered in `phrases.ts`; `ar/battle.ts` translates compound summaries.
- API and integration guidance: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:105`. New modes can call `game.setWeather('heavyRain')` and read `game.weatherNow`; Sea Strike can read `weatherNow.swell`.

## Performance

`sws.bench(120)` with GPU readback, calm six-bomber top-turret scene, 10 seconds of simulation to settle glass, rAF frozen, auto quality disabled. Milliseconds per frame. Firefox repeat used 30 warm frames, High 2058×1117 / Low 1287×698:

|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| High | Clear | 16.26 | 15 | 24 |
| High | Heavy rain | 15.35 | 15 | 18 |
| High | Thunderstorm | 16.15 | 15 | 23 |
| Low | Clear | 9.01 | 8 | 15 |
| Low | Heavy rain | 9.74 | 10 | 14 |
| Low | Thunderstorm | 9.60 | 8 | 16 |

Chrome's original sweep is retained in `results.json` (High 2063×1114 / Low 1291×697). It had a large heavy-rain timing outlier, so these repeat figures are reported separately; host/browser variability means these are observations, not an isolated overhead guarantee. No extra weather render passes or per-frame particle allocations.

## Limits

Optional sleet/hail omitted. Fog uses atmospheric extinction rather than another low-cloud volume; wet sheen is procedural without a reflection pass. Lightning strikes are visual and harmless. Low intentionally retains the existing disabled glass overlay. Future Phase 7 modes receive the documented API/swell hook; their implementations are outside this branch. No new radio dialogue required.
