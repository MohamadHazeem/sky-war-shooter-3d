# Phase 9 · Goal 05 — B-17 damage and crew

Implemented on `phase9/05-b17-damage-crew`.

- Heavy combat hits can cause governor runaway, cabin/bomb-bay fires, or damaged control cables. Shared 80–150 s cooldown, 30 s opening grace, difficulty tuning, and event scheduling guards keep incidents intermittent. Existing engine-fire simulation remains active.
- Hold the remappable X action at either waist gun for a fuselage fire, or at the top turret for bomb-bay access / engine feathering. Holds take 1.2–3 s, reset when interrupted, and suppress gunfire. Smoke, flame, extinguisher spray, progress, engine states, and an interior action camera make the response visible. The engineer eventually feathers failed engines; wounded engineers cannot do so.
- The radio operator walks the catwalk with ammunition, an extinguisher, or a first-aid kit and crouches to work. First aid transfers the actual named helper into the wounded gunner's post. Wounded AI guns stop firing until relieved; roster wounds persist. No invented extra crewman appears at the gun.
- Cable damage produces bounded lateral/vertical drift and formation lag. Runaway props visibly overspeed; feathered props stop through the existing damage renderer.
- Bail-out uses a snapshot of the ten people actually aboard. Wounds delay exits; altitude limits crew escape time. The player gets the existing jump sequence plus visible canopy/suspension lines. Debrief lists every name and parachute/missing status. Skipping preserves the results, and the old random fatality roll cannot contradict this record. Duplicate player-ship chute timers were removed.
- Arabic covers all new prompts, outcomes, and radio lines. Seven Chatterbox clips were generated with `python3 tools/voice/generate.py --only dc_` (0 rejected); only those seven manifest entries changed. README controls updated.

## Scope notes

The radio operator is the single spare helper; after taking a gun, he cannot service another casualty until round reassignment. Bomb-bay firefighting uses a guided access-hatch view from the top station, not free walking. Escape records describe parachute deployment, not subsequent rescue/POW outcomes; the player's chute is guaranteed. No required task items deferred. No dependencies installed, no push, no deployment.
