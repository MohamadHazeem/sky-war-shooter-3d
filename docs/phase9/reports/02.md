# Phase 9 · 02 — Bomb run drama

Implemented a mandatory 62-second gunner bomb run: IP inbound → straight and level → open bays → countdown → bombs away → falling bombs → strike → rally. A held Norden release extends it to at most 74 seconds; entering the bombsight remains optional. Target-bound campaign missions now all have releases; m6/m10 remain return-leg missions.

Walking flak boxes approach from alternating sides, activating the existing close-burst/shrapnel sound and shake. A nearby B-17 takes engine damage and catches fire. Fighters hold outside the barrage, then re-engage at rally. Formation evasions stop before release; the player ship lifts and pitches gently as the bombs leave. Bay wind swells, earlier-wave smoke and target indicators mark the ground, and overcast keeps the target obscured. Automatic releases now use the same wind-aware pattern and strike photograph as the bombsight, with an on/off-target debrief (50% inside 1,000 ft is on target); skill bonuses remain tied to the bombsight.
