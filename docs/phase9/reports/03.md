# Phase 9 / 03 — Gun feel 2.0

Implemented on `phase9/03-gun-feel`; baseline `4abe723`. No dependencies, new player-facing text, radio lines, push or deployment.

## Changes and hooks

- `src/game/GunRecoil.ts`: exact damped springs, with a flexible waist pintle and progressively firmer tail/top/ball mounts. Recruit receives 55% angular recoil. Actual shot impulses drive camera vibration at the weapon's modified cadence; reduced motion scales vibration to 25%.
- `GunnerController.ts:216,449,513,684`: attach/release barrel haze and per-gun springs; spring-back, current-frame muzzle sampling, independent receiver recoil, 300 m twin-gun convergence, every-fifth .50 tracer (including Overdrive), pooled spent links and existing casings. Aggregate damage/fire rate is unchanged when alternating paired guns.
- `Viewmodels.ts`: belt feed jiggle and one red tip in five; top turret now has two visible M2s, matching ball/tail pairing. `Brass.ts` reuses the bounded 96-piece flying pool for steel links. `LivingInterior.ts:653`, reached by `World.prewarm`, warms the link material. No new shader/pass.
- `Gunnery.ts`: shared drag-aware travel/drop/lead math, inherited bomber velocity, convergence and tracer burnout. `Ballistics.ts:175` fades non-cannon tracers over 1.15–1.55 s (about 0.93–1.23 km at 870 m/s); bullets remain live for their existing lifetime. Gravity, drag and collision damage remain unchanged.
- `Game.ts:368`, `GameHud.ts:159,281`: gunner assist and pip share the ballistic prediction. Ace gunner disables magnetism, hit-radius boosts and the lead pip, including perk overrides; pilot assistance is unchanged.
- `Effects.ts`: every shot gets a short flash; close gun smoke clears in 0.4 s and follows aircraft motion. Flash layers and cabin light respect reduced motion. Sustained-fire cooling is now .075 heat/s (idle .16, overheated .22); previously the stock 13 Hz M2 cooled faster than it heated. Stock overheating takes roughly 15 s without cooling upgrades. Heat haze starts at .35 heat and follows the barrel while cooling. Low keeps recoil, smoke, flash, belts, links and tracers; its existing haze-pass disable remains.
- Existing Phase 8 hit flashes, hit ticks, damage stages and layered M2 audio remain; top turret now uses the existing twin-gun audio mix.

## Validation

`npm run check`: **64 files / 674 tests passed**, including 10 new gun-feel tests: frame-rate-independent recoil, station/difficulty response, settling, cadence/accessibility, sustained heat, rotated convergence, travel/drop, crossing/climbing-target interception at 30/60/144 Hz, and burnout without bullet removal. Existing ballistics tests also pass. `git diff --check` passes.

Chrome/Playwright used the worktree's Vite server on **5293**, with RAF frozen and simulation advanced through `sws.step`. Both browser scripts use an existing Playwright installation. `tools/gun_feel_browser_check.mjs` reproduces the accuracy runs; `tools/gun_feel_visual_check.mjs` reproduces station sequences and runtime assertions. No browser/shader errors. The final server was stopped after verification.

Live assertions cover one moving breech after one top-turret shot, two top guns, tracer cadence, recoil settling, live hot haze/links, Ace assist=0/hitScale=1, 25% reduced vibration and haze cleanup on mission exit. Shader program counts do not grow during firing: **107 Low / 114 High**. Timings and raw assertions are in [checks.json](../shots/03/checks.json); these are local smoke-test timings, not a before/after performance claim.

## Before / after accuracy

Campaign m1, fresh browser profile per run, seeded RNG 903, default high assist, both scripted skill levels, 16,000-step cap. **All six before and all six after runs completed successfully.** One run per cell: random-number consumption, wall-clock behavior and earned perks differ, so this is a sanity check rather than a statistical balance claim. The autoplay aiming algorithm itself was not changed.
