# Phase 9 goal 06 — Four-player B-17 crew

Implemented on `phase9/06-crew-coop-4p`. No push, deployment, dependency installation, or leaderboard submission.

## Changes and hooks

- `src/game/CoopSession.ts:113,264,412,599,825`: one host-owned `CoopLink` per guest, independent sequenced input, gun cooldowns, shot ownership, hit/kill ledgers and personalized reliable debriefs. Four crew slots choose among five gun positions; all connected players must ready up. Host-authoritative seat reservations also cover in-flight transitions. Empty/disconnected stations return to AI without interrupting other links. Late joins receive the current sortie; departed slots get fresh offers under the same displayed room code. Historical combat credit survives departures; only sortie participants share rewards.
- `src/net/Crew.ts`, `Signal.ts:55,123`: seat/ping validation and snapshot budget helpers. Three adjacent-code signalling documents reuse the existing per-link Firestore security rules; guests verify the same host when trying the next slot. The lobby exposes only the base six-character code. Rooms remain available for late joins, expire after the existing two hours, and are deleted on host leave. P-51 sessions retain one guest.
- `src/game/CrewPings.ts`, `GameHud.ts:319`, `core/KeyMap.ts:24`: remappable **U** and a HUD button share a target or clock direction. Calls reuse the existing recorded Tail clock lines and their Arabic translations; no new voice assets are needed. Five-second cues, host-stamped sender IDs, two-second rate limits and sortie epochs prevent stale/spam pings. Target rings use `MarkerProjection` visibility, clouds/fog, live-target and front/on-screen checks. Standard HUD shows the direction cue; Full HUD enables target rings; Minimal retains its clean display.
- `CoopScreen.ts:195`, `i18n/ar/coop.ts`: four-person roster, ready states, occupied guns and Arabic copy. `CoopResult.ts:49` splits personal combat points and shared bonuses without rounding inflation. `Leaderboard.ts:39`, `LeaderboardPanel.ts`, `firestore.rules:221`: up to four full callsigns, 89 characters for `coop-gunner`; pilot/solo limits remain 43/20.
- Aircraft snapshots reuse the compact encoding, trim sampled tracers, and adapt their rate up to 20 Hz within a 27 KB/s payload budget. No materials or shaders added; Low remains supported. B-17 co-op uses Survival with boss/special/mid-round events disabled because those entities are outside the shared aircraft protocol.
