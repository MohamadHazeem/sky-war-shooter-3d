# Phase 9 · Goal 04 — Deep Penetration

Implemented a separate five-mission historical chapter under **Fly a Mission → Deep Penetration**, unlocked after the first tour. Original mission IDs/order, unlocked index, campaign strategy and second-tour operations remain intact. Chapter completion uses existing per-mission Air Medals (`dp1`–`dp5`); failures do not unlock the next operation, and completed missions remain replayable.

| Mission | Signature / Phase 8 tactic | Target silhouette | Autoplay result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Regensburg, 17 Aug 1943 | Straggler / multi-axis | Assembly halls, runway, river | Victory, 288 s, 34 kills, 1 bomber(s) lost |
| Schweinfurt, 14 Oct 1943 | Bomb-bay explosion / air bombs | Long bearing-factory roofs | Victory, 279 s, 52 kills, 1 bomber(s) lost |
| Leipzig, 20 Feb 1944 | Little Friends / Me 410 stand-off | Assembly works, crossing runway | Victory, 323 s, 39 kills, 0 bomber(s) lost |
| Berlin, 6 Mar 1944 | Collision / multi-axis | Broad rail yard and city grid | Victory, 336 s, 50 kills, 1 bomber(s) lost |
| Merseburg, 2 Nov 1944 | Direct flak hit / armoured wedge | Leuna tank farm | Victory, 300 s, 51 kills, 1 bomber(s) lost |

Each mission has weather, a date-appropriate enemy pool, a bomb run, three voiced briefing paragraphs and a voiced crew-room story beat. Jets appear only in the November mission. Regensburg's chart continues south toward North Africa and skips the English landing scene. All new interface, mission and dialogue text has Arabic.
