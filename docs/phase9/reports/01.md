# Phase 9 / 01 — Living formation

Implemented on `phase9/01-living-formation`; no push or deployment.

- Neighbouring B-17s have persistent damage scars, stopped/feathered prop visuals, existing engine fire/smoke, two-engine straggling, and up to two opportunistic fighter pursuers. Red/Blue/Gold flight markings and individual nose art identify nearby ships.
- Formation records track station/damaged/straggler/lost states, lead/deputy succession and gradual contraction after losses. The player's slot stays fixed; movement respects 48 m separation. Scripted set pieces retain their separate loss accounting.
- Combat losses select spin, two-piece fuselage break, explosion or controlled glide. Chutes run on simulation time, follow the wreck, and count successful pool spawns (4/3/0/5 respectively). New recorded calls count through five chutes. No wall-clock callbacks for neighbouring casualties.
- Damaged companions can ditch once per campaign return leg (75–96% route progress), using the warmed sea/spray studio. It records one combat loss and restores the gunner view; disabled bookends skip the scene but retain the loss.
- Neighbouring guns fire ballistic tracers and can kill close attackers. `livingFormation.kills` tracks their finishes; they do not automatically award the player kills. No new HUD panel.
- Pools: 16 instanced scars per neighbour, 64 reusable chutes and 27 warmed B-17 wreck sections. Wreck-pool exhaustion uses the existing transient wreck allocation fallback. Identity/scars, smoke and gunner work have distance LOD; Low retains every gameplay state.
