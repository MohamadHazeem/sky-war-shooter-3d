# Phase 8 goal 04 — Luftwaffe tactics

Implemented on `phase8/04-luftwaffe-tactics`. Extends the existing CompanyFront, Pincer, Jet and BK 5 machinery; Wgr. 21 / Zerstörer specials remain available.

## Behavior and tuning

| Tactic | Tuning / counter |
|---|---|
| Sturm wedge | Eight Fw 190 A-8s by default; API clamps to 6–12. Level at twelve, 3,800 m ahead, 65 m lateral / 48 m aft slot spacing, 1.55× structure and zone HP. Anchor closes at 285 m/s. No firing before 10.18 s; opens inside 900 m. Top turret: engage early. |
| Me 410 | Two existing BK 5 heavies, entering at 1,900 m. Every shell is gated to 800–1,200 m, including the second shot. 3.8 s minimum aiming warning, two shells 1.6 s apart; 640 m/s projectile, existing flash, smoke and bright tracer. Sustained long-range fire breaks the run. |
| Air bombing | Three Bf 109s start 3,200 m ahead / 420 m above, 90 m apart. Eight-second release lockout; ballistic intercept check before release. 4.5 s fuse, inherited velocity plus 72 m/s downward ejection, gravity, 105 m blast radius, maximum 85 damage with distance falloff. Top turret can kill carriers before release. Pool holds 12 bombs, relative to formation for floating-origin safety. |
| Jet slash | Two Me 262s, one above and one below six, 3,600–4,200 m astern, initial speed 255 m/s. Existing full-power passes; minimum six-second setup, gunfire inside 800 m, break at 200 m. Tail gun counter. |
| Multi-axis | Existing synchronized pincer: two pairs at three and nine, with at least eight seconds before simultaneous release. Choose a waist and defeat one pair. |

The director offers one pack per eligible round, after 25 s, with at least 35 s remaining and within the 32-enemy cap. It respects boss rounds, pacing holds, specials, tutorials, pilot roles and restricted custom scenarios. New groups and random specials wait 12 s after a pack arrives. Campaign unlock thresholds use the **displayed mission number** (2/4/6/8/10), so pilot missions defer their lesson to a subsequent gunner sortie. Survival unlocks over rounds 2–6; boss rounds defer appearances. Later rounds revisit the unlocked tactics. Classic wave paths are hooked too.

Each tactic has a recorded warning and Arabic text. Six-second, single-line first-encounter tips queue behind existing tips, honor Minimal HUD, and appear once per tactic per application session. Existing generic Me 410, jet and pincer encounters also teach their respective counter.

## Scope notes

Optional ram finisher omitted. The Me 410 retains its existing Bf 110 stand-in model and repeating approach/break-away behavior; 800–1,200 m is its firing band, not a stationary hover. Air bombs use a high, level approach with a release warning rather than a prolonged orbit. Existing five playable gunner stations are unchanged; the top turret is the playable head-on counter (chin/nose are not additional selectable seats). First-encounter knowledge is session-local. Dev server stopped after verification. No push or deployment.
