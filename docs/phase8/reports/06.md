# Phase 8 / 06 — Gunner hit feedback

Implemented on `phase8/06-hit-feedback`. No dependencies, new player text, or radio recordings.

## Changes and hooks

- `DamagePanel.ts`, `DamageStages.ts`, `DamageLook.ts`: allocation-free projection onto aircraft triangles (including quantized GLTF); a small 70 ms panel flash replaces whole-aircraft glow. Seven preallocated skin patches per fighter, larger pooled tumbling panels, engine-zone oil/glycol streams, and smooth prop spin-down/windmilling. Existing staged smoke, fire, canopy glints and bailout sequences remain.
- `FighterDeath.ts:219,324`: slump the model's pilot on a cockpit kill; hide him when the existing parachute sequence starts. Models without a separate pilot node retain their existing bailout visuals.
- `InteriorDamage.ts`: twelve reusable interior patches with torn insulation, dangling/sparking wires, hydraulic streaks/drops, altitude-dependent frost and slipstream flecks. `GunnerController.ts:150,220,310,518` attaches, resets, places and updates them beside existing bullet holes. The existing wounded-crew animation remains in use. Hole beams are dimmer so they do not obscure the compartment.
- `GlassFx.ts`: intersect the actual projectile path with the turret pane; small hits create radial fractures, heavy hits add spider-web rings. Four persistent impacts per pane, reset on mission attach. A swept 32 m close-pass check transfers spray from damaged enemies to the occupied turret/tail glass, throttled to 10 Hz with a 1.5 s cooldown. Low retains battle damage while disabling glass weather, frost and scratches.
- `World.ts:309–322` warms the new damage materials and existing hole/shaft materials; `World.ts:565` routes player hits to panel feedback. `Effects.ts:499` adds an optional debris scale. Existing rate-limited `HitSounds` ticks and `GameHud`'s Minimal/Standard/Full policy are preserved.
- `main.ts:153` installs the development-only `hitFeedbackQa.ts` fixture: `sws.hitFeedback.enemy(range, stage, type)`, `.strike(range)`, `.interior()`, `.glass(heavy)`. These freeze simulation; resume with `sws.engine.start()`.
