# Phase 8 · 05 — Gunner soundscape and interphone

Implemented on `phase8/05-soundscape`.

## Changes and hooks

- Four independently panned radial layers now lose the corresponding engine when damaged; fuselage damage raises the air-leak loop. New interior M2/receiver, brass, thin-metal hits, near/far flak, shrapnel, masked breath and turret recordings retain procedural fallbacks. Existing positional attenuation, panning and propagation delay handle enemy fire/flak.
- Independent master/music/voice/SFX routing now includes ambience and reverb returns. Fixed-size duck envelopes preserve crew priority through bursts without allocating per frame. Reduced audio disables extra receiver/HRTF/recorded-breath detail; continuous damage and cabin updates allocate no new objects.
- Situational type + clock calls, missed-pass feedback, wounded-crew responses, spaced check-ins and quiet-time chatter. Voice playback remains serialized; ordinary chatter is limited to three lines per ten seconds with a 650 ms gap.
- 34 Chatterbox clips cover new chatter, nine type prefixes and all 11 previously deferred templates. Arabic covers every added phrase and all 324 type/clock combinations. Open-ended names, locations and victory totals remain in subtitles; their recordings use finite contextual equivalents.
- Shared hooks: `src/game/Game.ts:536` supplies engine identity and skin damage; `src/game/Director.ts:179` advances conversation; `src/world/World.ts:1029` supplies actual aircraft type and clock. Core implementation: `src/audio/{Sound,Soundscape,Cabin,Breathing,TurretDrive,Voice}.ts`, `src/game/RadioChatter.ts`, `src/i18n/ar/{soundscape,radio}.ts`, phrase registration and generated voice tables/manifests.
