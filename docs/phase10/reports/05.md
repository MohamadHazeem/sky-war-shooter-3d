# Phase 10 · Goal 05 — Luftwaffe hero models: report

Branch `phase10/05-luftwaffe-models`. The five FlightGear enemy models (Bf 109, Fw 190, Me 262, Bf 110, Ju 88) are
replaced in the game by own-work hero models: Bf 109 G-6, Fw 190 A-8, Me 262 A-1a, Bf 110 G-2 and Ju 88 A-4.
Each is built from scratch by a headless Blender pipeline and has named structural parts, three LODs, hinged
control surfaces, a cockpit with a pilot figure, a baked PBR atlas with period camouflage and weathering, and an ace
livery. `npm run check` passes (67 files, 712 tests).

## What was built

**Pipeline: `tools/hero/luftwaffe/`** (one command: `npm run models:luftwaffe` or `sh tools/hero/luftwaffe/build.sh [kinds]`,
about 90–100 s per type on this Mac). See its `README.md`.
- **Shape** (`geom.py`, `parts.py`, `types/<kind>.py`):
  - Fuselages, nacelles, canopies and the Ju 88 glazing are lofted through cross-section stations (super-ellipse halves, monotone-cubic between stations).
  - Wings, tailplanes and fins use NACA 4-digit sections over a planform function, with rounded tips. They are split into the named parts, with the control surfaces cut on their hinge lines and a 7 mm gap.
  - Detail: cockpit cut-out and tub, instrument panel with dials, seat, head armour, Revi, pilot figure (helmet, goggles, jacket, arms on stick and throttle), Bf 110 rear gunner facing aft, Ju 88 crew of three, and canopy frames.
  - Per type: G-6 Beulen and supercharger intake; Fw 190 annular cowl with the 12-blade cooling fan; Me 262 Jumo 004 nacelles with starter bullets and nozzle cones; Ju 88 "beetle eye" glazing, Bola gondola and annular radiators; Bf 110 long glasshouse, MG 81Z, twin fins and chin coolers.
  - Common: ogive spinners, twisted blades, exhaust stubs, radiators, guns, antenna wire, and gear for parked aircraft.
  - Every type keeps the anchor points of the FlightGear model it replaces (prop hubs, eye, muzzles, fans, `gun_rear`), so existing gun offsets and code still line up.
- **Bakes** (`build.py`): one shared UV atlas per type. Cycles (Metal) bakes position, normal, paint-class/node and surface-coordinate maps, plus AO (48 samples; gear and blades hidden).
- **Paint** (`paint.py`, numpy, per texel):
  - Camouflage: RLM 74/75/76 (109, 190), 81/82/76 (262), 74/75/76 mottled (110) and 70/71/65 splinter (88). It uses splinter regions in plan view, soft blotches, side mottles and sprayed, wavy demarcations.
  - Markings: Balkenkreuz only (late-war outline style on top surfaces; **no Hakenkreuz**), yellow under-cowls, JG 1 red band, JG 7 red/blue band, ZG 76 / KG 54 style codes, numbers in a stencil font, the 109's fuel triangle, and spiral spinners.
  - Panel lines, hatches, rivet rows and fabric rib tapes go into a height field, which becomes a tangent-space normal map in the atlas's own UV frame.
  - Weathering: exhaust staining, gun soot at every muzzle, oil streaks under engines, airflow dirt streaks, sun fading, and chipping to aluminium and primer at walkways, leading edges, seams, canopy frames and blade tips. Baked AO goes into the ORM map and, lightly, the colour.
- **Ace liveries**, generated the same way: kill bars on the rudder, Kommandeur chevrons, emblem (JG 54 green heart on the 109, JG 53 Pik-As on the 190), white spinner and a unit band.
- **LODs**: LOD0 is the separate parts (moving controls, crew, cockpit). LOD1 is the whole airframe merged into one mesh at 40 %. LOD2 is merged and decimated to 2.6–4.2k triangles. All three share the atlas.
- **Finish** (`finish.mjs`, glTF Transform): base colour 2048, normal and ORM 1024, WebP textures, meshopt geometry, pivots kept off quantized nodes, glass material. Output goes to `public/models/<kind>.glb` + `<kind>_ace.webp`.
- **QA tools**: `turntable.html` (4-view or single-camera, LOD and ace switch), `hero_shots.mjs` (turntables + in-game rig shots), `hero_bench.mjs` (24-fighter stress bench), and `HERO_DEBUG` / `HERO_UVCHECK` diagnostics.

**Named-parts contract: `docs/phase10/hero-parts.md`** (for goal 09 destruction):
- Parts: `airframe/part_fuselage|part_fuselage_aft|part_tail|part_wingL|part_wingLtip|part_wingR|part_wingRtip|part_engine1|part_engine2`.
- Control surfaces: `ctl_aileron_*`, `ctl_elevator_*`, `ctl_rudder[_L/_R]`, `ctl_flap_*`, hinged about local X.
- Root nodes: `prop_N` with `spinner_N` and `blades_N`, plus `glass`, `pilot`, `crew`, `interior`, `gear_*`, `fan_N`, `cooling_fan`, `gun_rear/muzzle_rear` and the markers.

**Game integration** (new `src/world/HeroLook.ts`, plus hook lines):
- `HeroLOD`: picks the level from on-screen size (distance / model scale / FOV zoom). Thresholds are 45 and 140 model metres, so the 6x enemy fighters switch at about 270 m and 840 m. **Low pins every hero to LOD2** and drops the normal/ORM maps on the shared skins (lite shader).
- `prepareHero`: builds the LODs; pilot, crew and cockpit tub drop out past LOD0; gear is hidden; sky env on skin and glass.
- `animateControls`: ailerons, elevators and rudders follow `FlightModel` roll/pitch/yaw (allocation-free).
- `applyHeroAce`: lazy-loads `<kind>_ace.webp` onto the fighter's own material copies. The map already exists, so there is no shader change.
- `heroParts` / `partCentre` / `insidePlanes`: `Wrecks.cut` breaks hero aircraft along their parts. Each part, prop and crew node goes whole with the piece containing its centre; pieces stay on LOD0; no clipping, so no new programs. A twin loses its engine with the wing.
- `setGear`: parked aircraft (GroundTargets) stand on their gear.

## Performance

`hero_bench.mjs`: 24 frozen enemy fighters, all five types, 120–1500 m in front of the left waist gunner, `sws.bench(240)`,
1600×900, this Mac. The base checkout and this branch were run alternately on the same port. Frame times are noisy (±1.5 ms
between identical runs); calls and triangles are whole-frame totals.

| | High avg / p95 (ms) | Low avg / p95 (ms) | Draw calls H / L | Triangles H |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Before (3 runs) | 10.5–12.1 / 13.2–15.2 | 7.9–8.9 / 11.7–12.2 | 587–620 / 567–598 | 760–804k |
| After (final, 2 runs) | 12.0–12.1 / 14.4–14.5 | 7.7–8.9 / 11.4–12.6 | 490–493 / 463–493 | 660–679k |

- About 20 % fewer draw calls and 13 % fewer triangles. Past LOD0 an aircraft is one airframe call plus props and glass.
- Low is at parity (far LOD + lite skin).
- High costs about 0.5–1 ms more with 24 full-PBR heroes on screen, from the normal/ORM sampling on large on-screen fighters.
- No extra shader programs: the renderer had 124 programs after the before capture run and 120 after this branch's (hero models skip the SurfaceDetail variants).
- An earlier version with parts at LOD1 cost 738 calls, so LOD1 was merged.

## Licence log

`public/models/CREDITS.md`:
- The five files are now **own work** (GPL-2.0-or-later like the code), generated entirely by the scripts in `tools/hero/luftwaffe/`. No third-party meshes, textures, drawings or photos are included or traced.
- Shapes come from public-domain dimension data (span, length, wing area, chord/prop figures). The FlightGear sections are kept and marked as replaced.
- Liveries are generic period schemes with invented aircraft numbers, Balkenkreuz only.

## Pillar 1 self-score (enemy aircraft)

**Before 1–2 → after 5/10.**

What improved:
- Correct, recognisable silhouettes for all five types from every angle: G-6 hood and Beulen, Fw 190 radial ring and fan, Me 262 chine and swept wing, Bf 110 glasshouse and twin fins, Ju 88 beetle-eye nose.
- Real PBR with baked AO and normal detail.
- Period camouflage with crisp national and unit markings, and readable exhaust and oil weathering.
- Cockpits with pilots, spinners with spirals, and moving controls.
- Structural parts that break cleanly.

Still missing for 10:
- Shapes are lofted from dimension figures, not fitted to 3-view drawings. Wing-root fillets, cowling panel bulges and some intakes are simplified.
- The normal map comes from a procedural height field, not a sculpted high-poly mesh. Rivets are sub-pixel at gun ranges.
- The crew figures are low-detail.
- Ju 88 Junkers "double wing" flaps are modelled as plain hinged flaps.
- No KTX2 (see below).
- Textures are at 6–10 mm/texel. A 4096 atlas would sharpen the twins, at more GPU memory.

## Not done / notes

- **KTX2/Basis**: not used. No Basis encoder (toktx/basisu) is installed and the task rules forbid network installs. Textures are WebP; `finish.mjs` marks where a `toktx()` step goes.
- **Me 410**: no own model (optional in the task). It still flies the (now hero) Bf 110 stand-in with the EnemyDress cannon tray and barbettes.
- **Gear**: modelled and posed down, hidden in flight and shown on parked aircraft. No retraction animation.
- **Flaps**: modelled as separate hinged parts, but not deflected (no flap state in `FlightModel`).
- **Untested pages**: the gallery/hangar (`AircraftGallery`) and the Replay proxies use the new models through the same `instantiate` path; I didn't capture them.
- **Outside edits**: seven `tools/*_browser_check.mjs` / `tools/voice/browser_check.mjs` files in this worktree were changed by something outside this session at 14:12 (`channel: 'chrome'` removed). They are **not** part of my commit and are left as they were.
- **Leftovers**: a temporary detached worktree of the base commit was used for the "before" bench and has been removed. Intermediates (`tools/hero/luftwaffe/out/`) are git-ignored.
