# Phase 10 · Goal 06 — Effects 2.0: report

Branch `worktree-agent-aea4d26ce4c29b27f` (fast-forwarded to `main` 24e045f first: the Phase 10 specs were only on main).

## What changed

### Offline simulation pipeline (Blender Mantaflow -> relightable flipbooks)
- `tools/fx/bake_flipbooks.py`: one headless Blender script per effect. Mantaflow simulates a real gas
  (smoke + fire) at 128³ from a lumpy, displaced, growing emitter (open domain, reduced buoyancy so a
  mid-air burst stays in frame). The simulated density and flame grids are then baked with a small
  numpy volume renderer (exact for axis-aligned lights: cumulative optical depth, single scattering +
  3-octave multiple-scattering approximation) into a directional lighting basis per frame:
  - `A` RGBA: light from +X, −X, +Y (top), opacity
  - `B` RGBA: light from −Y (bottom), front, flame emission (sqrt), max(opacity, emission)
  Light from behind is derived in game from the opacity. Values are normalised by opacity so thin
  wisps keep precision. (WebP drops colour under fully transparent pixels, hence every alpha channel
  covers the colour data it carries.)
- Six effects, 64 frames each (8×8, 128 px): `explosion` (aircraft blast), `fireball` (rolling fuel
  fireball / cook-off), `flak` (88 mm: orange heart inside an instantly black lumpy puff), `fire`
  (engine-fire lick turning to smoke), `smoke` (oily black / grey, debris dust), `wisp` (white glycol,
  vapour).
- `pack` step: all six into one pair of atlases `public/fx/fx_a.webp` + `fx_b.webp`
  (2048×3072, **2.6 MB total**, budget 12 MB). Lazy-loaded the first time a tier above Low needs them;
  `vite.config.ts` puts `fx/` in the service worker's media cache (not the shell).
- `tools/fx/bake_all.sh`: bakes all six in parallel, writes lit preview contact sheets and packs.
  Intermediates (per-effect lossless atlases, float `.npz`, logs, previews, sim cache) go to the
  git-ignored `tools/fx/out/`. Re-bake: `sh tools/fx/bake_all.sh` (~15 min on this Mac, 6 processes).

### In game (Medium and up; Low keeps the old sprites)
- `src/render/Flipbooks.ts` — `FlipbookFX`: one instanced, back-to-front sorted draw for every flipbook
  particle. Per pixel: six-way relighting from the live sun direction/colour, sky from above, ground
  bounce from below, forward scattering through thin edges when the sun is behind, cloud-layer
  shadow on the sun side, blackbody flame ramp cooling with the particle's heat, frame cross-fading
  (+ timing curves so fire frames pass fast and smoke rolls slowly), procedural "cauliflower" detail
  noise where a texel is magnified (billow dents, eroded edge, hot/cool flame pockets), soft depth
  fade, near-eye fade, aerial-perspective haze, and a 2.5 px minimum size (opacity compensated) so
  far flak still reads. Delay / fade-in per particle. Pooled typed arrays, no per-frame allocation.
- `src/render/SoftDepth.ts` — half-resolution linear depth copy taken between the opaque and the
  transparent pass, only on frames where a soft effect is visible; also carries `clouds.sunShadow`.
- `src/render/SmokeRibbons.ts` — damage smoke trails as continuous camera-facing ribbon volumes:
  ring-buffered nodes that drift with the air and the wind, widen with age (faster in stronger wind)
  and thin as they widen; shaded as a cylinder cross-section eroded by scrolling noise, sun-lit with
  forward scattering, soft, hazed. Stateless `feed()` matches emitters by position (one draw call).
- `src/render/Sparks.ts` — sparks, flak fragments and explosion embers as motion-stretched cooling
  streaks (re-uses the tracer streak renderer).
- `src/render/Particles.ts` `TracerRenderer` — HDR white-hot core, coloured glow and a wide faint halo
  (quad 2.6× the streak width), brighter burning head; `flush()` for camera-less users.
- `src/world/Ballistics.ts` — the player's fresh tracers leave a short heat shimmer (HeatHaze).
- `src/render/Contrails.ts` — ice-crystal lighting: Henyey-Greenstein forward scattering (g 0.7) so
  trails toward a low sun glow gold, live sky colour as ambient; removed per-frame allocations.
- `src/render/LivingSky.ts` — the distant flak fields sample the simulated flak puff (once loaded).
- `src/world/Effects.ts` — wiring:
  - explosions: close (< 1.8 km) the crisp ray-marched fireball stays for the burning second, then the
    simulated smoke rises out of it (flipbook from its smoke frames, delayed cross-fade) — the old hard
    spherical smoke balls are replaced by simulated smoke puffs; at range the whole simulated blast
    (fire + smoke) is one flipbook; lobes are simulated fireballs; embers are streaks; cook-off pops
    follow the same hybrid (and their flash no longer outshines the fireball).
  - flak: simulated burst + 1–2 lumps at any range (no more sprite fallback beyond 4.5 km),
    fragments as streaks.
  - trails: simulated flame licks oriented to stream back from the engine, a flickering point light
    from the combat-light pool lights the burning wing / nacelle / neighbour (< 260 m), the smoke
    ribbon plus sparse, jittered simulated puffs (oily black, grey, or white glycol by damage level).
  - hit sparks as streaks.
- `src/dev/fxShots.ts` + `tools/fx/shot-server.mjs` — deterministic before/after captures (explosion
  0.3 s and 1.6 s, flak barrage, burning neighbour, contrails at sunset, tracers). The goal-00 capture
  rig is not merged yet, so this goal has its own small one.

## Pillar 5 self-score (Effects): 1 → 5 / 10
Big steps: flak puffs are now dense, lumpy, cauliflower-edged black bursts that hold together and fray
(the old ones were soft noise spheres); explosion smoke rolls as simulated smoke instead of hard
spheres; burning engines stream turbulent, oriented flames that light the airframe, into a continuous
widening smoke ribbon; sparks/embers fly as streaks; contrails turn gold toward a low sun.
Not yet film-grade: 128 px frames are soft when an effect fills the screen (the detail noise helps,
not fully), the simulated smoke is a little smooth/round at the end of its life (Mantaflow at 128³
diffuses), trail smoke still shows some puff structure up close, no true volumetric shadowing between
effects and aircraft.

## Not done / follow-ups
- Burning debris pieces still use the old sprite flames/smoke (only their sparks changed).
- Ground dust and hit puffs still use sprites.
- KTX2/Basis was not used (no `basisu`/`toktx` on this Mac); WebP lossy at q88 with lossless alpha.
- Low preset was checked by code path (Low → `hiFx` off: no flipbook loading, ribbons/streaks off) and
  unit tests; the in-browser Low run was blocked at the end by another session's popup in the shared
  browser pane.
- Higher sim resolution (192³ → 128 px with supersampling) or 256 px frames for close-ups would raise
  close-range detail at ~4× atlas size; a motion-vector flipbook blend would allow fewer frames.
