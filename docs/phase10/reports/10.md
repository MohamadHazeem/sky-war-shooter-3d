## What changed

**Combat director** (`src/game/CombatDirector.ts`, rules in `src/game/CineDirectorRules.ts`)
- *Close passes*: every 0.1 s the director looks for a fighter whose closest approach to the player's
  aircraft is 0.3–0.95 s away, 0.5–2.4 host radii (+30 m) out, faster than 55 m/s relative. The shot
  plays at real speed into the pass, drops to 0.22× from 0.3 s before the closest approach to 0.2 s
  after, then eases back (3.2 s real). Cameras: **waist window** (gunner at a waist gun: inside the
  fuselage, the window frame, gun and ammo belt in defocused foreground, the fighter flashing past),
  **wing-tip camera** (rigid on the tip, rolls with the aircraft, looks back inboard past the engines),
  **telephoto** from a neighbouring B-17's waist window (long lens framing fighter + the player's ship,
  handheld pan lag and sway).
- *Kill follows*: an ordinary credited kill (special kills keep the existing kill cam) may get a
  2.7 s follow of the breaking-up fighter, then straight back to the gun: **16 mm gun camera** (B&W,
  4:3 pillarbox, soft, heavy grain, gate weave, flicker, dust, gun vibration), **handheld chase**
  (keeps pace with the extrapolated flight path as it comes apart), **telephoto** from a neighbour.
  0.45× slow motion easing to 1.
- *Rules* ("rarely, never during critical moments"): Settings **Combat director** off / rare (default:
  75 s cooldown, max 4 per mission, 30 s warm-up, 60 % of passes / 25 % of kills) / often (38 s, 9,
  15 s). Never in the tutorial, co-op, photo mode, bomb run at the sight, boss battle, ditching,
  formation return, station change; never when the player is hurt or under fire (`playerFit`: integrity
  under 50 %, 3.5 % lost in 3 s, 3+ wounded); never with another fighter pressing an attack (closing
  inside 1.2–1.5 km or inside 500 m); shares spacing with the kill cam / drama cam.
- `ShotRig` places the camera every frame from the live aircraft (directions smoothed, no stored scene
  positions → floating-origin safe), restores `camera.up`, and reports slow motion, FOV and focus.

**Film look** (`src/render/FilmLook.ts`, parameters `src/render/FilmParams.ts`)
- *Per-object motion blur*: the nearest aircraft / wreck pieces that move against the camera (up to
  6 in play, 10 in a shot) are drawn again into a half-res velocity buffer with each **mesh's own
  previous world matrix** (spinning wreckage and props blur too), camera-relative (rebase-proof);
  tile-max (16 px) + neighbour-max, then a reconstruction pass blurs the object over the background
  with a depth test against the scene. Shutter in seconds of real time (1/48 s in shots, 1/60 in play),
  so slow motion looks like a high-speed camera.
- *Bokeh DOF*: physical thin-lens CoC from the FOV (full-frame gate) and f-stop with an artistic
  multiplier, half-res scatter-as-gather (Gustafsson) with near-field bleed, full-res composite, a
  focus puller easing in log space. Every cinematic (old kill cam, drama, fly-by, replay, theatre
  director, new shots) focuses on its subject.
- *Film pass* (LDR, after the grade): gate weave (24 fps damped random walk + a hair of rotation,
  overscan), halation (red-orange; white on B&W), spherical lens ghosts + halo from the bloom buffer
  (no anamorphic streaks), grades **Kodachrome**, **B&W newsreel**, **16 mm gun camera**, natural cos⁴
  vignette, exposure flicker, clumped 24 fps emulsion grain weighted to mid-tones, dust and scratches.
- Settings **Film look**: off (default) / film / Kodachrome / newsreel B&W — the play look, and the
  hook for goal 12's Cinematic preset (`engine.post.film.setLook('film')`).

**Combat film** (`Theatre.openReel`, debrief button "▶ Combat film"): the 90 s recording's best moments
(`reelSegments`: highest mark types first, −3.5 s/+1.8 s, merged, time order, up to 4) cut together by
the theatre director with a "COMBAT FILM" title card, 0.4× through each moment, newsreel B&W
(Kodachrome when that is the player's look), dip to black; any key/click ends it. The theatre
director gained **telephoto-from-a-neighbour** and **wing-tip** shots and drives the DOF focus.

**Accessibility / HUD**: Reduce motion → no slow motion (director shots, kill cam, drama cam and
Game's kill slow-mo beats), no handheld sway / gun vibration, no grain (film and PostFX), weave,
flicker or dust. HUD detail Minimal drops the cinematic captions (letterbox + skip hint only). The HUD
is hidden during shots as before; input is blocked and given back as before.

**Porting to Renderer 2.0 (WebGPU/TSL)**: each pass is texture(s)+depth+camera → texture with plain
uniforms; `FilmLook.pre/target/present` are the only three call sites. The velocity pass needs "draw
this object list with an override material" + a per-mesh previous-matrix uniform (`onBeforeRender`
here; a storage buffer or `MeshBasicNodeMaterial` with a `previousModelWorldMatrix` node in TSL, which
three's WebGPU renderer already tracks for its own velocity node). If Renderer 2.0 brings TAA with a
velocity buffer, `ObjectMotionBlur` should read that buffer instead of drawing its own.
