# Phase 10 · Goal 12 — Presets & performance

Performance acceptance remains open: the changes reduce GPU work and close warmup gaps, but the requested frame-time budgets are not all met on this shared M5 Pro. No push or deployment.

## Changes

- `Engine.ts:48`: tuned cloud resolution/steps and Medium/Ultra shadow sizes. Medium/High shade distant sky/terrain at half resolution; Ultra at ¾. Aircraft, interiors and depth edges keep their original pixel budgets. Low keeps direct sky, no TAA/AO, no temporal clouds or heat haze.
- `SkyCache.ts` + `Engine.ts:459`: separate HDR background target, shared live sky/terrain uniforms, preserved renderer state. `Sky.ts` retains the original material for environment captures. Both background programs warm before gameplay.
- `TemporalFrame.ts:194`: 1/2/3 AO slices by tier, rotated through the existing temporal history; one shader program.
- `BomberStream.ts:89`: atmosphere lookup moves from every overlapping fragment to segmented vertices; fewer trail segments. Stream trails/smoke/flak now respect cloud occlusion. Planar contrails, ribbons, precipitation and LivingSky billboards use one double-sided draw instead of two.
- `Engine.ts:390`, `FrameTiming.ts`, `AutoGraphics.ts:48`, `QualityPick.ts`: DRS reacts at 8% over target, ignores actual tab-pause intervals, resets stale ceilings after preset/detection changes. AutoGraphics allows 32 warm frames for the stream bake; low-core/low-memory devices start Low and cap at Medium.
- `FilmLook.ts`, `FilmParams.ts`, `UI.ts`: Cinematic defaults to Film; an explicit Off/stock selection survives tier changes. Reused FilmLook flags and HeatHaze clear colour remove small hot-path allocations. All labels already have Arabic translations.
- `World.ts:296/388`, `MissionFlow.ts:136`, `PlayerBomberDamage.ts:432`, `DustMotes.ts:171`, `InteriorAssets.ts:168`, `FilmLook.ts:621`: warm intact fighters, real damage geometry, parachute lines, persistent dust, both interior tangent variants, two-sided depth and the soft-depth copy. Film velocity now warms with the actual world lights and HDR output target. Later missions refresh mission-owned/lazy materials without rebuilding retained wreck pools.
- `VisualRigConfig.ts`, `VisualRigPage.ts`, `tools/visual_rig.mjs`: add Cinematic capture support and include the previously unbound bomber stream and optional interior/hero/FX assets. These fixture corrections were also applied to the **before** captures.

AutoGraphics picked **High at scale 1** on the M5 Pro (18 logical cores, reported 32 GB), and **Low at scale 0.8** under the emulated 2-core/4-GB, CPU ×4 profile. All stress checks had zero page errors and stable program counts.

The DRS check feeds the actual GPU-fenced frozen-scene render cost (minimum 16.67 ms display interval) into the real controller for 720 frames, measuring the last 180. It excludes gameplay simulation and cannot establish sustained combat 60 fps. Frame-time tails remain above 16.67 ms.

Low was also rendered with CDP CPU slowdown ×4 and with CPU ×4 plus four times Low's pixel budget. The latter is a GPU **workload proxy**, not a physical GPU frequency throttle. Both retain Low's cheap rendering paths. This verifies functionality under stress, not 60 fps on a real weak laptop. [Raw validation and timing](../shots/12/after/validation.json).

## Remaining acceptance gaps

- Mean rig budgets pass **0/16 Low, 0/16 Medium, 0/16 High, 3/16 Ultra, 15/16 Cinematic**. Cinematic formation-chase is 20.49 ms against 20 ms. Further scene traversal/draw submission reduction is needed; this is not a performance sign-off.
- DRS reacts and reaches its floor where needed, but sustained combat 60 fps is not established by the frozen-scene controller check. Real weak-laptop testing and physical GPU throttling remain unperformed; the CPU/fill stress profile is explicitly a proxy.
- Shader checks cover these rendered combat runs and all rig fixtures, not every campaign event. Existing hull-obstructed seat fixtures remain for subsequent visual QA.
