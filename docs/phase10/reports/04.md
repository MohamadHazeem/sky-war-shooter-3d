# Phase 10 · Goal 04 — Ground from altitude 2.0 (Europe 1943–44)

Branch `worktree-agent-a782c2fa71362b889` (fast-forwarded to `main` 24e045f for the Phase 10 specs).

## What changed

The far ground is still drawn by the sky dome's ray/plane intersection (no terrain meshes, works with
the cloud composite and every preset), but it is now **real Europe at true scale** instead of the
schematic, along-track-compressed coast strip and the procedural 96 km atlas.

**Real data, offline** (`tools/terrain/`, licences in `public/terrain/CREDITS.md`, in-game Credits `credits.p5` EN/AR):
- ESA WorldCover 2021 (CC BY 4.0), read by HTTP range requests from the COGs' 4500 px overview: coastline,
  lakes, canals and wide rivers, woods, meadows, heath/dunes, built-up areas.
- Natural Earth 10m (public domain): coastline where WorldCover has no data, narrow river courses
  (with per-river widths), railways.
- GeoNames cities5000 (CC BY 4.0): towns, scaled toward their 1940s extent.
- AWS Terrain Tiles / Terrarium z9 (SRTM, EU-DEM …): relief slopes, lowland (polder) areas, shallow water.
- 1943 edits: Flevoland is open water (drained 1957-68).

Raw downloads stay in a scratch directory (`fetch_raw.mjs <dir>`); `build_terrain.mjs` writes
**102 tiles of 64 km (15.9 MB) + a 2 km overview (0.24 MB)**, i.e. under the ~20 MB budget. Each tile
packs a 512² lines image (coast and river/rail *signed side distance* fields: crisp lines at any
texel size), a 256² cover image and a 128² relief image, row-delta coded and gzipped (format in
`tools/terrain/README.md`). Tiles are lazy: a mission fetches only its 3×3 block (≈1.5 MB),
decoded with `DecompressionStream`; the service worker does not precache them (`vite.config.ts`,
`public/_headers`).

**Mission on the map** (`src/world/TerrainGrid.ts`, `FarTerrainData.ts`, generated `TerrainIndex.ts`):
outbound missions end on the real target, approached on the real final leg (IP → target bearing);
the aim point lands on the target's real river or harbour shore (Bremen on the Weser, Emden on the
Ems, Kiel on the Förde …), and the target city's docks, works, rail yard and bridges follow that
water (`TargetGround.ts` hook). Homeward legs (Netherlands, Channel) cross the real coast just
past mid-mission. Scale is 1:1 (the old strip compressed 500-900 km into 10-20 km).

**Shading** (`src/render/FarTerrain.ts`, GLSL in the sky dome):
- Land use, procedural on top of the real shares: irregular farm blocks (jittered Voronoi) ploughed
  in their own direction, split into strips grouped into fields; long polder parcels with ditches on
  lowland; meadows / heath / woods per field from WorldCover shares; woods with organic edges,
  conifer/deciduous mix; hedges and lanes on some block borders; procedural roads with villages at
  their crossings; towns with district street grids, dense old centres, yards and gardens.
- Mapped rivers (with banks) and railways; sand and dunes only along open water; tidal flats.
- Seasonal palettes: summer, autumn (stubble/ploughed), winter snow (blue snow light), and a new
  spring palette (March-May) chosen from the mission date.
- Relief shading from the slopes (exaggerated ×2.6 for 7 km of air).
- Water: Cox-Munk sun glint with wind-lane patchiness, two swell trains (faded by footprint),
  Fresnel sky reflection, depth tint.
- Cloud shadows: the upper layer's shadow map (as before) **plus the low deck**, approximated from the
  cloud renderer's own weather map and shape noise (3 fetches, High+).
- Aerial perspective at altitude: aerosol (1.5 km scale height) + Rayleigh integrated along the slant
  path; the old distance haze is kept below 1.5 km so Countryside / Airbase seams still match.
- Distance LOD: texture mips, the 2 km overview beyond the 3×3 block, and pixel-footprint fades from
  strips → fields → regional mean colours (no shimmer).
- Low: one coast lookup and flat seasonal colours (as before, now on the real coast).

## Performance

GPU-synced render-only frames (`sws.ground.ab`, 90 frames × 3 alternating rounds): full terrain vs the
same program forced to the Low ground (one fetch), so the delta is the terrain's own cost. The Mac was
shared with ~10 agents (CPU-saturated), so single rounds were noisy; medians of the per-round deltas:

| View / preset | Resolution | Frame (full) | Terrain cost |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Straight down, High | 2022×1137 | ~9.6 ms | **~1.4 ms** |
| Horizon, High | 2022×1137 | ~10.8 ms | **~1.3 ms** |
| Horizon, Medium | 1632×918 | 8.7 ms | **0.43 ms** |
| Horizon, Low | — | 4.7 ms | unchanged path |

An earlier unoptimised version cost 2.3-2.5 ms on High; distance branches (no field/Voronoi work once a
farm block is a few pixels, a 2×2 instead of 3×3 site search, coast/wood/road noise only where it can
show) brought it to ~1.4 ms. No per-frame allocations; no new materials or shader variants (the sky
dome's program changes; preset switches only change uniforms). One-time costs per mission: 9 tile
fetches (≈1.5 MB), inflate and copy into three region textures (1536², 768², 384² RGBA8, with mips
≈ 16 MB GPU); the old 2048² atlas (21 MB GPU, ~580 ms bake at module load) is gone.

## Pillar 4 self-score

**Before 1 → after 6/10.** The ground from altitude is now recognisably the real place at true scale
(coastlines, estuaries, islands, rivers, forests, towns where they are), with seasons, glint and cloud
shadows that read like reconnaissance photos from 25,000 ft. Not 10 yet: fields are still a procedural
mosaic (Voronoi blocks read as polygons up close), towns are generic street grids, the target city's
works/docks are still TargetGround's stylised blocks, relief is shading only (no parallax or silhouettes),
and the haze/aerial perspective was tuned on this goal's own model; goal 03's sky should take it over.

## Not done / notes

- Elevation shading only; no displaced terrain (from 7.6 km relief is subtle on these routes).
- Railways and rivers are Natural Earth (main lines/rivers only); minor roads, canals narrower than
  WorldCover's ~50 m and field boundaries are procedural.
- Town sizes come from modern populations scaled down; WorldCover built-up is modern sprawl kept only
  near historic town radii. Only Flevoland is reverted to 1943 water.
- Homeward and outbound legs no longer show England and the whole sea crossing compressed into one
  mission (the old schematic strip); each mission shows its true-scale stretch (target approach or
  coast crossing). Kent appears only far ahead on the Channel leg.
- The low-deck cloud shadow reads the cloud renderer's private weather map / shape noise
  (`syncFarTerrain`, guarded: disabled if goal 03 renames them); it is an approximation of the
  cloud shapes, not a true shadow map of the deck.
- `farAerial` replaces Sky.ts's ground haze; if goal 03 brings a full aerial-perspective model, swap
  it in there (one call site).
- No push, no deploy. Dev server and save server stopped.
