# Phase 10 · 02b — FlightGear-based B-17G rework

Replaced the rejected procedural exterior with the pinned FlightGear B-17G geometry. The slimmer fuselage, canopy, nose, nacelles, wings and source turrets are retained; detail is added around those surfaces. **Pillar 1: 6/10** — substantially better shape and material control, still below the film-quality target.

## Delivered

- GPL FlightGear source and GD-L livery, with corrected derivative credits and a reproducible source/conversion/build recipe in [tools/hero/README.md](../../../tools/hero/README.md). Source commit: `949abb7615da96104a72c2b1a351a05d60a0bf3c`.
- **31.62 m span, 22.66 m overall length**; staggered starboard waist aperture/glazing/gun/markers; retained chin, ball and top turrets; Cheyenne-style tail sight enclosure; cowl-flap plates, exhaust/turbo/wastegate housings, conforming de-icer strips, antenna and pitot fittings.
- **1,364,112-vertex actual-aircraft relief source**, Cycles tangent normal/AO bakes, curvature derivation, panel seams/rivets/slotted access fasteners, faded olive/neutral-grey atlas with retained source soot/oil staining, chipped edges and scuffed walkways. Independent natural-metal colour/ORM atlases retain markings. Optional invasion stripes and existing nose art remain supported.
- Seven `part_*` owners and joint/fuel markers follow the goal-09 contract read from `main`. Existing prop/gun/gear/flap/bay pivots remain. No stream-LOD contract was present on this branch or `main`; the existing merged distant-stream integration remains.
- LODs: **71,655 / 32,129 / 21,620 triangles**, including optional markings and stopped props. Low preserves economical exterior surfaces, removes cabin/crew detail and reduces mechanical detail. Aggressive whole-skin decimation was rejected after it visibly broke layered surfaces.
- Actual **Basis UASTC KTX2**, mipmaps, local decoder/licence, and all five WebP fallbacks. Embedded startup maps are 256 px; streamed colour is 2048 px, normal/ORM 1024 px. Geometry is about **1.06 MiB**; manifest total including both texture formats and decoder is **8.89 MiB**, under 15 MiB. Encoder 2.50.0 was extracted locally from the Homebrew bottle; no shared npm dependencies or system ownership changed.

## Silhouette evidence

Reference: public sample of **USAAF AN 01-20EG-2, 15 February 1945, Figure 1, printed page 2** ([scan](https://app.aircorpslibrary.com/document/getsamplepage/tsteven3/6)). Only manually digitized contour coordinates are committed; no scan pixels or archive watermark are redistributed.

`silhouette-export.py` projects the exported LOD0 triangles; `silhouette.py` compares them to the trace at a fixed 31.752 pixels/metre. Legacy and hero use the same documented registration. Four scan pixels of contour uncertainty are allowed; raw overlap is also reported. The artifact hash is checked by the unit suite.

Section cuts: forward fuselage **2.370 m vs 2.289 m** traced width (**3.5%**); outboard nacelle at Z=2.2 m **1.402 m vs 1.481 m** (**5.3%**); both within the stated 10% section tolerance. [Full measurements, registration, tolerances and artifact hash](../shots/02b/silhouette.json).

The front view is weaker evidence: its thin projected surfaces are sensitive to scan roll and view pitch. This is a coarse accuracy/regression gate, **not** a certified restoration drawing match. No independent fuselage/nacelle scaling was used to fit the trace.

Low intentionally omits texture maps and transparent cabin detail. The goal-09 destruction integration is a named-parts contract check, not an end-to-end test of its unmerged implementation.
