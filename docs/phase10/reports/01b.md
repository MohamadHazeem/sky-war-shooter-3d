# Phase 10 · 01b — Visible renderer pass

Branch: `phase10/01-renderer`. **Implemented and verified, with performance acceptance still incomplete.**
High measures **8.33 ms** in the corrected live benchmark and exceeds 8 ms in 11 of the 16 frozen
rig scenes. Ultra averages **10.52 ms** live; all 16 Ultra scene means are below 14 ms. Frame-time
tails still exceed both budgets. This report does not close the performance goal.

## Visible changes

- Reduced cloud-floor IBL, hemisphere and artificial rim fill; increased the sun/fill ratio.
  Satin dielectric paint and oilier nacelles now show a defined highlight and shaded fuselage.
  Existing olive/neutral-grey textures and natural-metal classifications are retained.
- Corrected PCF bias for reversed depth, tightened near normal bias, and blended cascade visibility
  before evaluating **one** sun BRDF. Staggered mid/far refreshes avoid simultaneous update bursts.
- Aircraft now sample the upper-deck cloud-shadow map at each surface's sun-ray intersection.
  Removed the camera-only asynchronous probe, its readback, promises, target and warm-up hook.
- Replaced the eight-tap SSAO estimate with temporal, cosine-weighted horizon AO (three slices,
  four samples per side). Depth reconstruction snaps to sampled texel centres: GPU testing caught
  and corrected false occlusion on tilted flat panels. Tighter depth rejection and reduced TAA
  history weight improve moving detail retention.
- Stronger mood LUTs, cooler shadow tint and a lower print-black lift produce more separation.
  ACES and bounded exposure remain; measured all-channel white clipping is zero in all 16 shots.
  Daylight/interior changes are clear; the night fixture's improvement is comparatively subtle.
- Lens flare/shafts integrate at quarter resolution; clear sky no longer receives a uniform shaft
  veil. Sky renders last with far-plane depth testing. Invisible subtrees skip material sorting.
  High uses 0.45-scale, 56-step clouds while retaining its full 1920×1080 main image.

## Verification and performance

`npm run check`: **PASS — TypeScript, production build, 69 Vitest files / 719 tests**, including
voice-pipeline Python checks. `git diff --check`: PASS.

Both [fallback smoke sweeps](../shots/01b/smoke.json) pass: Low through Cinematic, ordinary/reversed
depth, finite HDR/AO/TAA, object velocity, camera cuts/rebase, studio, weather/IBL texture lifetime,
saved settings and zero sampled new shader programs. [GPU fixtures](../shots/01b/visible-runtime.json)
verify flat-plane visibility above 99%, additional crevice occlusion, visible geometry/cloud shadows
and rejection of a moving object's old TAA footprint.

Full High before/after and Ultra rig: 16 isolated 1080p fixtures each, zero browser/shader errors.
The comparison tool asserts identical cameras, weather and seed. Committed JPEG previews are 960 px;
lossless 1920×1080 PNGs remain local/ignored. Every committed JPEG is below 250 KB.

Apple M5 Pro / ANGLE Metal, Chrome 155.0.8059.41, DPR 1, DRS fixed at 1. Live measurement uses three
90-frame batches with simulation and GPU synchronization. Warm-up is fenced **before** measurement:
the previous method charged queued warm-up work to the first sample, producing false 75–440 ms
outliers. [Undrained](../shots/01b/performance-undrained.json), [drained/default pacing](../shots/01b/performance-paced.json),
and [final drained, frame-limit/vsync disabled](../shots/01b/performance.json) data are retained.
Other Blender/Node workloads were observed during captures; the spread prevents a dependable budget sign-off.
