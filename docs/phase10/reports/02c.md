# Phase 10 · 02c — B-17 material, marking and propeller polish

**Pillar 1: 7/10 (02b: 6/10).** Richer olive paint, readable markings, visible running propellers and stronger surface detail improve the close and waist views. Beauty, silver and Low retain those improvements. The widest formation shot remains a subtle improvement: aircraft occupy too few pixels to demonstrate rivet detail. I would not claim an unequivocal, large improvement in every view or a film-quality finish.

## Changes

- Rebuilt the FlightGear-derived hero with darker, panel-varied olive drab and neutral-grey undersides. Preserved light insignia and yellow prop tips; strengthened seam/rivet contrast, localized turbo soot and nacelle oil trails, chipped edges and worn wing-root walkways. Silver retains independent PBR maps and staining.
- Replaced baked **GD / 23772** lettering with small skin-conforming patches using a separate **1024 × 512 lossless alpha-tested atlas**, preserved at every LOD. Removed old lettering beneath the patches and factory nose script beneath personalized art. Increased the separate nose-art canvas to **1024 × 512** and separated legacy/hero geometry caches.
- Hero props keep their three yellow-tipped blades at idle and cruise, with a subtle RPM-dependent blur disc; engine loss stops the bomber prop and removes its disc. Other aircraft retain their existing behavior. No per-frame allocations or shader-feature changes.
- Low now retains a **512 px diffuse-only livery**, grey undersides, insignia and sharp stencil patches. Silver/desert recolour this embedded texture locally. Fresh Low startup still fetches no optional detail textures.
- Preserved structural owners, mechanical pivots and silhouette. LOD totals: **71,818 / 34,252 / 24,535 triangles**. Keeping props and stencil patches raises the old Low budget modestly; the test now enforces fewer than 26,000 triangles. GLB: **1.23 MiB**; complete manifest including KTX2, WebP and decoder: **9.54 MiB / 15 MiB**.

## Validation

- **`npm run check` passes:** TypeScript, production build, **67 test files / 705 tests**, plus **10 Python tests**. Added regression coverage for idle/cruise/dead-engine blade visibility, stable material versions, stencil resolution/alpha mode and diffuse-only Low materials.
- [Browser checks](../shots/02c/browser.json): four prop controllers, GPU-compressed detail, successful WebP recovery from corrupt/aborted KTX2, no page errors or warnings, zero optional texture requests on fresh Low startup. Scanned frames have no NaN/Inf, negative HDR values or clipped black/white clusters. Settled LOD sweeps add **zero shader programs** (hero: 140 → 140).
- [Artifact-bound silhouette gate](../shots/02c/silhouette.json) passes: tolerance-adjusted overlap **.957 top / .856 front / .936 side**; section errors **3.5% fuselage / 5.3% nacelle**. Same source drawing, registration and uncertainty as 02b.
- Local 45-frame spot samples: legacy High **5.03 ms mean / 5.9 p95**, hero High **11.17 / 14.1**, hero Low **6.59 / 9.0**. Hero costs more to draw; these short samples are not a general FPS guarantee. Harness `calls/tris` reflect the final pass, not total scene work.
