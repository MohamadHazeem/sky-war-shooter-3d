# Phase 10 / 09 — Physical destruction

Branch `worktree-agent-af9cbe4283b5722ad`. No new dependencies, assets or player-visible text. One new synthesised sound (`tear`).

## What changed

Enemy fighters now break apart where they are hit. Before, they were cut with clipping planes and played canned sequences.

- **Failure model** (`src/world/destruction/FailureModel.ts`, pure and deterministic with a seeded mulberry32).
  - Every round on an axis fighter is booked to one of seven structural parts: core, nose, tail, wingL/R, wingL/Rtip (`Parts.ts`).
  - Part strength is a share of the matching hit zone's hp. Kill timing still comes from the zones, so game balance is unchanged.
  - A part that is shot through comes off with its children: a wing takes its outer panel.
  - An outer panel can come off while the fighter keeps flying. It rolls toward the stump through the new `Fighter.rollTrim`.
  - Rounds on fuel tanks can start fires (per-kind fuel layout: fuselage tanks on the Fw 190 and Bf 109, wing tanks on the P-51 and twins, explosive T-Stoff on the Me 163).
  - Fire grows, eats the part's strength until it burns through, spreads to neighbouring parts, and a tank that burns long enough cooks off.
  - A doomed fighter's pilot bails out before the kill. The fire's burn is credited to whoever lit it.
  - `planEnding()` picks the break-up from where the damage is: which wing, root or tip, tail, or explosion.
- **Mesh separation** (`Split.ts`). Each model kind is cut once, triangle by triangle, along the part boundaries, so torn edges follow the real mesh.
  - Parts reuse the source vertex buffers with their own index buffers: no vertex copies and no new shader programs. Program count stayed at 115 through kills.
  - A reversed-winding copy in a dark material shows the inside of the skin through the hole.
  - Explosions shatter the part that held the burning tank into seeded triangle clusters (25–27 shards per type). The other parts fly off whole.
  - Split cost is 2–15 ms per kind, done during `World.prewarm`.
- **Flight physics for wreckage** (`Aero.ts`). Rigid bodies with flat-plate aerodynamics at real scale:
  - lifting wings with a rigging incidence;
  - a centre of pressure that travels with the angle of attack, so loose panels flutter and tumble;
  - rotational damping from the local airflow;
  - engine thrust that fades out.
  The hulk that lost a wing rolls toward the stump and autorotates. Without its tail it pitches over. Panels brake hard and fall at a bounded speed. Engines drop.
- **Runtime** (`Destruction.ts`, `World.destruction`):
  - the hulk is the aircraft itself, now drawn as its parts, so the pilot, props and damage decals stay on it;
  - a pool of 72 pieces; the dead aircraft's material copies are leased to its pieces and disposed with the last one;
  - fire trails from every burning part, smoke and fire from torn joints;
  - bail-outs from the hulk;
  - heavy pieces can strike other aircraft. `World.debrisHit` books the strike like a heavy round, credited to the player if the source was their kill;
  - sounds: `tear` (crack, popping rivets, shrieking skin, groaning spar), `whump` when a tank ignites, a `whoosh` for big pieces passing close, `hitHeavy` for debris strikes.
- **FighterDeath** asks `destruction.plan()` before falling back to its weighted random pick.
  - Fireball, wing and tail endings now go through the destruction system.
  - The engine-fire ending can include a bail-out, and its final blast is the destruction explosion.
  - `deathKindOf` reports `'bailout'` for pilots who get out of hulks, for the Nemesis code.
- **B-17 neighbours**. Losses from a shot-off wing, or a wing a fire burned through (55%), are now a `fold` on that side using the existing pre-warmed `wingFold` break (`FormationRules`, `LivingFormation`).
- **Co-op**. Part bits go in the snapshot flags (`flags >> F_PARTS`), so the guest sees wingtips shot off and fires. The kill event carries the ending (`d`), so the guest plays the same break-up (`forceDeath`). The guest's puppet runs `destruction.update(dt, true)`, which is motion and visuals only.
- **Named-parts contract** for hero models: [09-parts-contract.md](../09-parts-contract.md). It covers `part_<name>`, `joint_<name>` and `fuel_<name>` nodes and the per-kind cut shares and masses in `Parts.ts`. I also added a short pointer in `ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## Performance (this Mac, shared with other agents, 1280×720 tab)

`sws.destruction.perf(120)`: eight enemies 120–300 m off the waist, then all breaking up at once (2 explosions, 2 wing-offs, 2 tail-offs, 2 fuel fires). The result is 26 pieces and 4 hulks.

| Preset | Quiet avg / p50 / p95 / max (ms) | Eight break-ups avg / p50 / p95 / max (ms) |
|---|---|---|
| High | 8.68 / 8.3 / 12.1 / 15.7 | 8.91 / 8.2 / 13.7 / 27.3 |
| Low | 10.63 / 8.1 / 26.8 / 35.6 | 6.70 / 5.4 / 12.9 / 27.7 |

- Shader programs stayed at 115 through a fireball kill and a wing-off kill: nothing compiles at kill time.
- Frame times after a single kill were 6.2–7.9 ms, with no hitch.
- The two maxima (27–28 ms) are single frames. They are probably the first upload of the part index buffers.
- The Low quiet sample is noisy because the machine is shared. It establishes that Low works, not a difference between presets.
- No per-frame allocations in the destruction update: scratch vectors only. Emitters run on 0.05/0.1 s ticks, stop beyond 2.6 km and are capped by the particle pools.
