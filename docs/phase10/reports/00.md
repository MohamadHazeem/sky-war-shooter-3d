## Limits / handoff

WebGPU rendering cannot be exercised until goal 01 supplies `src/dev/VisualRigWebGPU.ts`; the flag was checked to fail clearly rather than silently label WebGL output as WebGPU. Hardware/browser changes may change pixels. Timing is 60 frozen renders plus a blocking GPU fence, excluding simulation and image encoding; it is not gameplay FPS. A still-image aliasing estimate cannot measure temporal shimmer. Re-scoring JPEG-only checkouts is supported and explicitly labeled lossy. Generated scorecards overwrite manual edits; keep judgments in goal reports.

The port 5300 server was stopped after verification. No push or deployment. The worktree's pre-existing untracked `node_modules` symlink is untouched and excluded from the commit.
