# Phase 10 · Goal 08 — The thousand-bomber stream: report

Status: **done** — implemented, unit-tested, `npm run check` green, captured and timed in the
browser (WebGL2, this Mac, port 5308), tuned after the first look (see "Visual tuning").

## What changed

A stream renderer (`src/render/BomberStream.ts`) that puts the player's combat box inside a real
8th Air Force bomber stream, plus its layout/motion model (`src/world/StreamLayout.ts`) and an
impostor baker (`src/render/Impostor.ts`).

- **Structure**: squadrons of 2–4 three-ship vics → combat boxes of 18–36 B-17s (lead / high / low
  squadrons) → combat wings of three boxes (lead / high / low groups, stacked ±330 m and staggered
  1.5 km to each side; ours is the lead group of wing 0) → wings in trail every ~4.2 km with a slow
  snake, plus a second bomb-division column 7.4 km to the left. Counts per tier
  (`STREAM_TIERS`): Low 162 bombers in 8 boxes, Medium ≈330, High 678 in 26 boxes (+16 escorts,
  26 gaggle fighters), Ultra ≈1,000 (capped). Bombers are drawn at 1.9× like the enlarged gameplay
  aircraft (box spacing scaled with them) so boxes read as aircraft, not dust, at 2–5 km.
- **Motion**: every stream aircraft is a closed-form function of time (box meander, ship wobble,
  bank/yaw from the drift), stored as 4 texels in a float data texture and evaluated in the vertex
  shaders (`STREAM_GLSL`, mirrored exactly in JS for the few CPU needs). CPU cost per frame is a
  few uniforms, a handful of flak/glow floats, and a 0.5 s LOD band check over ~30 boxes.
- **LODs**: impostor billboards for everything beyond ~1.1 km — atlases baked at load from the real
  models (16 azimuths × 8 elevations; albedo with coverage + object-space normal with a surface
  class; prop blades replaced by blur discs), 16 tiles per frame so the menu never stalls. Drawn as
  `MeshStandardMaterial`s with a patched vertex/fragment stage, so they get the same sun, sky
  hemisphere, rim and environment reflections as the real aircraft; 4-frame blend with exact
  per-frame UV reprojection (no popping as ships rock), alpha-to-coverage under MSAA, minimum
  on-screen size with ink conservation for the far dots. Boxes that come within 1.1 km switch to an
  **instanced mesh LOD** (the real B-17 merged to one draw per material). Natural-metal ships
  (per-group share) are made in-shader from the olive-drab bake.
- **Sun glints**: per ship, the sun's mirror off the fuselage cylinder and wing tops, winking as
  each ship rocks; stronger on natural metal (HDR emissive → bloom).
- **Contrails → persistent field**: one ribbon per engine (High/Ultra; 2 on Medium, 1 on Low),
  spreading, sinking and meandering with age, handing over to one wide, wispy, long-lived band per
  squadron (130–360 s, up to ~400 m wide) — the overlapping bands of the column make the contrail
  field. Air-fixed: they drift back at formation speed analytically. Weather's contrail switch
  applies. Haze matches the cloud pass.
- **Escorts**: P-51 (natural metal) and P-47 (olive drab) flights of four in finger-four, weaving
  S-turns above the stream (kept under the high cloud base), each with its own sinuous contrail.
- **Luftwaffe gaggles**: 6–16 Fw 190s making head-on passes through distant boxes (attack leg at
  ~260 m/s closing, wide swing back on one side), gun flashes, return-fire winks from the box,
  occasional fighter going down; every few passes the gaggle re-forms on another box.
- **Flak walking through other boxes**: barrages whose aim point walks from ahead of a distant box,
  through it, to behind it (black bursts with an orange flash), heavier when the gameplay flak is on.
- **Distant losses**: a bomber in a box under fire spirals down burning (or dives, or blows up in a
  flash and black smoke), trailing an analytic smoke ribbon, with chutes appearing over the next
  half minute; it vanishes into the cloud deck. Fighters fall too.
- **Low**: smaller stream, 64 px atlases, one contrail per bomber, no gaggle contrails; no MSAA →
  alpha-tested impostors.
- Night: the stream is hidden (`weatherNow.night`); it is only shown when there is a gameplay
  formation (`world.livingFormation`), so raid-defence / sea / no-bomber modes are unaffected.
- `LivingSky` keeps its dogfights; its own (old, 3–4 box) bomber boxes and their flak step aside
  while the stream is up (`boxesOff`).

## Visual tuning (after the first look)

- **Side-column / distant contrail bands** read as thick, hard white slabs (144 ribbons per box at
  26 m wide, alpha 0.7). Now 12 m wide at the end, alpha 0.5 → 0.1 and gone by 70 % of their life:
  thin parallel lines at distance, a light haze where they overlap.
- **Contrail veil**: the trails of boxes ahead washed out boxes flying behind them (seen at 800 m);
  fixed by the same change.
- **Impostor frame blend**: a wide 4-frame blend showed two views at once; now a short cross-fade
  around the half-way point (no visible doubles, still no pops).
- **Readability**: at life size + 1.3× the boxes were dust at 3–5 km; now 1.9× (box spacing scaled)
  and a 2 px minimum footprint.
- Unrelated, seen while capturing: white ice-crystal streaks over the top-turret sky are present with
  the stream hidden (not this goal).
