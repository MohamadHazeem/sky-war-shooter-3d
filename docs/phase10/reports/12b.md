# Phase 10 · Goal 12b — CPU frame time

All 80 rig scene means and all five heavy-combat means meet the task's budgets. CPU ×4 Low averages **13.91 ms**, below 16.7 ms. No gameplay shader compiles or browser errors occurred in the final combat runs. No push or deployment.

## Changes and hook lines

- `src/render/AircraftSubmission.ts`: render-only BatchedMesh submission with shared surfaces and independent damage tint, geometry, visibility and previous-frame transforms. Cached flat pose traversal skips hidden subtrees; aircraft spheres retain visible roots and refreshing shadow casters. Static matrices and main-camera multi-draw lists are reused. Unsupported meshes retain ordinary draws; source gameplay ownership stays intact. Reset releases batch resources.
- `src/core/Engine.ts:469/518`, `src/world/World.ts:258/297`: submit the prepared scene and prewarm batch, damage, compact and shadow variants before gameplay. `src/render/TemporalFrame.ts:49` supplies per-instance previous matrices for velocity. `src/world/Aircraft.ts:84/230`, `src/world/destruction/Destruction.ts:507` register aircraft and detached pieces; `src/world/DamageLook.ts:53` shares unchanged damage surfaces while preserving ace-livery overrides.
- `src/world/Models.ts:103`, `src/world/CompactBomber.ts`: freeze static authored transforms; retain live mechanical pivots. Bake a compact formation B-17 once. Low uses it beyond 120 m, Medium beyond 350 m; player aircraft, falling ships, open bomb bays and near views retain articulated models. High+ retain full models.
- `src/render/FilmLook.ts:257`: object velocity submits selected heroes and lights instead of traversing the full formation again. `src/render/DepthSort.ts`, `Flipbooks.ts:411`, `Explosions.ts`: stable merge sort with persistent scratch storage replaces quadratic particle ordering. `src/world/Ballistics.ts:112`: reusable team lists and conservative segment bounds reduce exact collision tests, including preserved player near-miss behavior.
- `src/core/Engine.ts:50`: Low uses 0.65 MP, half-size sky/terrain shading, 20-step/.18-scale clouds, neutral one-pixel shadow maps, no distant bomber stream/film/AO/TAA/haze and one bloom level. `Effects.ts:138` caps new legacy smoke/glow particles at 512/256. Medium uses 1.25 MP, 28-step/.24-scale clouds, 1024 close shadows and no AO/TAA/haze. High/Ultra/Cinematic visual settings are unchanged. Related hooks: `BomberStream.ts`, `SunCascades.ts`, `FilmParams.ts`, `Particles.ts`, `PostFX.ts`, `SkyCache.ts`.
- `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` documents the submission lifecycle. Existing performance tools accept explicit output/tier/headless-shell settings; new CPU profile, same-pose comparison and fallback validation scripts retain reproducible evidence. No assets, dependencies or player-visible strings were added.
