## What changed

Every Luftwaffe fighter now makes the decisions a skilled pilot would make, seen from the B-17's guns.
All decisions are in one pure, seeded module, **`src/world/PilotMind.ts`**. `EnemyPilot` (`src/world/AI.ts`)
flies them.

| Area | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Energy model | Each type's speeds come from its own flight-model numbers: 1 g stall `sqrt(g/(liftK·αstall))`, corner `vStall·sqrt(maxG)`, level top speed `cbrt(power/dragK)`, plus a never-exceed dive limit for each type. These are the "envelopes". After a pass, `energyPlan` picks one of four moves:<br>• **extend**: unload, nose about 14° low, full power, straight on, until back above corner speed<br>• **zoom**: trade the pass's speed for a perch above the box<br>• **dive**: trade height for speed<br>• **re-attack**<br>Jets never turn back in slow. Rookies (q < 0.35) re-attack at once, which is the old behaviour. `envelopeThrottle` keeps every type below its dive limit and never slow by choice. |
| Attack geometry | `chooseApproach` scores six approaches: head-on, stern, beam left, beam right, high and low. Each score adds up:<br>• the bomber's defensive cover for that sector (which guns reach it, and for how long)<br>• **weak guns**: a wounded gunner on the player's ship, or a shot-up tail or fuselage section<br>• the **dead-engine side**<br>• a **straggler** (from astern or below)<br>• the type's strengths (Fw 190 head-on; jets from astern, low or high; heavies on the beam)<br>• personality<br>A softmax picks the approach, and its temperature comes from decision quality. On the player's ship, a good pilot comes through a wounded gunner's sector (`station`). The navigator calls it (`ai_weak_*`), so the player can change seats. Otherwise attacks still come through the player's own arc (Phase 8 fairness). |
| Evasion | `Ballistics` counts the player's rounds that pass within 30 m of an aircraft (`Aircraft.fireNear`). The cost is one extra sphere test, made only on the player's bullets. Near misses and hits build "pressure", which fades over about 1 s. Above a threshold set by personality and quality, and after a reaction time of 0.9 s for rookies down to 0.3 s for aces, the pilot does one of three things:<br>• **jinks**: rolls the wings side to side and can't hold his aim<br>• **breaks**: a hard turn out of the line of fire, nose low<br>• **split-S**: only with room below the box<br>One evasion per pass, with a 5 s cooldown. Group tactics (Sturm wedge and others) never evade. A break gets "You drove him off". |
| Rotte / Schwarm | `Tactics.formSchwarme`: the free Fw 190s and Bf 109s of a director group fly as pairs, or as fours at q > 0.5, with chance `0.2 + 0.65q`. Wingmen hold a trail slot (`wingSlot`) and start their run `staggerDelay` after the leader's: 1.6–3.2 s, tighter in better units. The gunner sees each pass separately. The navigator calls "Pair coming in, one behind the other!" |
| Wounded | `woundedChoice` sets the limp threshold by personality: cautious 0.4, cunning 0.28, aggressive 0.2 (the old rule was 0.3 for everyone). A cautious pilot with a dying engine also limps. An aggressive pilot **presses on** for one more pass ("He's smoking but he's coming back in — finish him!"). Limping:<br>• power capped by the engine's health (0.3–0.45 throttle)<br>• wallowing wings<br>• he carries on his heading nose-down, then turns home over about 10 s, so he stays catchable |
| Personalities | Aggressive, cunning or cautious, mixed by type: Fw 190s lean aggressive; jets and heavies lean cautious. |
| Aces | Each Nemesis Experte gets a fixed persona from his trait and id (`acePersona`): a personality plus one **signature move**:<br>• **corkscrew run**: rolls through the run and only fires with the wings level<br>• **split-S** under the box (ball turret)<br>• **zoom** climb after the pass (top turret)<br>• **wingover** straight back in on the same gun<br>• **low six** up under the tail<br>On 70 % of passes he flies it as a clean slashing pass, without the duel weave. The crew learns it: the first time it is "He rolled over and split-S'd under the box!"; once known (met before, or seen earlier this mission) they call it ahead: "Here comes his split-S! Ball turret, catch him going down!". The dossier card shows "Aggressive · Signature: Split-S under the box" once the crew has met him, otherwise "Signature move: not yet seen". The yellow-nose Staffelkapitän gets a random signature move. |
| Difficulty | `decisionQuality(difficulty, skill, ace)`: Recruit about 0.25, Veteran about 0.55, Ace about 0.85, and aces get +0.2. It drives everything above: how well the approach is chosen, energy discipline, reaction time and evasion threshold, Schwarm chance and stagger. This is on top of the existing aim scaling. |
| Marker rules (Phase 8) | No new markers or HUD elements. The new states (`recover`, `splitS`) are pilot states only, and markers still come from the live aircraft. |
| Performance | No per-frame allocations: shared scratch vectors, and `targetState` refills one object. Allocations happen only once per decision (about one per pass). No new materials or shaders, so Low is unaffected. |

  - Cautious pilots limp; aggressive pilots press once, then limp, at reduced power.
  - The wingman starts his run within 0.1 s after the stagger delay.
