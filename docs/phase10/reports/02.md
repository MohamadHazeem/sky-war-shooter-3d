# Phase 10 · 02 — B-17 hero asset and pipeline

**Reviewable implementation; goal not fully complete.** The original B-17, Blender pipeline, runtime integration, tests and browser captures are implemented. The shipped textures are **WebP**, not Basis/KTX2: no encoder was installed, and the requested permission to download the official encoder remains unanswered. The KTX2 build/loader path exists but has not been executed or validated. No dependencies were installed, and nothing was pushed or deployed.

## Delivered

- Original parametric late-G exterior with a 31.62 m span, glazed nose and Norden sight representation, chin/cheek/top/ball/waist/tail guns, staggered waist apertures, Cheyenne-style fairing, radial engine detail, national/group/serial markings and existing per-ship nose art.
- Blender source generation, topology cleanup, explicit UVs, a 262,144-vertex relief pass, Cycles normal/AO bakes, curvature derivation, weathered olive drab/neutral grey PBR, transmission glass, and meshopt export. One-command rebuild: `HERO_WEBP=1 tools/hero/build.sh`; Basis path: `TOKTX=/path/to/toktx tools/hero/build.sh`.
- Three exported LODs: **32,010 / 12,185 / 3,288 triangles**, including optional markings and stopped prop blades. Shared mechanical pivots keep props/blur, guns, flaps, gear and bay doors articulated. Low uses LOD2; the existing distant stream uses the merged LOD2 in one instanced draw.
- Runtime geometry **516,252 bytes**, optional detail textures **101,654 bytes**; total **617,906 bytes (0.59 MiB)**. Startup maps are 128 px; optional maps are 1024 px. Low defers detail loading until a higher preset is selected.
- Paint schemes and nose art retained. Authored gear replaces the bookend gear when attached to the hero. Bomb-bay doors open around the existing release window. Player skin damage excludes glazing/weapons/decals; wreck clipping remains compatible.
- [Source/licence decision and rebuild instructions](../../../tools/hero/README.md), [asset credits](../../../public/models/CREDITS.md). No reference images or downloaded model parts are incorporated.

## Remaining work and self-score

**Pillar 1: 4/10.** This is a compact, reproducible asset foundation, not the Phase 10 film-quality target. Silhouette, separate glazing, mechanical nodes, original markings and a baked material pipeline improve the controllable asset contract, but close views still expose simplified cockpit/turret shapes, generic repeated skin relief and limited aircraft-specific weathering. More detailed reference-guided shaping and bespoke texture painting are needed before claiming a high-end hero finish.

Not completed: actual Basis/KTX2 generation and browser transcoding validation (encoder permission pending), integration with the unmerged goal-00 rig, full aircraft-specific sculpted panel/fastener placement, and film-quality visual fidelity. The existing foreground formation remains individual animated aircraft; only the distant stream is instanced, as needed for its static silhouettes. The legacy B-17 remains on disk for reference, but the central runtime model path uses the new asset everywhere.
