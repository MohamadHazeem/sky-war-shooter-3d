# Phase 10 · 01 — Renderer foundation

Branch: `phase10/01-renderer`. Baseline: `24e045f`. No dependencies installed, push or deployment.

## Result and renderer decision

Kept Three r186 **WebGL2**. The [TSL prototype](../../../src/dev/rendererProbe.ts) rendered 200 PBR boxes
at 1080p using both WebGPU and WebGPURenderer's forced WebGL2 backend on Chrome / Apple M5 Pro.
Both node backends reported `Material "ShaderMaterial" is not compatible` for the legacy-shader test.
The game depends on GLSL clouds, effects, surface-detail hooks and clipping; swapping renderers would
lose those systems. [Prototype measurements and warnings](../shots/01/prototype.json) are a small-scene
experiment, not a whole-game backend comparison. Production remains a directly tested WebGL2 path.

Implemented:

- Three texel-snapped, blended PCF sun cascades: close interior/aircraft, formation, and aircraft out
  to 2 km. Constant light counts across tiers; mid/far maps update every 4/12 frames. Low retains
  the close shadow map.
- Dynamic-sky PMREM with persistent capture materials/generator, no solar-disk double counting,
  three-second IBL updates through weather fronts, and disposal of replaced render targets.
  Direct sunlight uses documented scene-referred units (25,000 lux/unit).
- GPU log-luminance metering with bounded, asymmetric eye adaptation; ACES fit and five preloaded
  trilinear film LUTs (warm/cold, overcast, dusk, night), blended through fronts.
- Half-resolution SSAO with motion reprojection, depth rejection and bilateral upsampling.
- Eight-sample jittered HDR TAA with object/camera motion vectors, neighbourhood clipping,
  disocclusion rejection, HDR reactive weighting and bounded sharpening. Projection restoration,
  reversed-depth initialization, resize/cut/rebase resets, and removal of jitter from camera blur.
- Normalized bloom reconstruction and energy redistribution on temporal tiers. The saved lens-dirt
  option disables both bloom dirt and solar dirt. HDR fire/tracer emission is preserved.
- Low / Medium / High / Ultra / Cinematic. Medium/High use TAA; Ultra adds 2× MSAA, Cinematic 4×.
  AutoGraphics still measures the device and caps automatic selection at Ultra. Arabic labels included.
- Converted all remaining Lambert terrain/ground-target surfaces to Standard PBR; painted aircraft
  become dielectric and bare P-51 skin metallic. A one-time material audit bounds PBR inputs while
  preserving maps, emissions, custom shader hooks and **dynamic** upstream cache keys. Deliberately
  unlit particles, markers, decals and procedural shaders remain purpose-specific.

## Performance

Chrome 155, ANGLE Metal / Apple M5 Pro, 1920×1080 viewport, DPR 1, DRS fixed at 1. Measurements include
simulation and rendering, synchronized by a one-pixel GPU readback. Low/Medium retain their native pixel
budgets; High and above render at 1080p. The fresh-session benchmark runs three batches of 90 frames per
preset, separately from image encoding and the 16-shot scene sweep. Times include host/browser stalls.

| Preset | Render size | Median of batch p50 (ms) | Mean, all 270 frames (ms) | Worst batch p95 (ms) | New programs |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Low | 1264×711 | 7.80 | 7.62 | 9.00 | 0 |
| Medium | 1632×918 | 8.80 | 9.72 | 11.80 | 0 |
| High | 1920×1080 | 10.50 | 11.68 | 15.10 | 0 |
| Ultra | 1920×1080 | 12.50 | 14.44 | 17.70 | 0 |
| Cinematic | 1920×1080 | 13.40 | 15.95 | 19.50 | 0 |

**Performance acceptance is not fully met.** High exceeds the 8 ms target. Ultra meets 14 ms at the median, but its aggregate mean and p95 exceed that budget. Large outliers remain despite stable Three program IDs; their cause was not isolated. Earlier samples were faster, so the report retains the final multi-batch result rather than selecting the fastest run. Further profiling/batching work is needed for a dependable budget.

[Raw fresh-session measurements](../shots/01/performance.json). The capture-run timings are also retained
in the before/after JSON; they vary with host load and repeated scene construction. Neither measurement
is a claim that every combat scene or every individual frame meets budget.

## Self-scores and remaining limits

| Pillar | Score / 10 | Assessment |
|---|---:|---|
| 2 · Lighting & shadows | 6 | PBR/IBL, bounded exposure, distance cascades and temporal contact AO; still stylized analytic sky lighting |
| 7 · Camera & post | 6 | Real rigid-object velocity, TAA, film LUTs and stable camera blur; transparent effects use reactive rejection |
| 10 · Performance & polish | 6 | Low and ordinary-depth fallback verified; warm-up and resource lifetime checks; frame-time tails remain |

This is a foundation, not a 100/100 visual finish or a complete performance sign-off. Texture-level metal/paint masks remain part of the hero-asset work. SSAO was selected instead of GTAO; PCF instead of
PCSS. Far-cascade update cadence can lag fast aircraft. Velocity supports rigid meshes and current
instance transforms; independently moving instances and future skinned crew need previous-instance/
skinning data. Transparent props/tracers use reactive/clamped history, not per-particle velocity.
IBL captures the analytic sky/cirrus and cloud-floor approximation, not the volumetric cloud field.
The existing artistic hemisphere/rim fill remains. AutoGraphics' cost model is conservative, not a new
cross-device calibration. The minimal capture set does not replace goal 00's full reference board/rig.
