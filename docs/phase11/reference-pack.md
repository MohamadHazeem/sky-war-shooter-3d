## Acceptance for art work
- Same capture-rig shots before/after; the after must be clearly better in every touched shot (side-by-side sheet).
- Silhouette overlay within 5% on length/span; paint value variation by panel; markings crisp at the close camera.
- Budgets (goal 12b): Low ≤ 6 ms, High ≤ 11 ms at 1080p on the M5 Pro.
