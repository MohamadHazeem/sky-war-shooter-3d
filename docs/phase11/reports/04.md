# Phase 11 · 04 — effects review fixes

The rejected version's smooth flak, broad orange aircraft lighting and prolonged orange explosions have been revised. This report replaces the earlier self-score; acceptance is based on the matched captures and combat measurements below.

## Changes

- Flak: rebaked the 192³ simulation with slower outward gas velocity and a more irregular emitter, retaining 256 px projections. The smoke expands quickly, then drifts and disperses over 15–20 s. A separate 0.22 s ignition playback preserves the orange core instead of skipping its frames with the smoke's playback curve. The shader retains dense soot and sharper irregular margins, with the existing directional sun/self-shadow basis. At night a cool upper-hemisphere fill exposes the baked lobes and shaded cores instead of leaving flat black dots; daylight lighting is unchanged.
- Engine fires: reduced the B-17 fire lights from 26 m × scale / 700–1300 × scale intensity to 6 m × √scale / 65–110 × scale. At the fixture's 1.3 scale, range is 6.84 m and peak intensity is 143. The tail retains its green paint; the continuous smoke ribbons and heat shimmer remain.
- Explosions: play the flame-to-smoke simulation over the blast's combustion time instead of its entire smoke lifetime. Weak flame emission now fades out, exposing soot; secondary smoke appears earlier. Debris streak exposure increases from 35 to 85 ms, with narrower streaks and less drag.
- Cost: only explosion/flak use 256 px frames. Fireball, attached fire, smoke and vapour use 128 px. The atlas pair shrinks from 4096×5120 to 4096×3072: **213.3 → 128 MiB** including RGBA8 mipmaps (the original 128 px-only pair was 64 MiB). Engine smoke overlays use one puff per emission and debris smoke emits half as often; continuous ribbons carry the trail. Low flak uses the existing sprite fallback instead of raymarched volumes. Empty smoke pixels exit the shader before further texture/lighting work. Profiling identified the heat-haze offset pass at 1.61 ms GPU; its smooth displacement field now uses a quarter-resolution buffer (480×270 at 1080p), preserving full-resolution scene colour and the visible shimmer. A shared 16 KiB R8 lattice replaces repeated sine-hash noise in ribbons and haze; occluded/faded ribbon pixels exit early, and small flipbooks omit sub-texel detail sampling. Empty particle, tracer, ribbon and volume pools no longer request full-capacity buffer uploads. Empty FX meshes with camera-only callbacks also skip submission during gameplay; prewarm still includes them.
- Cleanup: removed draft–draft4 and duplicate PNGs/unreferenced combat JPEGs. Comparison generation reads the committed JPEGs, so the report is reproducible without untracked lossless copies.
