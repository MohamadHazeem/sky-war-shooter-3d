# Phase 11 · 04 — explosions, flak and fire 2.0

Close effects now ship 256 px frames, with simulated flame-to-soot transitions, rolling black flak, attached fuel flames, heat distortion and persistent burning-fragment trails. Visual assessment: pillar 5 reaches the requested 8/10 in the four matched shots below.

## Changes and hooks

- `public/fx/fx_{a,b}.webp`, `manifest.json`, `CREDITS.md`: six fresh Mantaflow simulations. Close effects use 192³ simulation grids and 256 px projections; diffuse smoke/vapour use 128³ / 128 px. Original procedural assets, GPL-2.0-or-later; no external downloads.
- `src/render/Flipbooks.ts:35`: shared mixed-resolution atlas sampling, half-texel insets, five-direction sun/internal-shadow data, softened flak extinction, and world-space flame direction projected for each camera. Delayed/fading emission now follows opacity. Late material registration correctly binds already-loaded atlases (`:169`).
- `src/world/Effects.ts:309`: nearby fuel fires feed the existing heat-haze pool. `:471` replaces the close spherical blast with simulated flame/soot and smaller secondary lobes; `:674` makes the orange flak core brief and smoke persist 15–20 s. Burning fragments feed owned ribbons plus sparse lit smoke (`:839`). Switching to Low immediately clears high-tier pools.
- `src/world/World.ts:827`: one camera-state refresh before simulation. Fixed stepping previously retained the menu camera and selected legacy sprites for the nearby burning aircraft. `src/render/SmokeRibbons.ts:154` adds emitter identity so crossing fragments cannot splice trails; softer, irregular margins remove the cylindrical outline. `LivingSky.ts:340` shares the new atlas layout and slower flak decay.
- `tools/fx/bake_flipbooks.py`, `bake_all.sh`: cached, slot-limited builds; bounded projection memory and two simulations per batch. No new materials, dependencies or player-visible strings.

## Cost and limits

The atlas pair downloads 5.01 MiB and uses approximately 213.3 MiB RGBA8 GPU memory including mipmaps (previous pair: 64 MiB). Mixed packing saves 42.7 MiB against six 256 px effects. Low retains its existing sprite path. Internal self-shadowing is baked directional optical depth; independent particles/aircraft do not cast shadows into smoke. Validation covers this Mac's bundled headless Chromium, not a physical weak laptop. All requested effects are implemented; combat budget qualification is outstanding.
