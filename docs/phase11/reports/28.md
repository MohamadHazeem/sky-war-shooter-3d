# Phase 11 · 28 — Dynamic in-mission events: report

Branch `phase11/28-mission-events`. A data-driven library of **22 short, readable gunner events**,
paced by a director that never lets them overlap with each other or with the existing set pieces,
random events or bonus-objective moments.

## The library (`src/game/MissionEventLib.ts`, pure data + rules)

Every event in the brief, plus twelve more. Each is 9–22 s long and made of timed beats: a radio
line (recorded clip + subtitle) and/or an action from a small vocabulary.

| Event | What happens |
|---|---|
| nearMiss | a B-17 slides in to 48 m off the gun window; the pilot jinks, the ship is rocked |
| friendlyExplodes | fire pours from a B-17's tanks, a drama shot, then she blows apart (scripted loss) |
| bailout | a B-17 in the gun's view is hit: two engines burning, she sinks out of the box slowly (wings almost level) while 4–6 chutes open beside her, counted live on the banner; then she spins in and the tail calls the count |
| jamming | radar dial fills with static and a JAMMED stamp, interphone static bursts, then signal back |
| oxygenFire | flash and sparks in the radio room, smoke pours out past the waist gun |
| gunnerFreezes | the other waist gun's AI crewman stops firing for 10 s (`World.frozenStations`); the radio calls it, and at HUD detail Full his station is struck through on the station bar |
| salute | a smoking, crippled Bf 109 slides up 40 m off the wing, waggles his wings, peels away; the crew's guns hold fire |
| lostMustang | a P-51 slides in alongside, asks to tag along, then joins the escort AI for the rest of the mission |
| cookOff | rounds cooking off in a neighbour's waist (sparks, pops, smoke), ammo boxes thrown out |

## Director (pacing) — `eventDue` / `eventFits` / `pickEvent`

- Gunner missions only; never in co-op, signature/tutorial missions or with `midRound: false`;
  never in a boss fight or the final wave; never in the first 30 s of a mission or within 15 s of a round edge.
- One at a time, 50–90 s apart, at most 6 a mission, never repeated in a mission. Weighted pick;
  a second B-17 loss in a row is less likely.
- 'calm' events need a quiet sky (≤2 fighters near, ≤4 in the breather); 'tense' ones ≤6; events that
  spawn fighters ≤4. 'loss' events share the set pieces' loss budget (`SP_MAX_LOSSES`, `World.scriptedLoss`),
  so they never count toward a mission's loss limit.
- **No overlap:** MidRound's objectives, random events and set pieces wait while an event runs and for
  8 s after (`events.busy`). Events wait for a random event (and 20 s after it starts), a set piece
  (and its quiet after), and the first/last 8 s of a bonus objective. A running objective may carry on:
  events never pick the ships it marks. While an event runs, low-priority crew chatter is held back
  (`World.holdRadio`); urgent combat calls still get through. **Events keep clear of the bomb run:** none may start unless it
  (with its staging) ends 14 s before the lead-in (IP call and bombsight offer, 30 s before bombs away;
  `bombLeadIn` / `EV_BOMB_CLEAR`), and a running one is stopped and put back if the lead-in begins.
- Neighbour events stage a B-17 into the gun's view first (`SetPieces.stage`, ~4 s slide), so they
  happen where the player is looking. Everything an event changes is put back when it ends or is
  stopped: slots, the frozen gun, the jamming, scripted aircraft, pinned attackers.

## UI (Phase 8 simple UI)

The banner ("IN FLIGHT · NO SHOT · A crippled Bf 109 pulls alongside"; bailout keeps a live chute count)
shows only at HUD detail **Full**: Phase 8's clean-HUD rule hides optional panels on Standard and Minimal,
and there the radio subtitles and the visuals carry the event. Loss events also get a drama shot
(kill-cam rules). All new text is in Arabic (banner, 22 titles and lines, 62 radio lines, the JAMMED stamp).

## Review round 2 (rejected shots) — what changed

- **bailout:** the ship is staged into the gun's view (as nearMiss is) and no longer drops out of
  frame: `fall` now sinks her at 6 m/s for 9.5 s (`Bomber.gliding`), two engines on fire with a fire
  trail, before she spins in. The six chutes come out within 3.4 s and drift near her
  (`Effects.parachute` takes an optional `drag`; the default 2.2 keeps every other chute the same).
  `bailout.jpg` / `bailout-full.jpg`: the burning B-17 with five open chutes and "Chutes: 5".
- **gunnerFreezes:** the radio line is now in the shot (see the harness fix below), and at HUD detail
  Full the quiet gun's station ("2 RIGHT WAIST") is struck through and blinks red until he's back
  (`gunnerFreezes-full.jpg`).
- **weatherWall:** the storm wall was invisible everywhere, not just here. Since the Phase 10 renderer
  work the sky dome draws last of the opaques (`renderOrder` 1000, no depth write) and painted over
  the wall at -999. `StormWall` now draws at 1001, right after the dome. It is a one-line change, and it
  also brings back the wall in missions with their own storm fronts. The event's front also comes
  sooner (14 s instead of 25 s), so the wall is large while the navigator calls it. The shot is from
  the top turret looking ahead, the gun that faces the wall.
- **lostMustang / weatherWall bombsight prompt:** fixed both in the director (the bomb-run rule
  above) and in the harness (below). No shot has a bomb-run prompt; the harness now fails if one does.
- **brassRain:** the falling pieces are now small brass .50 cal cases: 72 instanced, tumbling
  casings that use the waist pile's own geometry and material (`Brass.ts`, so nothing new compiles:
  0 programs during the event). They are simulated relative to the gun window and drawn 2.4× true size
  so they read.
- **salute-full "P-51" marker:** it was the 109 itself. It is moved to the allied team so the guns
  leave it alone, and allied fighters got the friendly chevron labelled P-51. New
  `Aircraft.noMarker` (one skip in `GameHud`'s marker loop) removes the marker from the saluting 109
  and the burning Fw 190 crosser.
- **Shot harness (`tools/mission_events_check.mjs`):** a fresh mission for every event (all shots
  are taken about 25 s in, long before bombs away at 85 s). The start-of-mission pilot line runs on a
  2.5 s real-time timer, so the harness lets it play out first. Subtitles appear on a real-time
  interphone timer, so frames are now driven in chunks with a real pause after each of the event's
  lines. Every shot now shows that event's own line. The harness fails on a visible bombsight
  offer or an active bomb run.
- Tests: `bombLeadIn` and the fit rule; a running event stopped and put back when the lead-in
  begins (16 tests in `missionEvents.test.ts`).
- Round 2 CI (full suite): [green run](https://github.com/MohamadHazeem/sky-war-shooter-3d/actions/runs/38757465161).
