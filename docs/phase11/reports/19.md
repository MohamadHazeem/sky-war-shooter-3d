# Phase 11 · 19 — Seasons and fair leaderboards: report

Branch `phase11/19-seasons`. Firestore rules and indexes were **changed, not deployed** (per the spec: merge only).

## What changed
- **Monthly seasons** (`src/game/Seasons.ts`): seasons are UTC calendar months, keyed `yyyymm`, numbered from
  Season 1 = October 2026. Every new score carries `season`; the rules require it to equal the server's current month
  (`request.time.year() * 100 + request.time.month()`). So season boards reset by themselves on the 1st, and past
  seasons are frozen archives (no write can target them).
- **Season boards and archives** (`src/ui/LeaderboardPanel.ts`): boards get a **Season** tab (the default) next to
  **All time** and **Squadron**. The season view shows "Season N · ends in 16d 04h", ‹ › buttons to browse past seasons
  ("final standings"), the player's tier progress, and a "Your past seasons" list (tier, scores posted, best). Daily and
  weekly boards keep their two tabs (they are already time-limited).
- **Plausibility checks in the rules**: every entry now carries `duration` (mission seconds, from `DebriefData.time`).
  - HARD limits (rejected): the existing points-per-kill/round cap, `score <= duration*1500 + 20000`,
    `kills*2 <= duration + 20` (30 kills/min), `rounds*10 <= duration + 60` (10 s per round), duration 0..36000.
  - SOFT limits (`score > kills*2500 + rounds*2000 + 3000`, `> 24k points/min`, `> 15 kills/min`) make the rules
    require `shadow: true`.
  - The numbers live once in `Seasons.LIMITS`; a test checks that `firestore.rules` uses the same numbers.
- **Shadow ban**: new `shadowbans/{uid}` collection. Clients can only `get` their own doc (needed to tag scores);
  nobody can write it from a client. Entries go in through the Firebase console / Admin SDK. A banned player's entries
  must be `shadow: true`. Shadow entries are stored and their owner still sees them (their own best is merged into the
  season board, and shadow rows that are theirs show in all-time and squadron boards), but other players never see them.
  The season query filters `shadow == false` on the server; the all-time and squadron boards filter on the client
  (older entries have no flag).
- **Season rewards (cosmetics)**: posted, non-shadow scores count as season sorties. 3 / 10 / 25 in one season reach
  Bronze / Silver / Gold. Each tier gives a `season:<yyyymm>:<tier>` ribbon and, the first time ever, an exclusive nose
  art: Bronze/Silver/Gold Season Wings (`src/render/NoseArt.ts`, drawn on canvas like the others). They are exclusive, so
  they never come from crates and can't be bought with merit. The local record (`sws3d.seasons`) moves to the archive
  when the month changes.
- **Arabic**: new table `src/i18n/ar/seasons.ts` (registered first in `src/i18n/phrases.ts`). The countdown reuses the
  existing weekly countdown pattern.

## Not done / caveats
- **Not deployed**: rules and the index need `npx firebase-tools deploy --only firestore:rules,firestore:indexes`.
  Until then, the live rules reject the new fields, so **don't ship this client before deploying the rules and index
  together**. Score submissions from old cached clients (no `duration`/`season`/`shadow`) will be rejected after the
  deploy until their PWA updates.
- The rules weren't run in the Firestore emulator (no Java or firebase-tools on this cloud machine). The rules syntax
  follows the existing file. `timestamp.year()` / `.month()` are standard rules functions.
- `duration` is reported by the client. The server limits points and kills against it, but can't confirm the real
  flight time. A server-stamped "sortie start" document would close that gap and is a possible follow-up.
- A score submitted in the last seconds of a month by a client with a skewed clock can land on the wrong season and be
  rejected. The player sees the usual "could not submit" message.
- No capture-rig screenshots or quality-gate comparison: this cloud machine has no GPU. The change is UI text and
  online logic, not graphics. No Blender or voice work was needed.
