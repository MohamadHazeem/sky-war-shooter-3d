# Phase 11 · 16 — Adaptive score 2.0: report

Branch `phase11/16-adaptive-music-2` · built in a Claude cloud session (no Blender, no voice tools, no GPU).

## What changed
- **Bomb-run bed** (`music_bombrun.m4a`): an original 8-bar D-minor loop at 100 bpm (driving low-string
  eighths, sustained chords, timpani, a horn line, a quiet field snare). From the IP to the strike photos
  (`inbound` → `strike`) the score crossfades to it on the bar line and goes back to the mood score at the rally.
  A climax mood (fight pressing hard) or a boss still wins over it.
- **New layer: field snare** (`snareBar` in `MusicLayers.ts`, procedural and tempo-locked like the taiko,
  brass and strings). Two patterns: `march` and a crescendo `roll`. During the bomb run its level follows
  the phase (it tightens towards the release, rolls through the countdown, pulls back while the bombs fall).
  In day missions it also plays in combat (0.3–0.6) and climax (0.65, rolling every 4th bar). Night,
  special-op and room scores do not get it. Reduced audio detail turns it off like the other layers.
- **Six stingers**, played over the bed:
  | cue | when | sound |
  |---|---|---|
  | rise | the mood climbs into the climax | snare roll + bowed cymbal; the accent lands on the climax track's first downbeat |
  | bombsAway | bomb release | bass drum, crash, low brass chord |
  | strike | strike confirmed (falling → strike) | timpani roll into a D-major brass chord + trumpet call |
  | loss | a friendly B-17 goes down (not the player's, not a ditching) | bell toll + muted-trumpet lament |
  | win | mission success | an original brass fanfare; the victory track fades in under its last bar |
  | bunk | crew room with an empty bunk | a quiet solo horn over the room music |
- **Smooth transitions**: each stinger has a grid rule (`now` / `beat` / `bar`) and lands on the playing
  track's beat or bar line. A pickup that has no room starts partway in. Pitched stingers are transposed to
  the current bar's root, by at most a tritone, but only over tracks marked `pitched`. The beds and layers
  duck under a stinger through a new `bedBus` (attack 60 ms, smooth release).
- **Rules in a pure module** (`src/audio/ScoreDirector.ts`): per-cue cooldowns, a 3 s minimum gap between
  cues (a higher-priority cue may break it), at most 3 loss cues per mission, bomb-run phase → cue / bed /
  snare level, mood → snare level, `transposeRate`. Fully unit-tested in Node.
- **Setting**: Settings → **Music cues** (On/Off). Off keeps the adaptive beds and layers and drops the
  stingers. Arabic added in the new table `src/i18n/ar/score.ts` ("المؤثرات الموسيقية"), registered in
  `phrases.ts`. It is the only new player-visible string.

## Offline synthesis (period instruments, no copyrighted material)
`tools/audio/score2_build.mjs` is a new tool built on the existing offline audio tooling (`tools/audio/lib/pcm.mjs`,
ffmpeg or afconvert, no npm installs). It synthesizes everything from scratch: additive brass section, muted
trumpet, five-desk strings, timpani, field snare, bass drum, suspended cymbal, church bell and a small
Schroeder hall. Output is deterministic. Levels are calibrated against the shipped combat bed (mono measured
as the stereo the listener hears): the bomb-run bed matches it (−22.9 LUFS in game), and each stinger's loudest
3 s sits at a set offset (win +2 LU, bombs away +1, rise/strike 0, loss −2, bunk −4). Credits are in
`public/audio/CREDITS.md`, and the metadata is written to `tools/audio/score2_meta.json` and merged into `manifest.json`.

**Size: 394 KB in total** (bed 166 KB, stingers 29–58 KB each), well under the 3 MB budget. **Lazy**: none of
the files are in `Sound.loadMusic`'s first batch, so they decode once the first mission starts. Preload
only prefetches them at the lowest priority.

`tools/audio/loudness.mjs` / `synth_entry.ts` now also report the bomb-run bed and the snare layer:
climax snare −34.4 LUFS (next to strings −34.0) and bomb-run countdown snare −31.8 against the bed's −22.9.
`LAYER_MIX.snare = 0.12` was set from these numbers.

## Hook lines (shared files)
- `src/game/BombRun.ts` `update()`: `sound.music.setBombRun(this.clock.phase)`, and `'idle'` on the early return.
- `src/game/WorldEvents.ts` `bomberLost`: `if (!b.ditched) sound.music.bomberLost(b.isPlayer)`.
- `src/game/Game.ts` settings apply: `sound.music.cues = s.musicCues !== false`.
- `src/ui/UI.ts`: `musicCues?` in `Settings`, one `<select>` row, and the boolean conversion.
- Win fanfare and crew-room bunk: inside `Music.stinger()` / `Music.setCrewRoom()` (no new call sites).
- `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`: one row in "Where to hook" for adding a cue.

## Performance
No per-frame allocations: `setBombRun` returns at once when the phase is unchanged, and the snare bars are
cached and prerendered in idle slices (combat, climax, bombrun) like the other layers. No shaders or
materials. A stinger costs one buffer source and one gain.

## Not done / limits
- **No listening test on this machine**, and no capture-rig comparison (audio-only goal; no GPU). The headless
  Playwright Chromium here has no AAC decoder: it also fails on the already-shipped `music_combat.m4a`, so
  in-browser decoding of the new `.m4a` files could not be checked here. The files were decoded with ffmpeg
  (no NaNs, loop seam error ≈ 0.2 % energy at the wrap). The game page loaded with no console errors and
  `swsSound.music.cues === true`.
- Mix levels are by measurement, not by ear. **The owner should listen** to a bomb-run mission (m1-type
  gunner mission with `bombsAway`) and one loss, and adjust the `DEFS` LU offsets in `score2_build.mjs` if
  the stingers feel too forward.
- No new radio lines, so no voice generation was needed.
