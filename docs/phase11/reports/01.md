# Phase 11 · 01 — Aircraft detail

The B-17 and five existing Luftwaffe heroes now carry a new baked detail pass, satin paint variation, clearer glazing and additional engine fittings. Close views improve over the live-main assets; the wide combat shots show a smaller difference. This is a material/detail improvement, not a claim of IL-2 parity or a certified 8/10.

## Changes

- Displaced high-poly sources: **1.36 million B-17 vertices**, **2.04–4.00 million per fighter**. Cycles selected-to-active tangent-normal/AO bakes use **1024 px / 48 samples**; colour stays **2048 px**. Curvature is retained in build intermediates and contributes to B-17 roughness. Shipped KTX2 mip chains and WebP recovery remain intact.
- Recessed seams, flush/raised rivets, slotted service fasteners, access covers, shallow skin dents/oil-canning and fabric rib tapes. B-17 chart calibration uses 45 mm rivet pitch, 16 mm edge offset and filtered 5 mm heads; fighter rows retain their measured surface-coordinate layouts. These are plausible layouts, not a factory rivet-plan reconstruction.
- Restrained colour/roughness residuals from Poly Haven's CC0 photographed **Painted Metal Shutter**; corrugation/shading is removed before sampling. [Source/licence/hashes](../../../tools/hero/references/CREDITS.md), also credited in `public/models/CREDITS.md`.
- Fighter atlas markings now use quality-95 WebP; B-17's separate lossless 1024×512 stencils remain. Tinted clearcoat glass and canopy boundary seals add separation and framing depth. B-17 radials gain steel cooling fins/pushrods; Fw 190 gains two cylinder rows; fighters gain open exhaust rims.
- Extended B-17 wastegate mouths now clear their turbo housings. Low glass now uses tinted transparency instead of opaque pale panels, without transmission/clearcoat. Small night glows use the existing prewarmed particle material, persistent spawn data, weather's blended night state, and individual engine-failure checks.

## Checks and budgets

- `npx tsc --noEmit` and **32 focused tests** pass (`heroB17`, `heroModels`, `aircraftDetail`). Tests cover artifact hashes, meaningful/unit-length normal maps, clearcoat glass, all named owners and controls, LOD/download budgets, every silhouette gate, and night/dead-engine glow behavior.
- B-17 **76,138 / 35,804 / 24,967 triangles**; all five fighter far levels remain capped at **10–13k / 2.6–4.2k**. [Artifact-bound bake/triangle report](../shots/01/bakes.json), [B-17 silhouette gate](../shots/01/silhouette.json).
- B-17 complete manifest is **10.22 MiB / 15 MiB**; fighters including ace livery and KTX2 are **6.69–7.40 MiB / 8 MiB**. No new runtime texture slots, lights or render passes. GPU detail maps keep the prior resolutions; Low retains its diffuse-only path.
- Browser JSON alongside each capture records no page errors and unchanged shader-program counts during settled frame samples. All five fighters were checked on compressed maps; aborted B-17 KTX2 requests recover to WebP. Frozen-render timing is included in manifests; short samples on this shared Mac are not an FPS guarantee.
- The full `npm run check` is run by [task-branch GitHub CI](https://github.com/MohamadHazeem/sky-war-shooter-3d/actions?query=branch%3Aphase11%2F01-aircraft-detail). No deployment.

## Files / hooks / limits

- `tools/hero/build.py` (detail/relief and engine fittings), `detail.py` (metric deformation/scan sampling), `compress.mjs`; rebuilt `public/models/hero/*`.
- `tools/hero/luftwaffe/build.py:322` (relief bake), `paint.py` (surface finish), `parts.py:388` (fittings), `finish.mjs` (glass/export); rebuilt five GLBs, ace maps and KTX2 sets. Editable high-poly sources remain in ignored build output; recipes and licensed inputs are committed.
- **Only production hook:** `src/world/Exhaust.ts:30` calls `TurboGlow.emitTurboGlow`. Existing `World.prewarm` covers the reused glow shader and new authored glass materials. No Game/UI hook or player-facing strings.
- QA: `detail-qa.mjs`, `detail-compare.mjs`, `detail-report.mjs`; existing B-17 QA launcher now defaults to the bundled headless shell and accepts task port/output overrides.

Remaining limits: inherited faceted canopy/airfoil geometry, approximate B-17 UV chart placement, and texture-density limits on individual rivets at formation range. The legacy P-51/other non-hero models are unchanged. Scanned paint is a surface reference, not measured wartime ANA/RLM colour data. The largest visible gains are close material response, glazing and engine fittings; wide combat views cannot establish fine-detail quality.
