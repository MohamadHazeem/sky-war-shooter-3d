# Phase 11 · 01 — Aircraft detail

The B-17 and five existing Luftwaffe heroes carry baked surface detail, satin paint variation, clearer glazing and engine fittings. This revision clears the glazing, replaces dark uniform seams with lighting-driven laps, and strengthens fastener and fighter surface response at the shipped texture resolutions.

## Second review fixes

- **Clear glass:** B-17 glazing alpha is **0.075** (including Low); fighter alpha is **0.085**. Neutral tint replaces the pale cyan, and B-17 transmission is removed. Clearcoat and the existing dynamic sky environment retain highlights without obscuring the cockpit. The capture manifests now record opacity, tint, transmission and the bound environment map.
- **Skin instead of a grid:** B-17 sheet ends are staggered, bay widths and seam depths vary, and asymmetric relief represents overlapping skin laps. Recess colour darkening drops from 48% to 9%; seam-edge colour chipping and painted rivet highlights are removed. Service covers have deeper recesses and slotted fasteners. Fighter joints receive variable widths and lap relief too, with recess darkening reduced from 55% to 16%.
- **Fasteners:** metric tangent slopes supplement the selected-to-active bake at the shipped 1024 px resolution, preserving detail below the high-poly vertex pitch. B-17 slopes are filtered after differentiation to retain rows close to the two-texel limit. An offline normal finish boosts only fine slope changes, excludes steep geometry borders and renormalizes texels before WebP/KTX2 export. Stronger fastener normals and lower local roughness create the glints; they do not use extra colour contrast. Fighter paint also has broader satin roughness variation.
- **Gunner evidence:** new waist/Fw 190 and tail/Bf 109 pairs put a banked, oblique target at **25 model metres**, through the production ADS eye at 34° FOV. This is world distance divided by the game's existing target scale. Both sides use the same new pose; the older rig fixtures remain for comparison. The gate measures and asserts target range as well as poses, camera, weather and preset.
- No production TypeScript changed in this revision. Engine fins/pushrods, open exhaust rims, turbo glow, named parts and LOD geometry remain intact.

The prior pose-lock repair is retained: cameras alone were insufficient because asset-dependent UUID random draws changed simulated bomber poses. All bomber, target and animated-part transforms are now explicitly locked before rendering; waist/tail eyes come from the production gunner controller. [Geometry/pose diagnosis](../shots/01/camera-diagnosis.json).

## Changes

- Displaced high-poly sources: **1.36 million B-17 vertices**, **2.04–4.00 million per fighter**. Cycles selected-to-active tangent-normal/AO bakes use **1024 px / 48 samples**; colour stays **2048 px**. Curvature is retained in build intermediates and contributes to B-17 roughness. Shipped KTX2 mip chains and WebP recovery remain intact.
- Recessed seams, flush/raised rivets, slotted service fasteners, access covers, shallow skin dents/oil-canning and fabric rib tapes. B-17 chart calibration uses 45 mm rivet pitch, 16 mm edge offset and filtered 5 mm heads; fighter rows retain their measured surface-coordinate layouts. These are plausible layouts, not a factory rivet-plan reconstruction.
- Restrained colour/roughness residuals from Poly Haven's CC0 photographed **Painted Metal Shutter**; corrugation/shading is removed before sampling. [Source/licence/hashes](../../../tools/hero/references/CREDITS.md), also credited in `public/models/CREDITS.md`.
- Fighter atlas markings now use quality-95 WebP; B-17's separate lossless 1024×512 stencils remain. Low-opacity clearcoat glass and canopy boundary seals add separation and framing depth. B-17 radials gain steel cooling fins/pushrods; Fw 190 gains two cylinder rows; fighters gain open exhaust rims.
- Extended B-17 wastegate mouths now clear their turbo housings. Low glass now uses tinted transparency instead of opaque pale panels, without transmission/clearcoat. Small night glows use the existing prewarmed particle material, persistent spawn data, weather's blended night state, and individual engine-failure checks.

- B-17 **76,138 / 35,804 / 24,967 triangles**; all five fighter far levels remain capped at **10–13k / 2.6–4.2k**. [Artifact-bound bake/triangle report](../shots/01/bakes.json), [B-17 silhouette gate](../shots/01/silhouette.json).
- B-17 complete manifest is **10.60 MiB / 15 MiB**; fighters including ace livery and KTX2 are **7.27–7.96 MiB / 8 MiB**. No new runtime texture slots, lights or render passes. GPU detail maps keep the prior resolutions; Low retains its diffuse-only path.
- Browser JSON alongside each capture records no page errors and unchanged shader-program counts during settled frame samples. All five fighters were checked on compressed maps; aborted B-17 KTX2 requests recover to WebP. Frozen-render timing is included in manifests; short samples on this shared Mac are not an FPS guarantee.
- The full `npm run check` is run by [task-branch GitHub CI](https://github.com/MohamadHazeem/sky-war-shooter-3d/actions?query=branch%3Aphase11%2F01-aircraft-detail). No deployment.
