# Phase 11 · 01 — final aircraft review

This round removes the rejected broad skin deformation and scopes all fighter work to clear glazing on the approved main assets. The B-17 remains the focus: recessed seams, visible offset rivet rows, matte paint, a furnished forward compartment and a bombsight behind the transparent nose dome. Radial cooling fins, pushrods, exhaust mouths and night turbo glow remain.

## Final review changes

- **B-17 skin:** restored the source-chart panel layout and rivet rows, with restrained diffuse recess/highlight contrast so they remain readable without a specular glint. Removed broad oil-canning and asymmetric lap displacement. Reduced tangent-slope strength from 7 to 1 and removed export sharpening; roughness returns to matte paint. Panel detail is an approximation, not factory rivet-plan data.
- **Nose glazing:** retained the low-opacity tinted perspex, preserved the original interior shell and added a floor, seat and bombsight. The seated bombardier and other B-17 crew come from the newly approved main artifact; the nose figure is fitted to the compartment so its boots stay inside the shell. The dome uses a separate 20% tint, retaining a visible glass surface.
- **Bf 109/Fw 190:** live main changed during this review. Its approved Sketchfab conversion, corrected wheel-well/root surfaces, matte paint, panel detail, engine fittings and original pilots supersede this branch's rejected skins. `retain-main.mjs` pins those assets to `683275db`; all four texture files are retained byte-for-byte. Only the canopy material changes to the clear tinted finish. This removes the smeared root and weakened-wing-grid regressions without overwriting the newer approved work.
- **Other three fighters:** reverted the new skin pipeline and retained their live geometry and textures too. The Me 262, Bf 110 and Ju 88 keep only clearer glazing; no reduced panel definition or speculative normal-map improvement is shipped. All five fighter texture sets are locked to the live receipt in tests.
- **25 m gunsight evidence:** both sides use the same revised oblique target poses, production ADS eye and 34° FOV. These supplemental inspections use matched clear-day lighting (sun elevation 35°, azimuth 315°); the original shadowed rig fixtures are retained. Target distance is asserted at 25 model metres. Native 1920-pixel captures preserve cockpit and panel detail.
