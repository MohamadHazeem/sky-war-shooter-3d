# Phase 11 · 35 — Gunnery range 2.0

Branch `phase11/35-gunnery-range`. The game now has a free practice range. You shoot at target drones and towed
sleeves, either in fixed drills or at a speed, crossing angle and range you choose. Each drill gives a score and
stars. The ghost of your best run flies alongside you, and a lead tutor overlay can be switched off.

## What the player gets
- **Entry:** Hangar → *Aircraft & Armory* → **Gunnery Range**. This tab had 3 entries, so the Phase 8 limit of 6 per
  tab still holds. *Training* (the Gunnery School) is unchanged.
- **Range screen** (`#gunRange`):
  - Back button, top leading.
  - Gun position chips: Left Waist, Right Waist, Top Turret, Ball Turret, Tail Gun.
  - Five drill cards, each showing best score, stars and "ghost ready":
    - **Steady shots:** slow drones on shallow angles.
    - **Crossing targets:** fast drones at 60–90° crossing.
    - **Towed sleeve:** an orange cloth sleeve on a cable behind a gunless P-47 tow ship.
    - **Long range:** drones at 800–900 m.
    - **Free practice:** pick the target (drone / sleeve), speed (slow 45 / medium 75 / fast 110 m/s relative),
      angle (30° / 60° / 90°) and range (300 / 500 / 700 / 900 m).
  - Two switches: **Lead tutor** and **Ghost of your best run**.
  - One primary button, **Start drill**.
  - All choices are remembered.
- **The flight:**
  - A harmless sky: no damage to the player, no flak, no AI fire.
  - No mid-round events, specials, bosses, bookends, kill cams, service record or mastery.
  - A small card at top right shows the drill, score, the ghost's score at this moment, target n/m, sleeve hits and
    time left. The HUD score line shows the range score too.
  - Each drill is a **fixed timeline**: every run flies the same straight passes at the same moments. That keeps runs
    comparable and makes the ghost line up.
- **Lead tutor** (canvas overlay, replaces the ordinary lead pip while on):
  - The lead point is computed with the game's own ballistics (`gunLead`: drag, drop, own-ship speed).
  - A dashed track runs from the target to the lead point, with tenth-of-a-second ticks.
  - A tolerance ring around the lead point is sized to the target and turns green when the gun is on it.
  - It shows lead angle, range and time of flight.
  - It coaches with words: *More lead / Less lead / Aim higher / Aim lower / On target — fire!*
  - The labels go on the side away from the target, so they never cover it.
- **Ghost:** a blue dashed ring marks where your best run was aiming at this moment of the drill. The card shows the
  ghost's running score next to yours.
- **Score:**
  - +10 for each hit on a drone, +15 for each hit on a sleeve.
  - +100 to +160 for a kill (more for an early kill in the pass).
  - An accuracy bonus of up to +200.
  - Stars at 30 / 60 / 90 % of the drill's par.
- **Result:** when a drill ends, the range screen returns with a result panel at the top: score, stars, *New best!* or
  your best, targets down, hits, accuracy, the bonus, and *You beat your ghost by N* / *Your ghost was N ahead*.
  *Start drill* flies the same drill again. A new best saves its ghost.
- **Arabic** for all new text, plus French, German and Spanish. RTL and the phone layout were checked.

## Files
New:
- `src/game/GunneryRangeRules.ts` (pure, no DOM): drills, free set-ups, pass geometry kept inside each gun's field of
  fire (`passHalf` searches against the station's real stops and keeps targets beyond 60% of their range), timeline,
  score, stars, ghost recorder and interpolation, `coachLead`, `leadDegrees`, records and preferences in
  `sws3d.range.v1` with sanitising.
- `src/game/GunneryRange.ts`: the runtime.
  - Drones are gunless Fw 190s in target-drone orange, flown on rails.
  - A sleeve is a cone mesh on a cable behind a tow ship.
  - Sleeve hits come through `Ballistics.ground`; the hook is borrowed for the flight and handed back.
  - Also: the HUD card, the ghost, the tutor overlay and the result.
- `src/ui/GunneryRangeScreen.ts`: the screen and its CSS.
- `src/i18n/{ar,fr,de,es}/gunneryRange.ts`
- `tests/gunneryRange.test.ts` (14 tests)
- `tools/gunnery_range_check.mjs` (browser check)
- `docs/phase11/shots/35/*`

Hook lines in shared files:
- `Missions.ts`: `range?: boolean`.
- `Onboarding.ts`: owns `range`. `onMissionStart` / `direct` / `update` / `onTitle` run the range instead of the
  school when `m.range`. `OnboardingHost.rangeDone`.
- `Game.ts`: `showGunneryRange(back, result)` and the `rangeDone` host callback.
- `SimpleMenu.ts`: the `range` route. `MenuModel.ts`: the Hangar entry.
- `GameHud.ts`: the objective line for range missions, `range.draw()` in `drawOverlay`, and no lead pip while the

- `Tutorial.ts`: `harmless()` exported.
- `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`: a new section.

Performance:
- No per-frame allocations in the update, draw or ballistics callback (shared scratch vectors and a constant sphere
  list).
- The sleeve and cable use plain `MeshStandardMaterial`, double-sided and default, whose programs `World.prewarm`
  already compiles. Nothing new to warm.

## Quality gate (vs LIVE main)
Main has no practice range. Its only gunnery practice is the fixed, one-time Gunnery School, with no scores, drills,
sleeves, ghosts or tutor. This goal adds a mode and UI. It changes no rendering of the existing game, so the capture
rig's shots are unchanged by design. The new screens are the shots above.

## Fixes found while checking
- A mid-round "Clear the tail" objective spawned real Bf 109s into the range. The range mission now sets
  `midRound: false, specials: false, boss: false`. Main's Gunnery School mission doesn't set `midRound: false`
  either; I didn't change it, because that is out of scope.
- The kill feed overlapped the card at top left, so the card moved to top right in both LTR and RTL.
- A true-size sleeve was about 2 px wide at 600 m. It is now drawn and hit larger than life (22 m), like the game's
  ×6 fighters, and painted orange.

## Not done / limits
- No new voice lines. The crew's normal kill callouts still play on a drone kill, e.g. "We got a Focke-Wulf!", because
  the drones fly the Fw 190 airframe.
- A drone is an orange Fw 190, not a purpose-built OQ-2 radio-plane model.
- Pars are set from the drill's passes. The stars may need tuning after real players try them; a near-perfect bot
  reaches three stars on drones.
- If you change seats mid-drill, the targets stay in the drilled gun's arc (by design).
- The ghost records aim and score only. It does not replay the tracers of your best run.
