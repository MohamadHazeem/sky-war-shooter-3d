# Phase 11 · 10 — Strategic air war: report

Branch `phase11/10-strategic-campaign`. This was built in a cloud session with no GPU, no Blender and no voice tools.

## What changed
A war-industry layer now sits under the existing campaign (`CampaignState.ts`) and its second tour.

- **Map of German industry** (`src/game/StrategicWar.ts`, `SITES`): 21 real 1943–45 sites in four sectors. Each has a relative weekly production value.
  - Aircraft factories: Regensburg, Leipzig-Erla, Oschersleben, Bremen, Kassel, Brunswick, Gotha.
  - Ball bearings: Schweinfurt, Erkner, Stuttgart.
  - Oil: Leuna, Pölitz, Gelsenkirchen, Ludwigshafen, Misburg.
  - Rail: Hamm, Münster, Osnabrück, Hannover, Berlin, Frankfurt.

  Each aircraft site records what it builds (Fw 190, Bf 109 or twin-engine destroyers). Regensburg also counts as part of the Me 262 programme.
- **Raids and real lag.** Each story operation has an explicit strike table, for example Black Thursday → Schweinfurt and Big Week → Leipzig, Regensburg and Erkner. Side raids and second-tour operations are matched by their target text.
  - Damage is queued behind a sector lag: aircraft 3 weeks (finished airframes in the pipeline), bearings 5 (stockpiles), oil 1, rail none.
  - Damage is repaired every week (rail 30 %/week, bearings 6 %/week).
  - Strike strength depends on success and on bombers lost, with a seeded ±15 % spread for the bomb pattern.
  - The clock runs week by week between operation dates, so pacing follows the historical calendar.
- **Effects on later missions.**
  - Fighter deliveries are aircraft output held back by bearings and rail. They are smoothed into a fighter-strength value that scales the enemy count (×0.80–1).
  - Fuel is oil output held back by rail. It feeds a slower pilot-training value that scales enemy skill (×0.90–1).
  - Output per product changes the **fighter types**. A spawn-time `ENEMY_MIX` hook swaps a type whose factories were hit for the single-engine type in better supply. Hitting the Focke-Wulf plants brings more 109s; hitting Brunswick and Gotha brings fewer Bf 110s, Ju 88s and Me 410s.
  - Hitting Regensburg sets back the jets (fewer Me 262s and Me 163s). Hitting oil also cuts the Me 163's rocket fuel.
  - Second-tour operations list their enemy types explicitly, so a type in short supply is dropped from that list. The original list is restored every time.
  - **When nothing has been hit, every factor is exactly 1**, so a fresh campaign plays as before (this has a test).
- **Weekly newsreel card** on the debrief, under the intelligence report. It has a film-frame style:
  - a kicker such as "The air war · 4 weeks from 8 March 1944" and a headline such as "Ball-bearing works ablaze at Erkner";
  - one line per site hit, giving the output lost and when it will be felt;
  - four sector bars with their change;
  - a forecast, for example "Fewer Bf 109s expected", "The jet programme is set back about 6 weeks" or "Repairs are ahead of the bombing".

  Replays show no newsreel and change nothing.
- **Campaign map.** A small "War industry" card (four bars, one note, the latest headline, one button) and an **Industry map** overlay.
  - The overlay is a chart of the sites. A dark wedge shows damage and a dashed ring shows damage still on its way to the lines. It also lists the worst-hit works and shows deliveries, training and jets.
  - It has one primary button (Back), and Esc/Backspace close only the overlay. This follows the Phase 8 rule of one simple card and one button.
  - The "Fighters ×" chips on the map and the briefing line now include the industry factor.
- **Deterministic and saved.**
  - The model is pure and does not change its input.
  - The state is saved in `localStorage` under `sws3d.strategic.v1`, bound to the campaign seed. A new tour starts a fresh industry; a second tour carries it over.
  - A save that is missing, belongs to another seed or lacks operations (an older profile that was caught up) is rebuilt from the campaign's operations log.
- **Arabic:** a new table, `src/i18n/ar/strategic.ts`, covers sectors, works, cities, the card, the map and every newsreel template. A test enumerates every line the model can write.

## Pacing (a simulated tour, every mission a success, 2 of 12 bombers lost)

| Mission (date) | Enemy count ×, from industry | Bf 109 supply |
|---|---|---|
| m1–m8 (Sept–Oct 1943) | 1.00 | 1.00 |
| m9 (Dec 1943), Schweinfurt's bearings shortage arrives | ~0.97 | — |
| m7 (8 March 1944) | ~0.96 | 0.90 |
| m12 (11 April 1944) | ~0.90 | ~0.63 |

On the m12 row, about a third of the 109s become Fw 190s, and the jets are at about 55 %. This is on top of the existing Luftwaffe-strength gauge. The effect is noticeable but not decisive, which matches the history of 1943–44 bombing.
