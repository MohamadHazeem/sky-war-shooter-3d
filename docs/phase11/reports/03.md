# Phase 11 · 03 — Cloud revision after rejection

Replaces the rejected revision's claims and captures. Branch: `phase11/03-film-clouds`. No deployment.

## Changes and hooks

- `Clouds.ts:105`, `noise.ts:142`: independent coverage/maturity/height fields, three-dimensional lobes, widening anvil shoulders and shear in a 3.9–8.5 km envelope. Curl-distorted erosion, adaptive surface steps and centred integration replace smooth columns and random march grain. GPU probes across 30 km find 18/121 occupied samples at 6 km and 15/121 at 7 km; none at 7.8 km in this fixture.
- `Clouds.ts:201,315,383`: direct sun marches shade nearby erosion and neighbouring towers, removing the height-interpolated shadow bands. Dark bases and cool fill separate bodies from illuminated heads. The silver rim is gated by whole-column transmittance cubed, keeping its energy out of opaque bodies.
- `Clouds.ts:420,550,624`: height-resolved cached shadows drive world-space gap shafts, including off-screen casters; heterogeneous cloud water and two spatial mist intervals rebuild penetration.
- `GlassFx.ts:272,596,761,913`: surface-attached condensation and microbeads accumulate quickly and evaporate gradually on pilot/gunner glass.
- Shared hooks: `Atmosphere.ts:25` adds the tower envelope and its sky function fixes horizon continuity; `Engine.ts:52,454` supplies the correct shadow bounds and changes High from 0.36 scale / 40 steps to **0.30 / 48 adaptive steps**. Ultra and Low retain their settings.
- `Clouds.ts:988` plus two `VisualRigPage.ts` calls settle the light probe for captures only; flight keeps asynchronous readback. The supplemental tool reads the active sun uniform, fixing stale Vite/HMR camera aim. No new targets, dependencies, assets, player strings or shader variants.
