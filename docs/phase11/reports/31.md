# Phase 11 · 31 — Historical raid mode

Branch `phase11/31-historical-raids`. The player can now fly four famous Eighth Air Force raids with their real
numbers. An end screen then compares the result with the historical losses and what the raid achieved.

## What the player gets
- **Entry:** Hangar → *Crew & Skills* → **Historical Raids** (the second entry, after The Tour). The main menu still has
  its three choices (Phase 8 rule).
- **Raid list** (`#histRaids`): four cards: Schweinfurt-Regensburg (17 Aug 1943), Black Thursday (14 Oct 1943),
  Big Week (20 Feb 1944) and Berlin (6 Mar 1944). Each card shows the date, bombers dispatched, bombers lost, the loss
  rate and your best result. The selected raid shows a short note in our own words, the escort of the day, airmen lost,
  Luftwaffe fighters lost and the target, with **one primary button, *Fly this raid***. Schweinfurt-Regensburg offers
  the **Regensburg force** (flies on to North Africa) or the **Schweinfurt force**.
- **The sortie:** each force flies as a variant of the gunner mission already built for that day: Deep Penetration
  dp1–dp4, and m5 for the Schweinfurt force. Each keeps its set piece, tactics, target and weather. It gets the real
  date, a new briefing with the day's numbers ("376 Fortresses… 24 of the Regensburg force did not reach Africa…")
  and a compression disclaimer. **Fighter pressure follows the real loss rate**: `pressure()` sets ×0.82–1.12 on the
  base fighters. The Schweinfurt force, a 1944 base flown in August 1943, gets 1943 fighter types and no escort.
  The briefing kicker reads *HISTORICAL RAID*.
- **End screen** (on the debrief):
  - A verdict: *Better than history*, *As in history* (within ±4 points of the real loss rate), *Worse than history*,
    or *The target was not hit*.
  - A table with three columns: *your group*, *history scaled to your 12 ships* and *history, the whole raid*. Rows:
    bombers lost, loss rate, fighters downed against Luftwaffe losses, and your ship's fate (came home / reached North
    Africa / ditched / shot down / interned).
  - *What really happened*, then the write-offs (Black Thursday) and the gunners' claims against actual German losses.
  - *Back to Historical Raids*.
  - Best results per raid are saved in `sws3d.raids.v1`.
- **No campaign side effects:** raid sorties don't change campaign unlocks, per-mission medals or bests, and post no
  online leaderboard. Merit and the service record still count, as for every mission.
- **Arabic:** every new text is in `src/i18n/ar/historicalRaids.ts`. RTL desktop and phone layouts were checked.
- **Sources:** `docs/phase11/historical-raids-sources.md` has the figures table, where sources disagree, how the game
  uses the numbers, and the citations.

## Quality gate (vs LIVE main)
Main has no historical raid mode: the Hangar has no entry and a debrief has no comparison. This goal is content and
UI only. It changes no rendering, materials or capture-rig fixtures, so the rig's shots are unchanged by design. The
new screens are the shots above.

## Not done / limits
- No new voice lines. The briefings are text only (the recorded briefing button appears only for campaign missions).
- Big Week is flown as its first day (Leipzig) but compared with the whole week's figures; the screen says so through
  "Sorties".
- Luftwaffe loss figures are approximate ("about") and sources disagree, especially for Berlin. The sources doc
  gives the ranges. The book figures are as commonly quoted; I checked only the web sources.
- A debrief *Retry* goes back to the briefing, and *Back* from there goes to the main menu (game default). The end
  screen's *Back to Historical Raids* is the intended way back.
