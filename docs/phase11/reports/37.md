# Phase 11 · 37 — Share cards and stats: report

Branch `phase11/37-share-cards`. Players can now share a picture after every mission and after the Tour.
The card looks like a wartime crew photo pinned to an olive board. There is also a personal stats page with three
charts, opened from the Career screen.

## What the player gets
- **Mission share card** (debrief → **🖼 Share card**, next to "Share screenshot" / "Share result"). It is a
  1080×1350 PNG (4:5, sized for social feeds) and contains:
  - a **staged photo** of the player's aircraft (not the live view): a three-quarter front view of the B-17
    (or the Mustang) with the formation in the background, printed as a toned black-and-white print with
    auto levels, lifted contrast, grain, a vignette and tape on the corners;
  - a typed caption: `B-17G "<your ship>" · <mission date>`, or `P-51D Mustang · <date>` for pilot missions;
  - a rubber stamp: MISSION COMPLETE, DID NOT RETURN or SURVIVAL;
  - four big numbers: kills, accuracy, bombers home (bombers lost for the pilot, rounds survived in Survival) and score;
  - one painted **Fw 190 profile** per kill (cowling, low canopy, the tall fin, a small Balkenkreuz), two rows of
    12, then "+N". The marks face the reading direction (mirrored in Arabic);
  - the mission's medal ribbons, drawn from the Career's ribbon stripes;
  - the player's B-17 nose art and name (gunner sorties).
- **Tour share card** (Tour finale "Going Home" → **🖼 Share tour card**). The same staged photo, then the four
  numbers (sorties, kills, Fortresses flown, crew home x/y) and a **crew roster under the numbers**: the ten men who
  came home in two columns, names at 26 px and positions at 18 px, readable on a 1080 feed image. The roster takes
  the victory marks' room (the kills are on the tile). It also shows the tour medals and the nose art.
- **Preview first**: the card opens in a dialog with **Share** (Web Share with the file, on phones) or **Save image**
  (download, plus the share text and link copied), and **Close** (Esc or a click outside also close it). The share
  text is translated.
- **Personal stats page** (Career → **Personal stats**), on the same paper sheet as the Career record:
  - the chart SVGs keep `direction: ltr` (their x positions are already mirrored by hand), so in Arabic the
    kills-by-type values sit outside the bar ends and the type names stay inside the chart;
  - tiles: sorties logged, survival rate ("home from x of y"), logbook accuracy (hits/rounds) and career kills;
  - **Accuracy over time**: a dot per sortie (last 40) plus a line for the round-weighted average of the last five.
    The axis is 0–30/40/50% in 10% steps, or 20% steps above that. The page also names the best sortie;
  - **Kills by type**: horizontal bars from the Career's lifetime kills by type, all six main types always listed;
  - **Survival rate**: the rolling share of the last ten sorties the player came home from, plus the rate as B-17
    gunner and as P-51 pilot;
  - every point and bar has a hover/focus tooltip (SVG `<title>`) and is keyboard-focusable. Empty states explain
    how to start each chart. Charts are drawn at their real pixel width, so the text stays readable on phones.
    Arabic mirrors the time axis (newest on the left).
  - colours were checked with the dataviz palette validator against the paper (#e8dfc6): blue #2f5f9a / amber
    #a8661c pass lightness, chroma, CVD separation and 3:1 contrast.

## Files
- `src/game/StatsLog.ts` (new, pure + guarded storage). It is the sortie logbook, stored in
  `sws3d.statslog.v1` (newest 200; CloudSave carries it like any `sws3d.*` key). It also computes the chart data:
  `accuracySeries`, `survivalSeries`, `summarize` (with a per-role mode), `killsByType`, `accuracyCeiling` and
  `percentTicks`.
- `src/game/ShareCardModel.ts` (new, pure): `missionCard`, `tourCard`, `medalList` (de-duplicated, counted, ribbons
  first, 5 max + "more"), `cardText` (share text key + vars) and `cardFileName`.
- `src/game/ShareStage.ts` (new): the staged photo. `stageSubject` picks the player's aircraft (or the nearest
  bomber still flying); `bestSide` (pure) scores the four mirrored front/rear, left/right poses by how many other
  bombers fall inside the print's frame and prefers the front and sun-side views; `captureStaged` moves the camera,
  shows the B-17 parts the gunner view hides, hides the camera's children (gun viewmodels), renders 8 warm-up frames
  (the shadow cascades and exposure settle over a few frames; a single frame came out dark), copies the frame and
  then restores the camera parent/slot/pose/fov and the post settings exactly as PhotoMode does.
- `src/ui/ShareCard.ts` (new): canvas drawing, preview dialog, `mountMissionShareCard`, `mountTourShareCard`.
  In Arabic it mirrors the layout, uses `direction: rtl`, and wraps caption values in bidi isolates so Latin ship
  names and dates stay whole.
- `src/ui/StatsScreen.ts` (new) and `src/ui/shareCard.css` (new).
- `src/i18n/{ar,fr,de,es}/shareCards.ts` (new): every new string.
- `tools/share_cards_shots.mjs` (new): the QA shots. It seeds a logbook, flies one autoplay sortie to a real debrief,
  then opens the mission card, the stats page and the Tour finale card, in EN and AR, plus phone shots. It hides the
  achievement toasts the seeded career triggers. It uses the Playwright headless shell only.
- `tools/share_card_iter.mjs` (new): renders the mission card from the menu formation with `STAGE` overrides, for
  tuning the staged camera in seconds instead of flying a sortie.
- `tests/shareCards.test.ts` (new, 11 tests).

## Hook lines in shared files
- `src/game/ServiceRecord.ts` `onDebrief`: one `recordSortie(...)` line after the lifetime stats, so the logbook
  covers exactly the sorties the Career counts (not the tutorial). `survived` mirrors the Career's `deaths` rule.
- `src/game/MissionFlow.ts` `showDebrief`: `mountMissionShareCard(host, g.engine, m, d)` after `mountShare`.
- `src/ui/TourScreen.ts` `finaleView`: `mountTourShareCard(root.querySelector('.tour-actions'), s)`.
- `src/ui/CareerScreen.ts`: a "Personal stats" button.
- `src/ui/serviceCommon.ts`: `'svc-stats'` added to the leading-edge Back list.
- `src/game/Share.ts`: `installShare` stores the engine and the world (`shareEngine`, `shareWorld`) for the staged photo.
- `src/ui/cleanHud.css`: in Arabic menus the achievement toast sits on the left, so it no longer covers the
  leading-edge (right) Back button. Missions are unchanged.
- `src/game/Customize.ts`: `noseArtId()` pulled out of `dressPlayerBomber` (same logic, reused by the cards).

## Review round 2 (rejection fixes)
- **Photo**: staged camera (`ShareStage.ts`) instead of the live view; the B-17 sits large in the print with the
  formation behind it. Auto levels plus a median-based gamma (clamped 0.8–1.3), an S-curve and a shadow lift
  replace the old fixed contrast. The old washed-out cream sheet is gone (`en-mission-card.jpg`, `en-tour-card.jpg`).
- **Tour card**: same staged photo; the crew names moved out of the print foot into a two-column roster under the
  numbers (26 px names, 18 px positions; it was 15 px).
- **AR stats**: the chart SVGs are `direction: ltr`, so the kills-by-type values (76, 43, 19, 9) sit left of the
  mirrored bar ends and the type names stay inside the chart (`ar-stats.jpg`).
- **Toast over رجوع**: in Arabic menus the toast now sits on the left (cleanHud.css), and the shot script hides
  toasts before the shots.
- **Victory marks**: Fw 190 A side profiles (cowling ring, framed low canopy, tall rounded fin, tailplane, prop
  disc, Balkenkreuz), 66 px long, 12 per row.

## Not done / notes
- On the Low preset (used by the shot script), the low-res cloud upsample leaves faint light boxes around very small,
  distant bombers in the staged photo (bottom-left of `en-mission-card.jpg`). The subject B-17 is clean.
- If every bomber is down and the player is not in a fighter, the card falls back to the live view.
- The logbook starts empty for existing players. Kills by type uses the lifetime Career totals, so that chart has
  data at once; accuracy and survival fill in from the next sortie.
- Co-op guests' debriefs go through `coop.showDebrief()` and get no card.
