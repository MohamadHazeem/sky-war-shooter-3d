# Phase 11 · 17 — Spatial audio 2.0: report

Branch `phase11/17-spatial-audio-2` (cloud session: no Blender, voice tools or GPU, no headphones to listen with).

## What changed
The new logic is in pure, unit-tested helpers (`src/audio/Spatial2.ts`) and three small Web Audio pieces
(`src/audio/SpatialNodes.ts`). `Sound.ts` wires them in.

| Feature | How |
|---|---|
| **Doppler on flybys** | Doppler now uses the velocity of the source *relative to the listener*. Before, it used only the fighter's world velocity, so the gunner riding at ~75–105 m/s heard a pitch shift from fighters holding station with the bomber. `ListenerMotion` estimates the camera velocity from frame positions and ignores jumps from the floating origin, camera cuts and the kill cam. `doppler2(vs, vl)` covers both ends moving and equals the old `doppler(vs)` when the listener is still. It is applied to the fighter engine voices and the field emitters. |
| **HRTF for fighters passing by** | Before, every voice within 800 m got HRTF, and only at Normal. Now each fighter gets a pass-by score from `passBy()` (time and miss distance at closest approach) plus `hrtfScore()`. `pickHrtf()` gives the HRTF panners to the best k, with hysteresis so a panner does not flip between two similar fighters. A fighter about to pass close outranks a nearer one flying away. k is 4 at Normal and 1 at Reduced (Reduced had none before). |
| **Fuselage occlusion by station** | `occlusion(station, dir)` works in the B-17 model frame. Each station has its own openings: its open waist window (the far window lets a little through), the top turret dome, the ball under the belly and the tail glazing. Every other direction is heard through the skin at about -5 dB with the highs cut hard (lp ×0.3). This replaces the old flat "interior" factor for fighter voices and for outside one-shots (more than 15 m away, so the other waist gun stays as it was). It applies only while the camera is at the station (the same "near the cabin" test as the window roar). |
| **Interior reverb per compartment** | Waist tube, flight deck (top turret), ball turret and tail cone each have their own send level, pre-delay and damping (`ROOMS`). At Normal each also gets its own impulse length and decay, built once and cached. `RoomChain` (delay → damping low-pass) sits between `revInSend` and the interior convolver. Reduced keeps the waist impulse and changes parameters only. |
| **Distance-based flak rumble** | Each `flakBurst` adds energy to `FlakRumble`, weighted by distance (none past 8 km). The energy decays with τ = 3.5 s, and the energy-weighted distance sets the low-pass: farther barrages sound darker. `RumbleBed` is a brown-noise loop on the ambience bus, a little darker inside the fuselage. |
| **Engine harmonics per RPM** | `engineHarmonics()` gives the propeller blade-pass frequency, its 2nd harmonic and the firing order. Wright R-1820 Cyclone: 9 cylinders, gear 0.5625, 3 blades, so ~65 Hz and ~172 Hz at 2300 rpm. Packard Merlin: 12 cylinders, gear 0.479, 4 blades. `HarmonicBank` places 3 oscillators per engine under the recorded loops. The B-17 has 4 banks, panned per engine with the same off-speed trims as the drone, so they beat. Dead engines fall silent, and the remaining engines run 70 rpm harder per engine lost (`bomberRpm`). The P-51 bank follows throttle and the damage hunt. Normal only. |
| **Audio detail** | `SPATIAL_DETAIL`: Reduced keeps 1 HRTF fighter, has no harmonics and keeps one impulse. Occlusion, Doppler and the rumble are parameter changes on nodes that exist anyway, so both levels keep them. |
| **UI** | The Settings "Audio detail" row has a hover hint explaining Normal vs Reduced. The Arabic is in the new table `src/i18n/ar/spatialAudio.ts`, registered first in `src/i18n/phrases.ts`. |

## Hook lines in shared files
- `src/game/Game.ts`: one line before `sound.setCabin(...)`, `sound.setStation(... w.playerStationId() ...)`.
- `src/audio/Sound.ts`:
  - imports and fields
  - `build()`: the room chain and the rumble bed
  - `teardown()`
  - `setMode` / `setDetail`: `applyRoom`, `applyHarmonics`
  - new block: `setStation`, `compartment`, `applyRoom`, `applyHarmonics`, `occlude`, `updateSpatial`
  - `setCabin`: the bomber orientation for occlusion
  - `setListener`: listener motion
  - `updateFighterVoices`: relative Doppler, occlusion, pass-by HRTF pick
  - `shot()`: occlusion of outside one-shots
  - `flakBurst`: rumble energy
  - `updateWind`: calls `updateSpatial`
  - `setPlayerEngine`: Merlin harmonics
  - `Emitter`: relative Doppler
- `src/ui/UI.ts`: the `title` hint on the Audio detail row.
- `src/i18n/phrases.ts`: registers the table.

There are no per-frame allocations in the new code: scratch arrays and objects are reused, and `pickHrtf` and `occlusion` write into `out` parameters. There are no shaders.

## Tests
- `tests/spatial2.test.ts` (19 tests) covers:
  - Doppler: matches the old one-ended version for a still listener, a station-keeping wingman has no shift, and the result stays bounded
  - `ListenerMotion`: convergence, and rejection of teleports and NaN samples
  - closest approach, HRTF ranking and hysteresis, the Reduced caps
  - occlusion per station: open vs skin, smoothness, degenerate input
  - compartment ordering
  - flak rumble: near vs far, decay, junk input
  - Cyclone and Merlin order frequencies at known rpm
  - Arabic coverage of the hint
- `npx tsc --noEmit`: clean.
- Local `npm run check`: tsc, vite build and vitest all pass (94 files, 939 tests).
- Runtime check in Playwright's bundled headless Chromium against a dev server, running `sws.autoplay(CAMPAIGN[0], 400)` and then reading the `sound` module:
  - mode `bomber`, station `waistL`, occlusion on, room `waist`
  - 4 harmonic engines
  - listener velocity ≈ (0, 7, 105) m/s
  - 2 of 5 engine voices on HRTF (the passing ones)
  - switching station to ball / tail / top gives impulses of 0.28 / 0.40 / 0.55 s
  - six flak bursts at ~600 m raise the rumble level
  - Reduced drops the harmonics, goes back to the 0.75 s waist impulse and sets k = 1; Normal restores them
  - no console errors, no non-finite samples (`stats().nonFinite = 0`)

## Not done / caveats
- I could not listen to the result: there is no audio output or headphones in the cloud container. Levels are conservative:
  - harmonics are 0.05 (bomber) and 0.045 (P-51) on the ambience bus, behind a 700 / 1100 Hz low-pass
  - the rumble peaks at 0.3

  The owner should do a listening pass, ideally on headphones, in the waist, ball and tail stations during a flak barrage and a head-on pass.
- I did not use the capture rig or take screenshots: this goal changes nothing visual.
- No new radio lines, so no voice generation. Blender is not needed.
- The engine voices of enemy fighters do not get RPM harmonics. That would cost oscillators per voice, and their loops already follow throttle.
