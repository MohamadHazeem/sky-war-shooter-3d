# Phase 11 goal 25 — The ground war below

Branch: `phase11/25-ground-war`. Dev server: **5325**. Second review fixes: 15 October 2026.

## Rejection fixes

1. **Bright city stripe:** replaced the pale ballast with a narrow, terrain-relative dark service line that fades with pixel footprint. Moved the line outside the runway and taxiways. The release, walking-strikes and burning-city cameras are unchanged.
2. **Battery stickers:** removed circular ochre rings. Batteries now use subdued soil aprons and open, squared revetments with low-contrast interiors. Only the existing muzzle flashes emit light; distant earthworks still fade out.
3. **Unreadable takeoff:** increased the tapered fighter silhouette to an explicit 18× recognition scale, retaining the tailplane, contrasting nose and sun-projected shadow. Added a dissipating prop-wash/dust wake along the accelerating runway roll and initial climb. Three staggered sortie slots remain deterministic.
4. **Train hidden at runway:** moved freight to a separate service line beyond the dispersals, increased wagon separation/width, and replaced the low trailing smear with overlapping locomotive exhaust puffs that expand away from the railway. Exhaust remains attached to the moving engine.
5. **Low water artifacts:** restored Low's original continuous procedural city river and coast rendering. The rejected implementation switched between mapped and procedural rivers at mapped-width boundaries, creating breaks; its mapped river SDF also repainted thin spurs across Low terrain. Mapped water now supplies a separate effects-only mask, without changing Low's water drawing. A zero-width target river no longer draws an antialiased hairline on harbour layouts.
6. **Accepted smoke:** city soot, wind bending, plume growth and city fire rendering are unchanged.
