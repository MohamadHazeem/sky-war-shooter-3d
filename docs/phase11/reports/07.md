# Phase 11 · 07 — The Tour (25 missions)

Branch `phase11/07-the-tour`. A full 25-mission tour of duty for the B-17 gunner: one persistent
Fortress and crew from Emden (27 September 1943) to Friedrichshafen (24 April 1944).

## What the player gets

- **Entry:** Hangar → *Crew & Skills* → **The Tour** (first, primary entry), and a *The Tour · 25 missions*
  button in the campaign map/summary footer. The main menu keeps its three choices (Phase 8 rule).
- **Tour board** (`#tour`): one primary *Fly this mission* button; *Stand down a day*; 25-bomb tally;
  **field orders** for the date: one to three historical targets (title, target, danger 1–5 with label,
  gun station, escort for the date: none / to the border / Mustangs all the way); latest report; tour log.
  **The ship** card: top-view plan coloured by the carried damage per zone, the repair patches dotted on,
  serial, missions, patch count, condition and rough engines, ships lost. **The crew** card: morale and
  fatigue gauges, crew gunnery % and the ten men (portraits, rank, post, missions, wounded/new status, ribbons).
- **Field orders:** 25 stages in date order (Emden, Bremen, Marienburg, Black Thursday, Wilhelmshaven,
  Kiel with the first Mustangs, Ludwigshafen, Oschersleben, Big Week, Berlin...; 42 targets in all).
  Each target flies as a variant of an existing gunner mission (Missions.ts m1/m2/m3/m5/m8/m11/m12 and
  Deep Penetration dp1/dp3/dp4: their weather, set pieces, tactics, target ground and flak), re-dated,
  re-targeted, given its own station and winter/undercast weather, and re-tuned: fighters/skill/rockets/flak
  by danger plus a late-tour ramp (early-tour max × skill vs late: +40 % and more, tested). Escort arrival
  follows the date. Mission 25, **Going Home**, is a four-round maximum effort with a battle group in the
  last round.
- **The ship:** damage carried between missions — every hit zone's end-of-sortie strength is booked,
  the ground crew fixes 70 % overnight, the rest starts the next mission (never below a flyable floor;
  badly hit engines stay "rough"). New damage adds **repair patches**: riveted aluminium / zinc-chromate
  sheets that appear **on the Fortress's skin** (wings, fuselage top and sides, belly, tailplane — found
  by ray-casting the ship's own model once per ship, ~70 ms behind the mission fade) and **inside the
  waist** around the gun windows; seeded per airframe, so they accumulate in the same places all tour.
  Shot down or ditched: a replacement Fortress (*Second Chance*, *Lady Luck*...). The first ship is the
  player's own (*Sky Queen* / their custom name).
- **The crew:** the CrewRoster's ten men at their trade. Wounds (Purple Heart; light = one mission out,
  serious = hospital 2–3 missions, 4 = sent home), death of wounds (20 % of serious), men lost with a
  shot-down ship (those the bail-out manifest did not get out: killed or missing). Replacements: first the
  roster's rookie at that post (his name matches the recorded radio), then fresh names. In flight the
  roster puts rookies in for wounded/lost men (`CrewRoster.tourOut`), the radio names the tour's men.
  Promotions (Sgt → S/Sgt at 6 → T/Sgt at 16; Lt → Capt at 12; the skipper → Maj at 20), Air Medal at 5,
  Oak Leaf Clusters at 10/15/20, DFC at 25 — for every man and for the player.
- **Morale and fatigue:** sorties, losses, wounds, promotions and events move them; the days until the
  next historical date rest the crew (so October 8–10 and Big Week wear them down); a stand-down (once per
  gap) helps. They scale the AI gunners (`World.aiGunnerFactor` × `crewFactor`, ~0.6–1.1, with crew experience).
- **Only a successful sortie is credited**; a failed one is flown again; the debrief's *Retry* is replaced by
  *Back to the tour*. Maximum effort (event) = danger +1 and +50 % merit.
- **Mid-tour events** (one in two gaps, the flak house fixed at mission 12): mail call, a new engine,
  48-hour pass (London or help the ground crew), empty bunks, maximum effort, Walsh's lucky charm, fogbound,
  hangar dance, practice flight for green replacements, the general's visit, letters home, armorers' work —
  each a card with one or two choices and real effects (morale, fatigue, repairs, engine change, healing, drill).
- **Finale:** *Going Home* screen — the interphone, a "Lucky Bastard Club" certificate, sorties, kills,
  ships, patches, medals, and the crew's fates (going home with you / sent home wounded / remembered);
  *Begin a new tour*.
- **Voices (Chatterbox):** 32 new clips (`tools/voice/lines.json`, group *Phase 11 the tour*): the pilot
  counting each mission (*"Pilot to crew: that's number seven in the books."*), halfway, one more, going
  home, not credited, ship lost, mail call, flak house, maximum effort, fog. Played on the debrief report and
  event cards with the subtitle shown.
- **Arabic:** every text (orders, briefings, reports, events, labels, names, ranks, interphone lines) in
  `src/i18n/ar/tour.ts`; RTL and phone layouts checked.

## Not done / limits

- Tour targets outside the existing far-terrain set (Frankfurt, Paris, Augsburg...) fly over the generic
  ground; they are on the briefing chart only (`EuropeChart.EXTRA`).
- The 32 voice lines cover counts and events; crew-specific lines (a named man wounded, a promotion) are
  shown as text only.
- Gunner position bonuses still come from the global CrewRoster (levels earned anywhere); the tour adds
  its own experience/morale/fatigue factor on top.
