## What changed

### The crewman (Blender, `tools/interior/build_crew.py` → `public/models/crew.glb`, 518 KB)
The man is rebuilt from scratch in the same script. Everything is still original and generated.
- **Body.** It is lofted from superellipse sections along the rig instead of a skin-modifier blob, with human proportions. The head is 1/7.9 of his height and shoulders are 0.5 m across in the jacket.
  - B-3 jacket: a shawl sheepskin collar standing round the neck, knit cuffs and a fleece hem roll.
  - Bulky A-3 trousers and A-6 boots, with fleece tops, a zip and rubber soles.
  - Sewn seams, slash-pocket welts and a zipper.
  - Sculpted folds at the elbows, knees, waist and boot tops.
- **Face.** The head is a sculpted ellipsoid: brow ridge, eye sockets, nose bridge, cheekbones and a tapered jaw. It is vertex-painted: cold-reddened cheeks and nose, darker sockets, stubble.
  - **Eyes**: eyeballs with sclera, limbal ring, iris and pupil, a glossy `crewEye` material, and eyebrows.
  - **Eyelids** on their own `lids` bone, which blink. The lids were first built facing inward and were culled; that is fixed and checked in a closed / rest / wide test.
- **Headgear.**
  - A-11 leather helmet, open over the face, with stitched crown seams, a padded edge, earphone cups and a chin strap.
  - B-8 goggles pushed up on the forehead, with lenses, frames, bridge and strap.
  - **A-14 oxygen mask**: a rubber cup with rolled rim, valve boss and connector. A **corrugated hose** runs to the brass clip on the harness, and the straps lie over the cheeks to snaps on the helmet.
  - The **M4 steel helmet** has curved hinged ear-flaps. It is its own mesh, worn by the men who wear the vest.
- **Gloves.** Palm, four modelled fingers curled round the grip, thumb, flared gauntlet and strap. The index fingers have their own bones (`index.L/R`) so a man can point. The player's own first-person arm (`fpArm`) uses the same glove.
- **Kit.** B-4 Mae West with baffles, an inflation tube and collar. The parachute harness has brass hardware. The M1 flak vest is its own mesh, with quilted plates, apron, curved shoulder straps and the red release tab.
- **Materials by surface.**
  - Six materials: `crewLeather`, `crewFleece` (sheepskin), `crewCanvas`, `crewSkin`, `crewHard` (steel, rubber, brass, lenses) and `crewEye`.
  - Vertex colour RGB is the painted key that the game recolours per man.
  - Vertex colour alpha is a **shade**: ambient occlusion baked in Cycles (12 cm), times low-frequency wear.
- **Clips.** The clips are kept (`idle fire hit down help sit kneel`). The wounded `down` loop is new: laboured breathing, the right hand pressed on the wound, the drawn-up knee rocking, the head rolling, and the free hand lifting and falling.
- **Skinning.** Torso skin weights only take the upper arm round the shoulder joint, so the flanks no longer drag when he raises an arm.
- **Budget.** The body is about 59 k triangles (built for 0.5–3 m close-ups). The LOD is 7 k with no eyes. The glb is 518 KB, up from 271 KB, and the crew and interior files together are 0.9 MB, well inside the budget.

### Game side
- `src/render/CrewFabric.ts`: original tileable 256² normal and roughness microstructure per surface:
  - pebbled hide grain with creases
  - curly sheepskin tufts
  - plain-weave canvas
  - skin pores
  - the old cloth weave

  Relief is normalised per texel size. The legacy boolean API still works.
- `src/game/InteriorAssets.ts`: `CREW_SURFACE` sets roughness, environment response, normal strength and bounce per material. `splitCrewShade` keeps the key and the shade per geometry.
- `src/game/CrewActor.ts`. `recolour` keeps the shade and tints the whole skin surface by the man's look, so his own variation survives. The steel helmet follows the vest. New procedural layers, with no per-frame allocation:
  - **Recoil absorption**: `kick()` per round feeds a damped spring into the shoulders, the head and a rock back on the heels.
  - **Flinch on near misses**: `flinch()` makes him duck, hunch, turn the chest and head from the blast and screw his eyes shut.
  - **Pointing at threats**: `point()`. The left hand leaves the grip, the arm straightens toward the target, the index finger uncurls and the head follows.
  - **Aim tracking and looking**: `look()`. The chest twists about his vertical before the arm IK, so the hands stay on the grips. The face is clamped like a neck.
  - **Eyes**: random blinks, shut in a flinch, heavy-lidded when wounded.
- `src/game/LivingInterior.ts`. The other waist gunner now:
  - keeps his eyes on his target
  - calls a fighter closing on the player's side: with no target of his own he points at it (2.4 s, every 10 s at most), which helps the player find it
  - glances across at the player now and then
  - ducks with everyone else when a fighter flashes past within 75 m

  Each round he fires kicks him. A wounded man has spasms of pain and turns his head to the man helping him.
- `src/game/CrewFigure.ts`: the B-3 look is a darker, more realistic brown.

### Hooks in shared files

## Reference: the Sketchfab B-17 interior scan
I studied `assets-src/sketchfab/boeing_b-17_flying_fortress_-_matterport.glb` (tosolini, CC BY 4.0) as a reference only. Its 13 photogrammetry atlases are thousands of fragmented, museum-lit patches. They confirm the zinc-chromate yellow-green and olive interior palette that the kit already uses, but they contain no crew and cannot serve as tileable crew or interior textures. **Nothing from the scan ships in this branch**, so no credit line is added beyond the existing one in `assets-src/sketchfab/CREDITS.md`.

## Self-score, pillar 6
Before **5/10**, after **7/10**. The crew now look like people at 0.5–3 m: faces with eyes behind masks, period-correct helmets, oxygen gear and clothing, real materials, and they react (recoil, flinches, pointing, wounds, blinking).

What keeps it from 8 and up:
- The faces are stylised, sculpted from functions; there are no scanned likenesses.
- There is no walking locomotion: the helper still appears out of view.
- The first-person forearm is unchanged in shape.
- At very close range the vertex-coloured skin is smooth.
