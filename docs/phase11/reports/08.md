# Phase 11 · 08 — Gunner mastery: report

Long-term goals for B-17 gunners, on top of the career, achievements, dailies, weekly challenge and skill tree.
Hangar → Crew & Skills → **Gunner Mastery** opens one calm sheet with five tabs:
Stations · Challenges · Ace Hunt · Locker · Stats. A mastery report is added to every gunner debrief.

## What a player gets

| Feature | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Mastery ranks per gun station** | Ten ranks per station (Trainee → Legend, 100 … 7,500 XP). Mastery XP: 10 a kill, +10 deflection, +25 ace, +10 at 600 m or more, +5 heavy, +20 per untouched round, +15 for a win (the station flown longest), +20 for 25% accuracy over 100+ rounds at that station. Rewards: merit on ranks 2/4/6/8, the station's **crew patch** at 3, its **gun sight** at 5, its **bomber name** at 7, the **Master Gunner nose art** at 9 and the **Legend patch** at 10. Anything already owned pays 500 merit instead. |
| **Challenge sets** (3 tiers each) | Deflection Shooting (crossing angle ≥ 60°, relative speed ≥ 25 m/s: 15/75/250), Ace Hunter (aces: Ace Hunt aces, named Experten, special-event leaders, AI personas flagged ace: 5/25/100), Untouched (a round of ≥ 45 s at the guns with no hit on your B-17: 3/15/50), Long Shot (≥ 600 m: 10/50/150), Every Gun (the station with the fewest kills: 5/25/100). Each tier pays a sight, patch, nose art, name or merit. |
| **Weekly Ace Hunt + leaderboard** | Every gunner sortie of the ISO week scores points: 10 a kill, +20 for this week's quarry (seeded per week, the same for everyone: Fw 190s / Bf 109s / heavy fighters / jets), +40 ace, +10 deflection, +10 long, +25 untouched round. Goal 1,500 points → +1,000 merit each week and, once, the Hunt Master patch. Board **`acehunt-gunner-<yyyyww>`** with the usual global / squadron scopes and a Submit button (also listed under Records → Leaderboards). Resets Monday 00:00 UTC. |
| **Unlockables (Locker)** | 7 gun-sight styles (Standard Ring, Mk IX Reflector, Fine Crosshair, Gyro K-14, Ace's Chevron, Tracer Dot, Deflection Rings) drawn on the waist/top HUD reticle and the ball turret's reflector glass; 13 embroidered crew patches (inline SVG, no text, so the same in both languages); 2 new nose arts (Master Gunner: crossed fifties over gilded wings; Ace Hunter: the ace of spades in a gun-sight ring); 9 exclusive bomber names. Equip from the Locker; the nose art and names also show in Customize (marked “Gunner Mastery reward”, never in crates or for merit; exclusive names are hidden there until earned). |
| **Stats page** | Per station and in total: kills, rounds fired, hits, accuracy, time at the gun, deflection / ace / 600 m+ / heavy kills, untouched rounds, best sortie, and your most accurate gun (200+ rounds). Career kills by station from before this goal carry over once (as kill XP); rounds/hits are counted from now. |

## Review round 1 fixes

1. **Back button over the header:** the sheet's sticky Back button sat under a `.btn.ghost` transparent background, so the header medallion, title and tabs showed through it once the sheet scrolled. It now rides an opaque full-width top bar (`.ms-topbar`, z-index 10, pinned to the sheet's top edge) and the header has its own `z-index: 0` stacking. `tools/mastery_ui_check.mjs` scrolls Stations, Locker and Stats (desktop and Arabic phone) and asserts the Back button is the topmost element at its centre and its bar is opaque. Shot: `en-locker-scrolled`.
2. **Number formatting:** every count, XP, merit and point figure goes through the locale formatter (`+1,000 merit`, `1,500`); once the weekly goal is reached the header, Ace Hunt tab and debrief show `Goal reached ✓ 4,763` instead of `points/goal` (asserted in the check).
3. **Debrief section:** inner padding and a framed block; the XP figure never wraps; the hunt line and the quarry are two lines and aircraft designations keep a non-breaking space (`Me 410` never splits). Shot: `debrief-mastery-section`.
4. **Arabic phone stats:** both tables use fixed, centred columns, so each header sits over its own numbers in LTR and RTL (asserted: header and first-row cell centres within 2 px, tables fit the sheet); `600 m+` is localised (`600 م فأكثر`). Shots: `ar-phone-stats`, `ar-phone-stats-kinds`.
