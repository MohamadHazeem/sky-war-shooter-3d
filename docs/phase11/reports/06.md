## Round-2 review points and what was done

1. **Sunset seam / lilac band** — the anti-solar shift is now partial (50 %) and continuous across the horizon; at twilight the
   scattered-light term continues a little below the horizon and fades (`Atmosphere.ts`), so sky and hazed cloud sea meet without a seam.
2. **Cold wash / no golden warmth** — dusk key is brighter and more golden (`Weather.ts`: sun 2.7, colour 1/0.55/0.27, ambient
   restored to 0.42, exposure 0.93), the dusk print is warmer in the highlights and cooler in the shadows (`FilmLUT.ts`), contrails
   and the stream's wake sheet take +90 % low-sun light, so the sheet is gold, consistent with the clouds.
3. **Horizontal streaks at the horizon stream** — the stream trails (`BomberStream.ts`) and distant trails (`LivingSky.ts`) now take
   the same twilight sky light as the sky behind them.
4. **Flak lighting clouds, coned belly, tracers** — `Effects.nightFlak` adds two cloud lights per burst (flash + ember);
   `lightConed()` holds a cold light under each coned raider (RaidDefence, AADefence, rig); `World.nightTracerLight()` lights the
   airframe with the tracer passing nearest the eye. All use existing light pools: no new programs.
5. **Rebased on `origin/main`** (goal 00 auto-discovered Arabic tables; this goal adds no player-visible strings).
6. **Night readability** (round 3) — full-moon key/fill and a moon rim (`SunRim.ts`, detects the dim cold key; no uniform).
