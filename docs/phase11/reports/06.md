## What changed (files, hook lines)

New files
- `src/render/NightSky.ts` — GLSL for the sky dome: anti-aliased two-layer star field + faint milky way, the moon's face, and
  procedural ground fires (burning regions → towns → individual fires, land-only via albedo, extra blazes around the bomb target).
  Footprint-aware Gaussians keep their energy, so distant fires fade instead of shimmering.
- `src/render/SunRim.ts` — low-sun rim light patched into `lights_fragment_end` (like AircraftLook's sky rim): edges seen against
  a sun below ~25° get a burning rim. No new uniform/program; skipped within 4–12 m of the camera (interiors) and at night.
- `tests/nightSunset.test.ts` — twilight/night sky state, GLSL contract, one-time rim install, searchlight `lock()`.

Changed
- `src/render/Atmosphere.ts` — `Atmos.twilight`, `Atmos.night`, uniforms `uTwilight`, `uNightSky`; `skyRadiance` splits the
  horizon at low sun (earth shadow + belt of Venus opposite, burning glow toward the sun, wide horizon aureole). Everything hazed by
  `skyRadiance` (cloud composite, aerial perspective, stream) inherits it.
- `src/render/Weather.ts` — new blended sky slot `TWI` (low sun → 1, reduced by cloud, overcast, storm; 0 at night); dusk gets
  exposure ×0.86 and cloud ambient 0.34 so the frame keeps contrast. Pushes `Atmos.twilight/night`.
- `src/render/Sky.ts` — calls `nightStars`, `nightFires`, `moonFace` when `uNightSky > 0` (3 lines).
- `src/render/Searchlights.ts` — beam shader: core + sheath across the beam, haze that thins with height, spreading with range,
  Henyey-Greenstein forward scatter (g 0.4); narrower beam (0.9°); new `lock(target)` (nearest beam cones a raider at once).
- `src/render/Contrails.ts` — moonlit trails ×0.15; low sun: more sun, less backscatter floor, cool earth-shadow sky light.
- `src/render/BomberStream.ts` — wake sheet takes the earth-shadow sky down-sun at twilight.
- `src/render/LivingSky.ts` — `uFill` uniform: distant contrails/smoke no longer use a constant daylight fill at night (the white streaks).
- `src/render/PostFX.ts` — moon gets 25 % of the sun's starburst/ghosts. `src/render/FilmLUT.ts` — dusk print: a little more contrast/saturation.
- `src/world/Effects.ts` — `nightFlak()` (flash + inner glow, no shared-RNG use); burning engines' light reaches further at night.
- `src/world/Exhaust.ts` — `updateBomberNightExhaust()` (turbo exhaust glow under each B-17 nacelle; own xorshift, no Math.random).
- `src/render/Particles.ts` — optional `rot` in spawn options (lets the night emitters avoid the shared random stream).
- Hooks: `World.ts:867` (`if (Atmos.night > 0.3) updateBomberNightExhaust(...)`), `Engine.ts:190` (`installSunRim()`).
- Rig: `src/dev/VisualRigScene.ts` (`night-formation`, `sunset-rim`), `tools/visual_rig.mjs` (burning-engine assertion for the new shot).

Not touched: Clouds.ts, Flipbooks.ts, AircraftLook.ts (goals 02/03/04 own them).

## Performance / programs
No runtime shader changes: all new GLSL is compiled with the existing sky, searchlight, contrail and standard materials (night and
twilight are uniform-gated branches). No per-frame allocations (pooled vectors, module-level colours). Frozen render cost from the
rig (High, Apple M5 Pro, WebGL2): see `frameMs` in the manifests — night-flak/sunset/night-formation/sunset-rim stay within the
before ranges (≈4–13 ms, noise dominated). Low preset renders all touched shots.

## Tests
`npx tsc --noEmit` clean; related suites (`weather`, `renderer10`, `cloudAtmosphere`, `farTerrain`, `visualRig`, `nightSunset`, …) pass.
Full suite: GitHub CI on the branch (result in the final message / PR).

## Scores (subjective, pillar 7 camera & post in night/low-sun scenes)
Night shots move from "night as a blue-tinted day" to a readable moonlit scene lit by its own sources (fires, flak, beams, moon);
sunset from a single beige tint to a warm/cool split with rimmed silhouettes. Estimate 6 → 7.5; the remaining gap is in cloud
lighting at low sun (cloud bodies still flat-lit; goal 03's domain) and in-motion behaviour, which stills cannot show.

## Not done / limitations
- Tracers lighting the airframe: unchanged (the existing heavy-gun muzzle lights do this near the camera; per-tracer lights would
  change the light count).
- Searchlight-lit smoke and a lit belly on coned aircraft: not added.
- Cloud bodies at sunset are still lit by the isotropic cloud ambient (Clouds.ts left to goal 03).
- Ground fires are procedural everywhere at night (regions/towns), not tied to mission damage beyond the bomb-target boost.
