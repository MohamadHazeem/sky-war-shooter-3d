# Phase 11 · 13 — Between-mission decisions: report

Branch `phase11/13-crew-decisions` (cloud session: no Blender, voice tools or GPU on this machine).

## What the player gets
A new **BEFORE THE NEXT MISSION** section in the gunner hangar, placed after the crew roster. It has five
cards, and every choice shows a green "+" gain and an orange "−" cost:

| Card | Choice | Gain | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ground crew | 3 shifts a break, spent on **Armour / Guns / Engines** (± buttons, pips) | −7% damage taken per shift · −10% barrel heat and −5% spread per shift · +25% engine HP per shift | only 3 shifts; the work lasts one mission. A ship that came home below 60% integrity uses up 1 shift on repairs ("patching holes"). A lost ship is replaced, so it costs no shift. |
| The next mission | **Normal sortie** or **Volunteer for a dangerous mission** | +50% merit; the mission counts as two missions for every man who flies it (faster tour progress) | fighters ×1.2 difficulty (tougher and sharper); fatigue +2 instead of +1 |
| Rest and leave | **48-hour pass** for a tired man (up to 2 at a time) | he returns with fatigue 0 and +6% skills for one mission | he misses the next mission and a replacement flies his seat |
| Replacements | each empty seat (hospital, pass, killed): **Green rookie** or **Depot veteran** | veteran skill 42/100 instead of 24/100 | 250 merit for one mission (refunded if switched back before take-off) |
| The ship | ship name + nose art (owned art only) | cosmetic; the existing Customize data | none; the card says it has no effect in the fight |

**Fatigue**: a man who flies gets +1 fatigue per mission (+2 on a volunteer mission). A man who stays on the
ground loses 2. From the third mission in a row his skills drop by 4% per mission, to at most −16%. Fatigue
shows on the role-stat bars in the roster. The decisions lock while a mission is running (the hangar between
rounds) with the note "Locked in for this mission".

**Swapping crew stations** was already available in the crew roster (tap a gunner, then another seat). The
panel points to it, and I added no second UI for it.

## Hook lines in shared files
- `MissionFlow.startMission`: `beginCrewDecisions(g.world, m, !!g.coop.role)` before `g.abilities.reset`. It freezes the plan before `applyLoadout`, raises engine-zone HP and multiplies `world.difficulty` on a volunteer mission.
- `MissionFlow.showDebrief`: merit `* crewDecisions.meritMult()`, then `endCrewDecisions(w)` after `prog.earn`.
- `Upgrades.Progression.loadout()`: wrapped in `groundCrewLoadout(…, this.role)`. It changes armour, heat and spread only while a gunner mission plan is running.
- `CrewRoster`: `stat`/`roleStat` × `crewDecisions.skillMult(id)` (fatigue or rested bonus; 1 by default, so existing numbers are unchanged); `rookieSkill(c)` replaces the hard-coded `ROOKIE_SKILL` in `effects`/`effSkill`/`aiSkill`; new `setLeave(id, on)`.
- `CrewPanel.statusOf`: "On a 48-hour pass — misses the next mission" (no flight-surgeon button for a pass).
- `UI.hangar`: `mountCrewDecisions(...)` next to `mountCrewRoster`; the launch-key guard also skips `.crewdec`.
- `HangarFold`: a `decisions` fold on phones (closed by default).

Scope: gunner missions of the player's own crew only. Pilot missions, the Gunnery School, the AA mode and
co-op run with no plan (`decisionsApply`). With no choices made, gameplay is unchanged.
