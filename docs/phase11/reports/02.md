## Implementation / hooks

- `src/render/GlassReflection.ts`: eight exponential steps, four hit-refinement steps, foreground/depth-edge rejection, five-tap radiance filtering and grazing fades. Reads existing HDR/SoftDepth inputs safely.
- `src/game/GlassFx.ts:359`: interpolated dome normals, sky/cloud PMREM fallback and shared reflection uniforms. `src/render/FormationReflection.ts:31` adds a **256px aircraft-only cubemap**, one face every other frame, for actual offscreen aircraft behind the gunner. Six populated faces are required before sampling; camera cuts invalidate the probe. It borrows existing aircraft submission matrices without reparenting or rendering clouds/shadows/effects/interiors.
- `src/core/Engine.ts:585`: `sortLayers` identifies nearby visible glass; `render` at lines 480/496 gates SSR/depth-copy work and occupied-glass probe work; `warmRenderer` at line 522 warms probe and compact-mesh variants before play. Low/Medium retain the existing environment path. High uses three normalized bloom levels for compact glints. Constructor defaults also apply the High submission/bloom settings before any settings change.
- `src/render/SunCascades.ts:64`: keep the near map's 80 m width / **3.9 cm High texels**, shift it 20 m toward the eye's view, retain full near weight through 38 m, and blend at map edges. Near/mid caster depths become 160/520 m. The prior 345.6 m formation-map fit remains.
- `src/render/Clouds.ts:241`, `FilmLUT.ts:6`: low-sun warm/cool illumination and dusk grade. `Viewmodels.ts`: two peripheral top-dome rails; firing lane stays open.
- `src/game/InteriorFx.ts:110`: cached 32³ noise replaces repeated procedural hashes; plane-distance products are hoisted outside the ten-step shaft march. Additive shafts use a single double-sided draw.
- `src/render/AircraftSubmission.ts:240`: avoid redundant instance visibility toggles and per-instance map-entry arrays. High uses the existing authored compact far mesh beyond 650 m, or 60 m for offscreen shadow-only casters. Close/reflected, damaged, player, falling, open-bay and object-blurred film aircraft retain articulation. `AircraftFinish.ts` avoids applying transparent-glass alpha compensation to opaque merged far glass.
- Prior baked-ORM-aware satin paint, brushed-metal response, specular occlusion and sky PMREM remain. No Game/World/UI hooks, player-visible strings or radio changes.

## Limits

The offscreen probe supplies real aircraft geometry with a simple dark skin response; it does not reproduce full livery, shader-only damage cuts or volumetric clouds. Its 22–180 m range and twelve-frame refresh cycle complement SSR and the weather-refreshed environment. SSR still cannot recover hidden geometry outside these inputs. Brushed metal remains an inexpensive normal-response approximation rather than a full anisotropic BRDF. Frozen rendering, Low safety and shader stability are verified; other GPUs and long combat were not requalified.
