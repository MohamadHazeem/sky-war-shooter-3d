# Phase 11 · 01b — revised Sketchfab fighters and seated aircrew

Branch: `phase11/01b-sketchfab-fighters`. This revision addresses the rejected paint, crew, geometry budget and comparison evidence. No deployment or main-branch push.

## Changes

- **Crew:** replaced the reduced standing-animation derivative with an original **16,992-triangle seated mesh**: continuous bent sleeves/trousers, rounded shoulders, gloves/boots, leather helmet, goggles, oxygen mask/hose, shearling collar and canvas harness. Nine lit PBR materials separate leather, cloth, skin and equipment. Also fixed a conversion bug: transforming quantized normals collapsed their directions, making inserted crew appear flat. Copies are now dequantized before transformation and recompressed afterwards; regression tests check normal diversity in every replaced aircraft. Runtime tuning and the material audit now preserve the authored crew roughness and equipment metalness.
- **B-17 evidence:** the turntable previously displayed uppercase `_LOD0/1/2` together, allowing opaque far windows to cover the close cockpit. It now selects one level. Both seated pilots are visible through the original glazing; an additional cockpit view shows their materials and pose.
- **Fw 190:** reduced unnecessary source tessellation. LOD0 is **46,224 triangles including parked gear**, down from 66,980 (**−31%**). The gear-up turntable draws **45,284 versus live's 44,386 (+2.0%)**. The two removed source cowl gun islands now have replacement barrels at the retained muzzle anchors. No silhouette gate was relaxed.
- **Paint/decals:** twice the fuselage UV scale before atlas packing, quality-95 WebP fallback, a larger Fw 190 “8”, discrete irregular spray patches, paler RLM 76 lower paint and satin 74/75 upper paint. Both types retain 2048 colour atlases, UASTC KTX2, normal/ORM maps, weathering and ace liveries.
- **Bf 109:** the white Reich band is painted around the whole fuselage skin. The yellow tactical bar is separated from the cross and band; it no longer overlaps both markings.
- **Comparisons:** all gun scenes now use isolated Phase 10 fixtures. Combat/effect pools are cleared, and no simulation runs after fighter placement. `compare.mjs` asserts identical camera, fighter poses, weather, simulation clock and cleared-effect state. Old inconsistent mission captures/check files were replaced. The head-on fixture now preserves aircraft up instead of allowing shortest-arc rotation to invert approaching fighters.
