# Phase 11 · 01b — fighter and crew quality-review revision

Branch: `phase11/01b-sketchfab-fighters`. No deployment or main-branch push.

## Changes

- **Fw 190 geometry:** the Sketchfab source uses double-sided materials. The previous single-sided conversion skipped the exterior-visibility pass, leaving reversed panels and exposed internal faces in the banked waist shot. The converter now resolves winding, removes hidden faces and retains both sides of genuinely open sheets before refinement. This fixes 437 reversed Fw 190 faces (391 on the Bf 109). The remaining black diagonal was the rear wall of an open parked wheel well: retracted covers now follow convex lower wing sections and close it. Removed duplicate source cowl/wing guns; one replacement set now fits measured leading-edge sections, independently of the unchanged gameplay muzzle markers. Rebuilt the recessed BMW 801 fan and annular opening for the head-on view, and tripled spinner UV scale for a cleaner spiral.
- **Paint:** removed the pale RLM 76 override. Both types now use blue-grey lower surfaces, denser overlapping feathered 74/75 mottle and matte paint. Kept the Bf 109 white band and separate yellow bar, the readable Fw 190 “8”, 2048 colour atlases, KTX2/WebP fallback, ace schemes and panel/weathering detail.
- **Antennas:** removed sub-pixel source cables from all LODs; retained solid masts. No per-frame fade code, additional material or shader variant.
- **Crew:** retained the seated pose and visible B-17 pilots. Replaced separate spherical cheeks/jaw/nose with a continuous tapered face and slim bridge. The oxygen mask is a shaped shell with a cheek/chin seal and two straps, a smaller matte valve and aligned hose ribs. Reduced goggles/ear cups, narrowed the neck, removed intersecting shoulder caps and raised leather roughness to .82. The seated mesh is 16,274 triangles.

Runtime GLBs/textures and archived crew-modified conversions are regenerated through the existing pipeline. No production frame-loop changes or new dependencies.
