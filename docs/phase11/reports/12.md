# Phase 11 · 12 — Limping home · report

Branch `phase11/12-limping-home` · built in a Claude cloud session (no Blender, no voice tools, no GPU).

## What it does
When a campaign gunner mission would end in **success** but the player's B-17 is badly hurt, the mission does not
end yet: the **return leg becomes gameplay**.

- **Trigger** (`limpingDue`): an engine out *and* a holed tank, two engines lost, or the airframe below 45 % — always
  measured against how the ship started (Last Light / The Long Way Home start on two engines and need fresh damage).
  Never for P-51 missions, co-op, survival/side modes, a ship already going down, or one with all four engines dead.
- **Fuel leaks / lost engines**: a fuel gauge on a small card (left lane, under the status block). The tanks start
  the leg at 34 %, minus what already poured out of a holed tank and 3 % per dead engine; every dead engine makes the
  others burn 12 % more per kilometre; an open leak drains 0.2 %/s, also while the crew is still talking (deciding
  late costs fuel).
- **Falling behind the formation**: two engines out, or any choice other than "home with the box", sets the ship
  `straggling` (World already drops a straggler behind/below her slot, bounded by `PLAYER_STRAGGLE`). The co-pilot
  calls it.
- **German fighters hunting stragglers**: while she is alone, a pair of fighters is sent after her (pinned to her
  through `MidRound.spawnAttackers`) at 12 s, 30 s and 50 s into the leg — none once she is over neutral air or
  committed to ditching. The tail gunner calls them.
- **The crew decides, with the player's input**: after ~6 s of interphone (fuel check, leak report, "you get a vote
  too") the card shows the options with each choice's votes. Nine crewmen vote by the numbers, each with his own
  caution and leaning (the engineer wants fuel to spare, the radio man trusts Air-Sea Rescue, the ball gunner
  hates the water, the tail gunner has had enough of the war, the pilot leans to England). The gunner votes with
  **7 / 8 / 9 / 0** (or a tap on the card on touch) and counts as **three votes**: he swings a split crew but can't
  overrule nine men; the pilot breaks ties; with no vote in 15 s the crew decides alone.
- **Choices and outcomes**
  - *Home with the formation* (only while she can keep up): normal ending, landing scene.
  - *Emergency field · RAF Woodbridge*: shorter route; the debrief reason says she diverted; landing scene plays.
  - *Ditch in the Channel · Air-Sea Rescue*: Mayday with a position fix, then the existing **Ditching** scene
    (dinghies, flare, RAF rescue launch, Goldfish Club). Running the tanks dry on any route also ends here.
  - *Neutral Sweden / Switzerland · interned* (only from targets in reach: north Germany → Sweden, south →
    Switzerland, coastal targets such as Emden → none): no landing scene, the bomber counts as lost, the debrief
    says the crew is interned, and **the tour ends**: the campaign is closed (`interned` saved, current story null,
    "Tour ended · interned" on the map and summary, *second tour refused*, only "Begin a new tour").
- Failure endings (shot down, too many losses, the gunner seriously wounded) are never held; they end the leg.

## Files
New
- `src/game/LimpingHomeRules.ts` — pure rules: trigger, fuel model, routes, options, crew biases, utility, vote,
  decision, distances, all player text (`LIMP_TEXT`) and radio (`LIMP_RADIO`).
- `src/game/LimpingHome.ts` — the runtime: intercepting the success ending, leg timeline, fuel, straggling, hunters,
  vote input, outcomes, the card (inline CSS, rebuilt at most every 0.1 s, nothing allocated per frame).
- `src/i18n/ar/limpingHome.ts` — Arabic for every new string (card, options, outcomes, campaign notes, radio).
- `tests/limpingHome.test.ts` — 26 tests (rules, crew vote, neutral mapping, campaign internment, Arabic coverage,
  and the runtime class driven frame by frame with a stub `Game`).

Hook lines in shared files
- `Game.ts`: import, `readonly limping = new LimpingHome(this)`, `limping.release()` in `startMenu` / `showBriefing`,
  `limping.frame(dt)` after `b17Damage.update` (frame step 10).
- `MissionFlow.ts`: `limping.reset()` in `startMission`; first line of `finish()`:
  `if (!g.endResult && g.limping.intercept(success, reason)) return;` (bombardier.leave moved one line down);
  no landing scene when interned; `interned` passed to `mountCampaignReport`.
- `Ditching.ts` (`eligible`): `if (g.limping.ditch) return true; if (g.limping.owns) return false;` — the crew's
  decision owns the ending.
- `CampaignState.ts`: `interned` on `CampaignData` / `HistoryEntry` / `Outcome`; `applyOutcome` closes the tour and
  adds the notes; `sanitize` keeps it; `currentStory` → null; `startSecondTour` refuses an interned crew.
- `CampaignMap.ts`: interned wording on the tour-complete card and summary; no second-tour button.
- `i18n/phrases.ts`: table registered first (goal 00's auto-discovery is not on main yet).
- `main.ts`: `sws.ditch` now calls `game.limping.skip()` (its forced success ending would otherwise start a leg);
  new dev hook `sws.limp(enginesOut = 2, leaking = false, mission = 1)`.

## Not done / notes
- **No recorded voice** for the 13 new radio lines: this machine has no voice tools. They play as interphone
  subtitles with the speech fallback (as every unrecorded line does). To record: add them to
  `tools/voice/lines.json` (or `lines.d/` once goal 00 lands) and run `python3 tools/voice/generate.py`.
- No new art/scene for the emergency field or the neutral landing (no Blender/GPU here): Woodbridge reuses the
  home landing scene; internment ends with the fly-by and the debrief. A Swiss/Swedish airfield scene would be a
  natural follow-up.
- Geography is narrative (World has no route); distances on the card only have to agree with the fuel model.
- Autoplay/regression runs: a damaged ship on a successful mission now flies ~45–60 s more before the debrief
  (the crew decides on its own after 15 s), so mission times in those logs grow a little.
