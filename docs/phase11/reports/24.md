# Phase 11 · 24 — new enemies

Three recognizable enemy airframes now have their own hero assets and combat roles. The Me 410 no longer renders the Bf 110 stand-in; the Me 163 has bounded rocket fuel and a real unpowered withdrawal; the new Ju 88 G acquires a radar contact, settles underneath it, and fires fixed 75° cannon in short bursts.

## Implementation

- `tools/hero/luftwaffe/types/{me410,me163,ju88g}.py`, `tools/hero/enemies/`: reproducible GPL-derived geometry, baked panel/rivet/weathering detail, 2048 base colour, 1024 normal/ORM, WebP fallback, KTX2 mip chains, ace skins and three LODs. Sources, references and reconstruction limits are in [the asset README](../../../tools/hero/enemies/README.md). Credits updated in `public/models/CREDITS.md`.
- Me 410: new fuselage, swept wings, canopy and single central fin; BK 5 geometry and side barbettes now reside in the asset. Existing telegraphed stand-off shell attacks and rear-gunner logic retained; two forward cannon and two MGs fitted. Removed the runtime Bf 110 dressing code.
- Me 163: dimension-registered original shape, animated elevons/rudder, fitted hit zones, cockpit/pilot and skid. Two finite-ammunition MK 108s. Combat fuel is compressed to **32 seconds of full boost**, or the end of its one climbing pass, rather than historical multi-minute endurance. Exhaust and rocket audio stop after burnout; thrust cannot restart even if another AI state commands throttle.
- Ju 88 G: solid nose, enlarged fin, FuG 220 aerials, flame dampers and visible oblique mounts. Radar needs three seconds in its forward cone; an additional four-second warning precedes firing. Upward guns require a real close-range alignment, have finite ammunition and their own trigger, and cannot inherit forward-gun aim. Three short bursts maximum; dead/lost contacts or timeout cause release/withdrawal. Both the initial warning and first-sighting line have recorded voices and Arabic subtitles.
- Custom Battle/gallery include Ju 88 G. Normal night Ju 88 waves can become night fighters; late ordinary Bf 110 waves can include Me 410s. Explicit mode/mission enemy restrictions remain authoritative.

## Hooks

`src/world/World.ts:551` fits weapons before scaling; line 990 separates the oblique trigger and the following firing loop clamps finite ammunition. `Aircraft.ts:196` enforces fuel/thrust; `AI.ts:511` clears oblique triggers and withdraws exhausted Komets. `Tactics.ts:505` owns the radar intercept (entry at line 1262) and routes all three types, including station-assigned groups. `Models`/`HeroLook` register assets and movable barbettes/elevons; existing `World.prewarm` automatically warms every registered enemy model. `Director.ts:174` selects night variants only for the unrestricted normal mix. Audio, hit layouts and destruction mass/part mappings recognize the new model kinds.

## Limits

Aircraft are game reconstructions derived from the existing GPL sources, not engineering scans. The Me 410 reuses DB-engine/cockpit components from the Bf 110 source; the Ju 88 G retains Ju 88 source wing/nacelle detail. This task adds local AI; no new co-op protocol for separate upward-gun muzzle flashes was introduced. Performance measurements are frozen full-render timings on the shared machine, not a claim about worst-case combat frame time. No deployment or main-branch push.
