# Phase 11 · 11 — Crew stories and morale: report

Branch `phase11/11-crew-stories` · cloud session (no Blender, no voice tools, no GPU).

## What changed
All ten crewmen now have lives: personalities, relationships, moods and stories that change how they work.

- **Traits**: two per man (e.g. Stern *Precise · Worrier*, Kessler *Proud · Superstitious*, Walsh *Cheerful · Lucky*).
  Each trait does one small thing: losses hit a worrier harder, a cheerful man bounces back, a superstitious man
  cares more about his ritual, a tough man tires more slowly, a stubborn man holds a grudge. Hovering a trait shows what it does.
- **Morale (0–100) and fatigue (0–100)**. Inside the normal band (morale 40–75, fatigue ≤ 50) nothing changes.
  High spirits lift a man's role skills by up to +6%; low morale drags them down to −15%, exhaustion to −12%.
  The multiplier is applied in `CrewRoster.roleStat`, so AI gunnery, spotting warnings, repairs, first aid and the
  pilots' nerve all follow it. It is exactly 1 at the default state (no balance change for players until things happen).
  - Morale: mission result, kills at his gun, his own wound, the group's losses, a wounded/lost friend, letters,
    rituals, friends or friction aboard, trait drift, slow return toward the middle.
  - Fatigue: builds for the men who fly (more on a mauled mission or when wounded), drains for men resting; a
    **48-hour pass every 5 missions** rests everyone.
- **Friendships and friction**: 10 seeded relationships from the existing bios (Brenner & Doyle, Price & Walsh,
  Stern vs Kessler over initial points, Price vs Gallo…). Good missions flown together bring men closer; bad ones
  sharpen friction; a wounded man's best friend stays at his side (bond up). Crossing thresholds writes journal entries
  ("patched things up", "had words after the mission", "closer for it").
- **Letters from home and replies**: mail call after each mission (at most two letters, from each man's family; some
  are bad news). Between missions the player can **help one man write back** per break (+8 morale, +12 for bad news).
  Unanswered letters fade after three missions.
- **Rituals and superstitions**: each man has a pre-mission ritual (Kessler's socks, Walsh's rabbit's foot, Mendez's
  Saint Christopher, Reilly's left foot first…). Rolled at take-off; a broken one unsettles him for that mission
  (twice as much for the superstitious) and gets its own written story.
- **Reactions to losses**: a man killed (the roster's rare permanent loss) — everyone grieves, his closest friend
  most ("sat on his empty bunk for an hour"); heavy group losses noted by the biggest worrier; wounded friends.
- **Crew journal**: the latest 14 happenings, shown in the hangar.
- **Crew room**: up to two short *written* vignettes chosen from the crew's state are shown on the left of the
  crew-room scene (letter by the stove, friends splitting a parcel, rivals at opposite ends of the hut, a man asleep
  in his boots, a memorial bunk nobody will clear…). Text only; no new voice lines.
- **UI (simple, Phase 8 style)**: the hangar crew card gets one line ("Morale: Holding up · Fatigue: Fresh",
  plus "Performance ±n%" when it matters). Under the roster a folding **Crew stories** section: mail call with the
  reply buttons, the men (morale/fatigue bars, traits, ritual), friends and friction, the journal.
- **Arabic**: every new string in a new table `src/i18n/ar/crewStories.ts` (registered first in `phrases.ts`);
  names and senders are translated inside templates; RTL layout checked.
