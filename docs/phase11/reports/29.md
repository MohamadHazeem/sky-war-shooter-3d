# Phase 11 · 29 — Adaptive difficulty director

The old `Adaptive` looked only at health and kills in the last minute, and it changed only the fighter count and the enemy skill. The new director measures four things: **accuracy, damage taken, reaction time and kills per minute**. Each one is compared with a human-level baseline for the player's role. The director then adapts the **enemy count, aggression and accuracy** gently, inside the band of the chosen difficulty. It is a visible setting (on/off) and it never punishes the player. A test runs synthetic bots of different skill through a closed loop. Real autoplay bots (near-perfect, human-level and rookie) were also run with the director off and on.

## What changed

- `src/game/Adaptive.ts` (rewritten, pure, no THREE/DOM):
  - **Measures** use 45-second windows. Accuracy is hits over rounds fired, and it only counts once 30 rounds are in the window. Damage taken is integrity lost per minute; repairs never count back. Reaction time runs from a fighter entering gun range (900 m gunner, 700 m pilot) to the player's first hit on it, scanned 4× a second. Fighters that are never answered are dropped: they may be in another gun's sector, and the damage measure covers them anyway. Kills per minute is the fourth measure.
  - **Performance** runs from −1 to +1. It is a weighted mix: accuracy 0.4, damage 0.3, kills 0.2, reaction 0.1. In autoplay, accuracy and damage separated the bots best, while reaction time was noisy.
  - **Four knobs:**
    - `mult`: fighters in the air, used by the Director and by Pacing's final wave. Range 0.75–1.2×.
    - `skill`: enemy skill, which drives aggression, BFM and aim. Range 0.88–1.1×.
    - `aim`: multiplies `World.enemyAimMult`. Range 1.3× worse to 0.92× sharper.
    - `focus`: multiplies `World.playerFocus`. Range 0.75–1×. It never sends more attackers at the player than the mission sets.
  - **Difficulty bands** (`ADAPTIVE_BANDS`): Recruit eases at full strength and pushes at half. Veteran uses 0.8 both ways. Ace eases at half and pushes at full, so Ace stays brutal. Combined with `difficultyBalance`, the effective enemy skill never crosses the midpoint to the next difficulty (tested).
  - **Never punishing:**
    - Easing is quick (τ 10 s) and starts at once.
    - Pushing is slow (τ 40 s) and only begins after 60 s of play.
    - Health below 40% cancels any push in the same frame and eases hard. Health below 60% never pushes.
    - Score and merit are never touched.
  - **When off:** every knob is exactly 1. The measures still run for the dev readout `sws.game.adaptive.state()`.
- **Settings:** the "Adaptive difficulty" On/Off switch is a basic setting (`settings.adaptive`, undefined = on). It sits next to Difficulty with a one-line hint: "Eases or presses the enemy to match how you fly, within your difficulty. Never lowers your score."
- **Debrief:** a new "Adaptive difficulty" tile shows what the director did over the mission: Eased off / Steady / Pressed harder / Off. It is not shown in co-op.
- **Arabic:** new table `src/i18n/ar/adaptive.ts`. The debrief words are plain (أسهل / ثابتة / أصعب), because shadda/damma marks collided with the tile label in the large tile font.
- **Docs:** a section in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## Hook lines

- `src/game/Director.ts:74–79`: `observe` + `update` each frame of the round director, then `enemyAimMult = loadout.enemyAim × aim` and `playerFocus = baseFocus × focus`. The `mult`/`skill` uses are unchanged.
- `src/game/MissionFlow.ts`: `g.adaptive.reset(diff, m.role, g.world.playerFocus)` in `startMission`, and `adaptive: g.adaptive.summary()` in the debrief data.
- `src/game/Game.ts`: `this.adaptive.enabled = s.adaptive !== false` in `applySettings`.
- `src/ui/UI.ts`: the Settings type, the switch and hint, the boolean parsing, the basic-row order and the debrief tile. `src/ui/SettingsModel.ts`: `BASIC_SETTINGS` gains `adaptive`.

## Tests

`tests/adaptive.test.ts` has 10 tests. The balance tests fly synthetic rookie, human and ace bots for 5–15 minutes in a closed loop, where more, sharper or more focused fighters hurt more. They check that:
- the rookie is eased, the human-level bot stays steady and the ace is pressed;
- easing cuts the rookie's damage taken by more than 25%;
- the director stays inside the band for all three difficulties;
- there is no push in the first minute, and pushing is much slower than easing;
- a crisis cancels any push and eases;
- the reaction tracker ignores allies, out-of-range fighters and unanswered fighters;
- the P-51 baseline holds;
- reset works, the setting is basic, and the Arabic text exists.

Related suites pass: tsc, cleanHud, i18n, pacing, menuRouting, score2 and cinema. The full `npm run check` result is in the "Verification" section below.

## Balance runs with autoplay bots (real game)

`tools/adaptive_balance.mjs` runs Mission 1 (B-17 gunner, Veteran) on this worktree's Vite (port 5429; the task names no port) in Playwright's bundled headless shell, with one fresh page per run. There are three bots:
- the near-perfect bot (skill 1);
- the human-level bot (skill 0);
- a rookie (skill 0 with 2.4× the aim error).

What the runs show:
- The rookie is eased for the whole mission: fewer fighters, worse enemy aim and attacks spread over the formation. It finished at 97% integrity instead of 78%, with more kills.
- The human-level bot stays near 1 ("Steady").
- The near-perfect bot is pressed slowly, by design: the multiplier rises to 1.06 by the end of a six-minute mission.

These are single runs per row. Autoplay is not repeatable (docs/ARCHITECTURE.md), so kills and losses vary between runs; the knob traces are the signal. The calibration pass is where the baselines came from: human-level accuracy about 0.31–0.40, about 0.04 integrity lost per minute, about 5–6 kills per minute (capped by the spawn target), and a reaction time of about 4 s from 900 m.

## Limits / not done

- **Scope matches the old Adaptive:** the director runs inside the round director, which covers B-17 gunner missions and Survival. The P-51 campaign missions run PilotOps' scripted rounds, raids, strafe or sea, and are not adapted.
- **P-51 baseline is not calibrated in autoplay:** in Survival the pilot bots were shot down suddenly while still at high integrity (75 s and 167–233 s; `balance-pilot-survival.json`, director off and on alike). So the pilot baseline keeps the documented human P-51 accuracy (0.24, docs/BALANCE.md).
- **Real players differ from the bots:** the baselines come from autoplay bots with aim assist on High. If real players miss far more, the director will lean toward easing, which is the safe direction.
- **No per-mission campaign balance sweep** beyond Mission 1, because the shared machine was under heavy load (about 8–10 min per run). No deploy and no push to main.
