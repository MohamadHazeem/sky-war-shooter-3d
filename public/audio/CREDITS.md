## Crew voices — Chatterbox / VCTK

The Chatterbox sample clips in `voice/` contain speech generated with
[Chatterbox TTS](https://github.com/resemble-ai/chatterbox) (MIT licence, Resemble AI),
conditioned on distinct reference speakers from the
[CSTR VCTK Corpus, version 0.92](https://doi.org/10.7488/ds/2645).
VCTK: Junichi Yamagishi, Christophe Veaux and Kirsten MacDonald,
Centre for Speech Technology Research (CSTR), University of Edinburgh, 2019;
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
References were fetched clip-by-clip through the
[sanchit-gandhi/vctk dataset viewer](https://huggingface.co/datasets/sanchit-gandhi/vctk),
trimmed, concatenated with short gaps, resampled and normalised. Generated speech is
resampled, trimmed, faded, normalised and encoded as AAC for the game.
Speaker IDs, source utterances and modifications are recorded in
[`tools/voice/refs/README.md`](../../tools/voice/refs/README.md) and `refs/sources.json`.
Unmigrated clips remain the original macOS `say` recordings until the full render is run.
